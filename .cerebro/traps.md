## A spoken line without a recording blocked the gate (rq-mwz, 2026-10-24)

`web/src/lantern/narrationCasts.test.ts` demanded a clip for every script line. A producer cannot record (Audio Studio and the Fish key are the navigator's), so rq-mwz was parked with its code done. Ship new or changed lines in `tts/act<N>/script.json` without recordings: the navigator records them by hand after the merge. See *Narration Recordings* in `CLAUDE.md`. Never park a bead, or file one, to get a line recorded.
