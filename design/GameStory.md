# Game Story: RetroQuest – The Awakening

## Story Canon

This is the settled truth behind the story. Every act design must agree with it.

- **The Wardens of the Dawn.** Elior's parents, **Aren** (father) and **Lysa** (mother), and **Malakar** were three equal friends who made the three virtue relics together: the Crystal of Light (courage), the Phoenix Feather (wisdom) and the Dragon's Scale (selflessness). They were fellow Wardens, not master and apprentices.
- **Why Malakar fell: grief, then power.** Malakar was the Warden of Wisdom. When catastrophe threatened his friends he could not wait: he reached for time magic to save them, tore a wound in time that feeds on life, and the power he found there corrupted him into wanting more. The Phoenix's riddle in Act III ("What is wisdom when time is short?" / "patience") names the virtue he failed.
- **The parents are the ward.** Aren and Lysa poured themselves into a seal that hides Elior and holds Malakar's wound shut. It is anchored in the **Ancient Amulet** and in King Alden's royal bindings, which is why Malakar drains the king. When the wound is healed, the parents speak to Elior one last time and then rest in peace. They are not alive, and nothing in the epilogue hints that they are.
- **Willowbrook was their home.** The family lived in Willowbrook. Grandmother is Lysa's mother and knows the truth. **Mira** was the Wardens' pupil; when the parents became the ward she settled in Willowbrook as its healer to watch over Elior. This is hinted in Act I and confessed in Act III.
- **The dream voice is the Shadow of the Past**, the uncorrupted remnant of Malakar, asking his friends' son for help. It feels familiar because Malakar held baby Elior. It is heard once per act, is never tied to a parent, and is met in Act IV's Memory Vault.
- **Malakar's agent.** A cloaked stranger seen in Willowbrook is Malakar's agent, searching for the bloodline. His work feeds the same draining magic that is killing King Alden, so the blight has a single cause. The same agent framed Sir Cedric.
- **Sir Cedric** was on guard the night King Alden vanished; Malakar's agents framed him, and the Secret Documents prove it.
- **The Sorceress of Lies** (an Act IV lieutenant) left her mark on Elena's curse in Act II.
- **The refrain** "Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth." is carved on the moss-covered stone (Act I), spoken by Nyx (Act II), echoed by the Dragon (Act III) and said by Elior to Malakar (Act IV).
- **Three endings:** **Redemption** (the golden path: forgiveness heals the wound), **Justice** (Malakar is defeated and sealed away) and **Sacrifice** (Elior gives up his magic to seal the wound). All three end at home in Willowbrook; Crown and Home are no longer separate endings. The closing image is the withered field from Act I, green again, as Elior casts `grow`.
- **Virtue moments:** two or three optional moments per act (mercy, truth, help that gains nothing). They never block the golden path and add no new puzzles. They are remembered across acts and decide which endings open, plus a few lines of dialogue. Old saves are rejected when the save format changes; no migration is written.

## Prologue

Elior, a humble farmer boy, lives in the quiet village of Willowbrook on the outskirts of Eldoria. Raised by his grandmother after his parents vanished, Elior’s life is simple—tending crops and caring for animals. He believes they simply vanished, and he longs to uncover the truth behind their disappearance. One stormy night, a strange light appears in the sky, and Elior dreams of a shadowy figure calling his name—a voice that feels achingly familiar. It is the first time he hears the dream voice, which is not tied to either parent.

## Act I: The Awakening

Elior awakens to find his crops withered and the village in distress. The healer, Mira, senses a dark magic at work and reveals to Elior that he possesses latent magical abilities. She gives him an ancient amulet, hinting that it once belonged to someone important in his family, and instructs him to seek out the wise knight, Sir Cedric, in the nearby town of Greendale. A cloaked stranger has been seen in the village, and Elior learns that the amulet was his parents’ and that someone has come to Willowbrook looking for it. The refrain “Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.” is carved on the moss-covered stone in the glade.

**Key Events:**

- Elior learns basic magic from Mira and begins to question his family's past.
- The player explores Willowbrook, helping villagers and discovering clues about the darkness and Elior’s parents.
- Elior sets out for Greendale, facing minor obstacles and learning to use his powers.

**Highlights from the Act I design** (see `Act1Design.md`):

- **Opening:** Elior wakes in his cottage after the dream. Grandmother teaches him his first spell, `revive`, and his journal records the dream before he may leave the house.
- **Main quest – _Shadows Over Willowbrook_:** withered crops, a murky well, a changed river and frightened villagers point to a spreading shadow. Elior finds the **Rare Flower** in the Hidden Glade, guided by a strangely calm **Magical Deer**, and gives it to Mira, which turns his "practice" magic into real magic (`heal`, `unlock`, `light`).
- **Side quests:** _Hint of Magic_, _Know your village_, _Curiosity killed the cat_ (the Abandoned Shed), _Fishing expedition_ (the Fisherman teaches `purify`), _Oh, deer, oh deer_, _Magnet fishing expedition_ (a ring from the purified well, traded for **wandering boots**), _Let there be light_ (a hidden founder's **locket** in the chapel unlocks `bless`), _Magic for real_ and _Preparing for the road_ (cloak, food, protection, boots, map and every basic spell).
- **The family mystery:** _The Faded Photograph_ and the locket draw a tender but guarded memory of Elior's parents from Grandmother and tie his family to the darkness over Eldoria. The Priest speaks of the village founders' ancient safeguards, and the Old Mill is said to hold secrets older than the village.
- **Spells:** `revive`, `purify`, `bless`, `heal`, `unlock`, `light`, and `grow` (read from the moss-covered stone in the glade).
- **Ending:** Mira presents the **Ancient Amulet** ("May this guide and protect you"), and the map reveals a hidden road to Greendale.
- **Canon to add (sibling bead):** the cloaked stranger searching for the amulet, Mira hinting that she knew his parents, the refrain on the moss-covered stone, and the dream voice as the Shadow of the Past rather than a parent. Malakar, King Alden and the prophecy are not yet named, and Mira does not mention Sir Cedric.

## Act II: The Gathering

In Greendale, Elior meets Sir Cedric, who recognizes the amulet as a symbol of the Chosen One. Cedric, in disgrace for the night King Alden vanished, pledges his loyalty once the Secret Documents clear his name, and guides Elior to the Enchanted Forest, where they must find Nyx, the forest sprite, to unlock the next stage of Elior’s powers. Elior has believed Malakar a legend from old books; by the end of the act he learns Malakar is real and seeks the Willowbrook bloodline, which means Elior. Nyx speaks the refrain, the dream voice is heard again, and the Sorceress of Lies’ mark is found on Elena’s curse.

**Key Events:**

- The player navigates Greendale, gathering supplies and information.
- Cedric joins the party, offering combat and guidance.
- In the Enchanted Forest, the player solves riddles and puzzles set by Nyx.
- Nyx reveals the legend of Malakar, the evil sorcerer, and the prophecy of the Chosen One, hinting that Malakar once sought something in Willowbrook.

**Highlights from the Act II design** (see `Act2Design.md`):

- **Setting:** Greendale is a trade city of gates, markets, a castle and the **Silver Stag Inn**, and the threshold to the Enchanted Forest. The forest deepens from the standing stones of the Forest Transition to the Heart of the Forest.
- **Main quest – _The Gathering Storm_:** eleven side quests build up to summoning **Nyx** at the Offering Altar with three charms (Protective, Druidic, Nature's). Nyx grants `prophetic_vision`, and Elior shares the vision with Cedric to close the act.
- **Sir Cedric:** a formal, honourable knight in disgrace. _The Knight's Test_ earns his respect, and _Cedric's Lost Honor_ clears his name with **Secret Documents** recovered from **Caravan Master Thorne**'s lost caravan.
- **Greendale's people:** **Master Healer Lyria** takes Elior on as an apprentice (`greater_heal`). **Innkeeper Marcus**'s daughter **Elena** is cured of a curse with `greater_heal`, Crystal-Clear Water and `dispel`. A **Spectral Librarian** guards the Hidden Library, whose **Ancient Chronicle** teaches `dispel`.
- **Forest quests:** _The Hermit's Warning_, _The Forest Guardian's Riddles_ ("tree", "water", "ants") and _Whispers in the Wind_ (the Ancient Tree Spirit, the Enchanted Acorn and Moonflowers).
- **Lore:** _Echoes of the Past_ and _The Ancient Library_ reveal that Malakar's interest in Willowbrook concerns **ancient bloodlines**, and that the library holds prophecies of the **Chosen One** and of a coming darkness.
- **Spells:** `mend`, `dispel`, `greater_heal`, `nature_sense`, `forest_speech`, `prophetic_vision`.
- **Canon to add (sibling bead):** Cedric recognises the amulet, the Secret Documents prove Malakar’s agents framed him, Nyx speaks the refrain, the Sorceress of Lies’ mark appears on Elena’s curse, the dream voice is heard and the virtue moments are placed. Nyx shows a vision rather than telling Malakar's legend, King Alden is absent, and there is no combat.

## Act III: Trials and Revelations

With Nyx’s help, Elior unlocks new magical abilities. The group learns that Malakar has captured King Alden and is draining his life force; the same draining magic is behind the blight over Eldoria. To save the king, Elior must retrieve three ancient relics hidden across the kingdom: the Crystal of Light, the Phoenix Feather, and the Dragon’s Scale. Each relic represents a virtue Elior must embrace—courage, wisdom, and selflessness. Along the way, Elior faces Malakar’s minions and optional moments of mercy, truth and selfless help that shape which endings open.

**Key Events:**

- The player embarks on quests to retrieve each relic:
    - **Crystal of Light (Courage):** Hidden in the Sunken Ruins, guarded by water spirits.
    - **Phoenix Feather (Wisdom):** Located atop Mount Ember, requiring the player to solve elemental puzzles.
    - **Dragon’s Scale (Selflessness):** Deep within the Caverns of Shadow, protected by a sleeping dragon.
- Optional virtue moments (mercy, truth, help that gains nothing) are remembered and decide which endings open; they never block the golden path.
- Elior has believed his parents were Malakar’s victims. He learns they were Malakar’s closest friends and that they are warded away, not dead; Mira confesses she was the Wardens’ pupil. The Dragon echoes the refrain, and the dream voice is heard again.

**Highlights from the Act III design** (see `Act3Design.md`):

- **Opening:** Nyx's parting words, _"Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth"_, frame the act. At her hut, Mira reveals the stakes: if King Alden dies, the bindings that protect Eldoria unravel. She gives Elior her **Keepsake Note** and **teleports** the party (Elior, Mira and Cedric) between the relic sites.
- **Main quest – _The Three Virtues_:**
    - **Sunken Ruins:** _Tideward Sigils_ and _Lanterns of the Deeps_. The Tide-Born Guardian asks for a vow naming who Elior will not abandon; the answer is **"myself"**.
    - **Mount Ember:** _Mirrors of Emberlight_ (with the Ash Scholar) and _Breath of the Mountain_. The Phoenix asks _"What is wisdom when time is short?"_; the answer is **"patience"**.
    - **Caverns of Shadow:** _Miners' Rescue_ and _Oath of Stillness_. A wise, tired **Ancient Dragon** surrenders its scale for a selfless binding oath, even though taking the scale weakens the prison it keeps.
- **Storytelling quest – _Echoes of the Hidden Bond_:** the Sea-Sealed Letter, the Charred Inscription and the Dragon's Memory reveal that Elior's parents, **Aren and Lysa, were Malakar's closest friends and fellow Wardens**, who gave themselves to hide Elior and vanished: _"not dead, but warded away"_. Act IV completes the truth: the parents are the ward, a seal that hides Elior and that Elior himself carries.
- **Ending:** Mira's Warding Rite fuses the relics into the **Lifelight Elixir** and teleports the party to the gates of Malakar's fortress.
- **Canon:** the Ancient Amulet glows at each Echoes clue and shows a fragment of the parents’ faces, Mira confesses she was the Wardens’ pupil before the Warding Rite (“Why didn’t you tell me?”), the curse is visibly worse at each relic site, the Phoenix links patience to a friend who had none, the Dragon echoes the refrain and the dream voice is “the friend who stayed behind”. Three optional virtue moments (truth, mercy, selfless) are recorded. The virtue tests remain fixed answers rather than branching choices.

## Act IV: The Final Confrontation

With all relics in hand, Elior and his companions infiltrate Malakar’s fortress. They navigate traps, confront illusions, and face Malakar’s lieutenants. In the Parents’ Vault, opened by the amulet, Elior learns that Malakar was his parents’ friend and fellow Warden, and that he did all this to bring them back; the ward that hides Elior and holds the wound shut is made of the parents themselves, and Elior carries it. In the throne room Malakar tempts Elior to join him, offering answers about his family in exchange for loyalty. Elior answers with the refrain.

**Key Events:**

- The player must make critical choices: refuse Malakar’s offer, show mercy or vengeance, and choose how the story ends.
- In the throne room one choice remains: the Lifelight Elixir breaks King Alden’s crystal prison or heals Malakar’s wound.
- With the help of his friends and the relics, King Alden is freed in every ending and Elior chooses how Malakar’s wound is closed: Redemption, Justice or Sacrifice.

**Highlights from the Act IV design** (see `Act4Design.md`):

- **Setting:** a fortress that mirrors Malakar's crumbling mind: the Mirror Labyrinth, Hall of Echoes, Tower of Shadows, Chamber of Whispers, Memory Vault, the Parents’ Vault, the Throne Chamber and, at the end, the field in Willowbrook.
- **Main quest – _The Final Stand_:** breach, lieutenant trials, the Parents' Vault and the final choice, after seven side quests. King Alden's rescue is part of the final choice.
- **Redemption over violence:** the **Shattered Ward Guardians** are dispelled and the **Trapped Servants** freed. The **Knight of Despair** and the **Sorceress of Lies** are redeemed, not slain. In the Memory Vault, Elior restores **the Shadow of the Past**, the uncorrupted remnant of Malakar.
- **The truth:** Malakar and Elior's parents were **friends and allies** who made the virtue relics to protect the kingdom. When catastrophe threatened them, Malakar reached for **time magic** to save them, and tore a temporal wound that feeds on life energy. That wound is both his corruption and the reason he drains King Alden. In _The Parent's Legacy_, a vault opened by the **Ancient Amulet** before the throne room, the parents' final memories show they **poured themselves into the ward** to hide Elior and hold the wound shut. The dream voice is revealed as the Shadow of the Past.
- **Climax:** Elior refuses Malakar's offer and says the refrain to him. The Lifelight Elixir then either breaks **King Alden's** crystal prison or is offered to heal Malakar's wound, and that one choice decides the ending.
- **Endings:** Redemption (the golden path), Justice and Sacrifice. All three end at home in Willowbrook; Crown and Home are no longer separate endings. Redemption is always open; Justice and Sacrifice open through the optional virtue moments of the whole game. When the wound is healed, the parents speak to Elior one last time and rest in peace.
- **Spells:** Truth Sight, Redemption's Light, Heart's Truth, Forgiveness and Grow.

## Epilogue

Peace returns to Eldoria. Elior is celebrated as a hero but chooses to return to Willowbrook, humbled by his journey. Mira, Cedric, and Nyx remain close allies, and the kingdom flourishes once more. His parents have spoken to him one last time and now rest in peace; In the last scene Elior walks out to the withered field from Act I, now green again, and casts `grow`.

What Elior believes and what is revealed, act by act, with the recurring threads.

“The ward is Elior himself” means the parents’ ward lives in Elior. Amulet, dream-voice and refrain placements beyond the canon’s wording are provisional until the act designs refine them. The final truth, told in Act IV and the Epilogue, is in the Story Canon: the parents are the ward, and rest in peace once the wound is healed.

## Story Path Summary

- **Beginning:** Elior’s awakening, his bond with his grandmother, and the mystery of his parents.
- **Middle:** Gathering allies, learning magic, retrieving relics, and facing moral choices.
- **End:** Confrontation with Malakar, saving the kingdom, and Elior’s return home to Willowbrook, where the withered field is green again as he casts `grow`.

The story adapts to player choices, offering three endings (Redemption, Justice and Sacrifice) shaped by optional virtue moments, but always centers on Elior’s growth from a simple farmer boy to the savior of Eldoria, and his quest to uncover the truth about his family.
