# Game Story: RetroQuest – The Awakening

## Story Canon

This is the settled truth behind the story. Every act design must agree with it.

- **The Wardens of the Dawn.** Elior's parents, **Aren** (father) and **Lysa** (mother), and **Malakar** were three equal friends who made the three virtue relics together: the Crystal of Light (courage), the Phoenix Feather (wisdom) and the Dragon's Scale (selflessness). They were fellow Wardens, not master and apprentices.
- **Why Malakar fell: grief, then power.** Malakar was the Warden of Wisdom. When the Wasting, a fever that reached Willowbrook the year before Elior was born, threatened his friends he could not wait: he reached for time magic to save them, tore a wound in time that feeds on life, and the power he found there corrupted him into wanting more. The Phoenix's riddle in Act III ("What is wisdom when time is short?" / "patience") names the virtue he failed.
- **The parents are the ward.** Aren and Lysa poured themselves into a seal that hides Elior and holds Malakar's wound shut. It is anchored in the **Ancient Amulet** and in King Alden's royal bindings, which is why Malakar drains the king; Alden, the Wardens' friend, gave his crown's oath to hold it willingly (the archive page in Act II and the Dragon's Memory in Act III both say so). The ward lives in Elior, and their gift is the magic he has: every spell that answers him came through them, all but `grow`, which he read from a stone in the glade (Act I says so at the stone: "this one you read, word by word, off the stone, and it feels like your own"). The Parents' Vault says this in so many words. The Sacrifice ending spends their gift to close what their friend opened, and `grow`, the one spell he did not inherit, is what remains. When the wound is healed, the parents speak to Elior one last time and then rest in peace. They are not alive, and nothing in the epilogue hints that they are.
- **Willowbrook was their home.** The family lived in Willowbrook. Grandmother is Lysa's mother and knows the truth. **Mira** was the Wardens' pupil; when the parents became the ward she settled in Willowbrook as its healer to watch over Elior. This is hinted in Act I and confessed in Act III.
- **The dream voice is the Shadow of the Past**, the uncorrupted remnant of Malakar, asking his friends' son for help. It feels familiar because Malakar held baby Elior. It is heard once per act, is never tied to a parent, and is met in Act IV's Memory Vault. Its four hearings are fragments of one sentence, heard whole in the vault and again through the amulet at the climax: "Be patient where I was not. Close what I opened, and let them rest." (Act I: "…and let them rest…"; Act II: "…close what I opened…"; Act III: "…be patient where I was not…").
- **The cradle song** ("Hush, little seed, the frost is going") is Lysa's. Grandmother hums it at the hearth in Act I and Elior knows it without knowing why; a woman hums it in the shell and the phantoms hum it in Act III; Malakar sings it to the baby in the Parents' Vault and hums it in the Redemption ending; Grandmother hums it at the gate.
- **The turned back.** The cloaked stranger is seen once in Act I, at the Old Mill, with his back to Elior, and leaves without turning. The amulet's third figure keeps his back turned through Act III. The Knight of Despair stands with his back to the door. Malakar looks at the wound, not at Elior, until the refrain turns him. In the Redemption ending the third figure has turned around.
- **Malakar's agent.** A cloaked stranger seen in Willowbrook is Malakar's agent, searching for the bloodline. His work feeds the same draining magic that is killing King Alden, so the blight has a single cause. (The Wasting that threatened Aren and Lysa is a separate sickness from this blight; Mira draws the line in Act III: "The Wasting killed. This drinks.") The same agent framed Sir Cedric.
- **Sir Cedric** was on guard the night King Alden vanished; Malakar's agents framed him, and the Secret Documents prove it.
- **The Sorceress of Lies** (an Act IV lieutenant) left her mark on Elena's curse in Act II. The curse was bait: she watched the inn door to see which healer would come for a mark like that, and the healer from Willowbrook never did (Mira burned Lyria's letter rather than leave Elior). She says so in her confession.
- **The fortress was the Wardens' hall.** The three made the relics there, at a workbench under the dome that is now the Throne Chamber. Mira walked its road as a girl and says so when she teleports the party there; the Warden sigil shows through the black stone at the gate arch. The Secret Documents name the place ("the bastion on the shattered ridge, north of the hills"), and Nyx's vision shows the king in crystal, which is how Cedric names him.
- **Malakar's name in Willowbrook.** The village uses it as a byword for bad luck and bad weather, with a knock on wood, and never as a living man; the one voice that names him as a man is the mother's letter ("He is not the friend we knew"). That is why Elior arrives in Greendale still half-believing Malakar is a tale (row II of the red line).
- **The refrain** "Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth." is carved on the moss-covered stone (Act I), spoken by Nyx (Act II), echoed by the Dragon (Act III) and said by Elior to Malakar (Act IV).
- **Three endings:** **Redemption** (the golden path: forgiveness heals the wound), **Justice** (Malakar is defeated and sealed away) and **Sacrifice** (Elior gives up his magic to seal the wound). All three end at home in Willowbrook; Crown and Home are no longer separate endings. The closing image is the withered field from Act I, green again, as Elior casts `grow`.
- **Virtue moments:** two or three optional moments per act (mercy, truth, help that gains nothing). They never block the golden path and add no new puzzles. They are remembered across acts and decide which endings open, plus a few lines of dialogue. Old saves are rejected when the save format changes; no migration is written.

## Prologue

Elior, a humble farmer boy, lives in the quiet village of Willowbrook on the outskirts of Eldoria. Raised by his grandmother after his parents vanished, Elior’s life is simple—tending crops and caring for animals. He believes they simply vanished, and he longs to uncover the truth behind their disappearance. One stormy night, a strange light appears in the sky, and Elior dreams of a shadowy figure calling his name—a voice that feels achingly familiar. It is the first time he hears the dream voice, which is not tied to either parent.

## Act I: The Awakening

Elior awakens to find his crops withered and the village in distress. The healer, Mira, senses a dark magic at work and reveals to Elior that he possesses latent magical abilities. She gives him an ancient amulet, hinting that it once belonged to someone important in his family, and sends him toward the nearby town of Greendale, where those who know what the amulet is will find him. A cloaked stranger has been seen in the village, and Elior learns that the amulet was his parents’ and that someone has come to Willowbrook looking for it. The refrain “Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.” is carved on the moss-covered stone in the glade.

**Key Events:**

- Elior learns basic magic from Mira and begins to question his family's past.
- The player explores Willowbrook, helping villagers and discovering clues about the darkness and Elior’s parents.
- Elior sets out for Greendale, facing minor obstacles and learning to use his powers.

**Highlights from the Act I design** (see `Act1Design.md`):

- **Setting:** fifteen rooms around Willowbrook: the cottage, field, coop, square, Mira's hut, forge, store, well, the Abandoned Shed (the father's workshop), the Old Mill (built on an old seat of power), the Riverbank (the bend from the photograph), Forest Path, Hidden Glade, chapel and the road to Greendale.
- **Opening:** Elior wakes after the dream. His journal records the voice and the three words he caught at its end ("…and let them rest…"). Grandmother is humming the cradle tune at the hearth when he comes in and stops; told the three words she goes quiet ("Some voices are older than memory"). She teaches him his first spell, `revive`, before he may leave the house. Spells are learned early but fizzle until Mira unlocks his magic.
- **Main quest – _Shadows Over Willowbrook_:** withered crops, a soured well and frightened villagers. The well was poisoned on purpose: a black-iron **rune token** comes up with the ring, and the Old Mill's whirring **mechanism** is the stranger's device, draining the power under the mill until the rope jams it. Mira names the token a fouling sigil, says the stranger came to her door asking for the amulet, and that the sickness in the land and the king's feel like one wound. Once the token is found, the next time Elior walks into the Old Mill the stranger is there, a man in a grey cloak with his back to him, and leaves toward the river without turning. The **Rare Flower**, found in the glade where the calm **Magical Deer** waits, turns Elior's practice magic real (`heal`, `unlock`, `light`).
- **The family thread:** the **faded photograph** ("Seek the truth where the river bends") leads through Grandmother to the Fisherman, the mother's friend, and to the **mother's letter** in a tin under the smooth stone ("If Malakar comes asking about our line, do not answer him. He is not the friend we knew"). The **locket** in the chapel is the founders' heirloom of the mother's line, hidden the night she left, when the bell rang by itself. The shed is the father's workshop; the mysterious box opens only to Elior's magic and holds the map and his father's note, "For when the light comes back to you." The ring from the well is the mother's wedding ring (_A & L_).
- **Side quests:** _Hint of Magic_, _Know your village_, _Curiosity killed the cat_ (Patches, Hemlock's cat, in the dark shed), _Fishing expedition_ (`purify`, which the mother taught the Fisherman), _Oh, deer, oh deer_ (rest in the glade), _Magnet fishing expedition_ (the ring and the token), _Let there be light_ (candle, locket, `bless`), _Magic for real_, _Preparing for the road_ (cloak, protection, four foods, boots, map and every basic spell) and _The Faded Photograph_.
- **People:** Grandmother (Lysa's mother; "your mother's gift", "Some voices are older than memory"), Mira ("I knew your parents better than I have said"), Tilda the baker's wife (the king is ill, the roads unsafe, a cloaked stranger asked after an old amulet), Borin the blacksmith, Hemlock, the Fisherman, the Priest, the Merchant and the Deer.
- **Spells:** `revive`, `purify`, `bless`, `heal`, `unlock`, `light`, and `grow` (read from the moss-covered stone in the glade).
- **Choices:** keep the mother's ring or trade it; let Patches stay with Grandmother. Three optional virtue moments: the truth to Tilda about the well (truth), the fresh bread for the hungry Merchant (selfless), the fox kit cut from the snare (mercy).
- **Ending:** Mira presents the **Ancient Amulet** ("May this guide and protect you"; "show the amulet to no one lightly, those who know what it is will find you"). On the road Grandmother catches up, straightens the mother's cloak, names **Aren and Lysa** for the first time and says someone has come to Willowbrook looking for the amulet. The chapel bell rings once with no hand on the rope, and the map reveals the hidden road.
- **Canon:** the amulet reacts to the locket, the letter, the jammed mill and the glade's stone; the refrain is carved beneath the `grow` inscription and Elior does not know what it means; the dream voice is familiar but unplaced and never tied to a parent. Malakar is named as a village byword (Tilda's "Malakar's weather", Hemlock's "Malakar's luck") and once as a man, in the mother's letter; the Wardens and the ward are not named, and Mira does not name Sir Cedric.

## Act II: The Gathering

In Greendale, Elior meets Sir Cedric, who recognizes the Warden sigil of the Wardens of the Dawn on the amulet. Cedric, in disgrace for the night King Alden vanished, pledges his loyalty once the Secret Documents clear his name, and guides Elior to the Enchanted Forest, where they must find Nyx, the forest sprite, to unlock the next stage of Elior’s powers. Elior has believed Malakar a legend from old books; by the end of the act he learns Malakar is real and seeks the Willowbrook bloodline, which means Elior. Nyx speaks the refrain, the dream voice is heard again, and the Sorceress of Lies’ mark is found on Elena’s curse.

**Key Events:**

- The player navigates Greendale, gathering supplies and information.
- Cedric joins the party, offering combat and guidance.
- In the Enchanted Forest, the player solves riddles and puzzles set by Nyx.
- Nyx reveals the legend of Malakar, the evil sorcerer, hinting that Malakar once sought something in Willowbrook.

**Highlights from the Act II design** (see `Act2Design.md`):

- **Setting:** Greendale, a trade city of gates, markets, a castle, the **Silver Stag Inn** and a hidden library, and the threshold of the Enchanted Forest, which deepens from the standing stones of the Forest Transition to the Heart of the Forest. There is no combat; the one bout is Cedric's sparring test.
- **Opening page:** quotes only what Act I said (Mira's parting words, Grandmother's warning) and adds that Malakar, to Elior, is a word from Willowbrook, a tale to frighten children, and one line in his mother's hand.
- **Arrival:** Elior enters on an **Entry Pass** found at the stranger's own abandoned camp, its name scraped off. The Mountain Hermit, Cedric's old drill sergeant, gives him Cedric's notched training sword.
- **Sir Cedric:** stripped of his knighthood for being on guard the night King Alden vanished. On the first talk he stops mid-sentence: the amulet bears the **Warden sigil of the Wardens of the Dawn**. _The Knight's Test_ (guard, turn, strike) earns his respect; _Cedric's Lost Honor_ clears his name with the **Secret Documents** from **Caravan Master Thorne**'s lost caravan, which show Malakar's agents bribed the watch and framed him under the cloaked stranger's seal, and name where the king was carried: the bastion on the shattered ridge. He then gives Nature's Charm.
- **The archive:** the stranger's **notebook** under the inn floorboard copies the castle's birth rolls; the Historians find the Willowbrook roll missing the page for the year Elior was born. _Echoes of the Past_ and the **Ancient Chronicle**, kept by the **Spectral Librarian** (a Warden in life), name the Wardens, their sigil, the king who lent the crown's binding to their last great work, and Malakar's search for the Willowbrook bloodline. The castle is half-empty and the throne room barred.
- **Greendale's people:** **Master Healer Lyria** takes Elior on as an apprentice (`greater_heal`). When she explains how Elena's curse is lifted she adds that she wrote to the healer at Willowbrook, Mira, about the mark weeks ago, and Mira never came and never answered. **Innkeeper Marcus**'s daughter **Elena** is cured in three steps (`greater_heal`, Crystal-Clear Water, `dispel`); her curse carries a silver **mask-shaped mark**, the Sorceress of Lies'. Brenn, the caravan guard who fled, waits in the warehouse.
- **Forest quests:** _The Hermit's Warning_ (the Protective Charm), _The Forest Guardian's Riddles_ (the Water Nymphs: water, mask, truth) and _Whispers in the Wind_ (the Ancient Tree Spirit, the Enchanted Acorn, moonflowers and water). `forest_speech` finds the caravan in the ravine.
- **Main quest – _The Gathering Storm_:** eleven side quests build up to summoning **Nyx** at the Offering Altar with the three charms. She speaks the refrain and grants `prophetic_vision`, Nyx's Token and the Forest Heart Crystal. The vision, cast with Cedric present, shows a village, a withered field, a robed figure reaching toward Elior and the dream voice once more ("Do not be afraid of me", and fainter, "…close what I opened…"), and above it all a crowned man in a prison of crystal, grey light running out of him. Cedric: "The man in the crystal. That was the king. That was Alden. Then Malakar is no tale, and it is you he is hunting." Elior hears himself say what the missing birth-roll page and the stranger's hunt add up to: "He took them. That is why they vanished, and that is why he wants me." Cedric does not correct him; he does not know either. Elior may then answer that the voice did not sound like a hunter (`say truth to cedric`), or simply talk to him, to close the act.
- **Spells:** `mend`, `dispel`, `greater_heal`, `nature_sense`, `forest_speech`, `prophetic_vision`.
- **Choices:** three optional virtue moments: the Mountain Flower for the sick child (selfless), the truth to Marcus that the curse was set on purpose (truth), speaking for Brenn so Thorne keeps him (mercy).
- **Canon:** Cedric recognises the Warden sigil in his first conversation, King Alden has vanished and the castle is half-empty, the Secret Documents show Malakar's agents framed Cedric, the archive and Chronicle name the Wardens and the hunt for the bloodline, the Sorceress of Lies' mark is on Elena's curse, and Nyx speaks the refrain and shows the Shadow of the Past. Nobody names Aren, Lysa or any friendship with Malakar.

## Act III: Trials and Revelations

With Nyx’s help, Elior unlocks new magical abilities. The group learns that Malakar has captured King Alden and is draining his life force; the same draining magic is behind the blight over Eldoria. To save the king, Elior must retrieve three ancient relics hidden across the kingdom: the Crystal of Light, the Phoenix Feather, and the Dragon’s Scale. Each relic represents a virtue Elior must embrace—courage, wisdom, and selflessness. Along the way, Elior faces Malakar’s minions and optional moments of mercy, truth and selfless help that shape which endings open.

**Key Events:**

- The player embarks on quests to retrieve each relic:
    - **Crystal of Light (Courage):** Hidden in the Sunken Ruins, guarded by water spirits.
    - **Phoenix Feather (Wisdom):** Located atop Mount Ember, requiring the player to solve elemental puzzles.
    - **Dragon’s Scale (Selflessness):** Deep within the Caverns of Shadow, protected by a sleeping dragon.
- Optional virtue moments (mercy, truth, help that gains nothing) are remembered and decide which endings open; they never block the golden path.
- Elior has believed his parents were Malakar’s victims. He learns they were Malakar’s closest friends and that they are warded away, not dead; Mira confesses she was the Wardens’ pupil. The Dragon echoes the refrain, and the dream voice is heard again.

**Highlights from the Act III design** (see `Act3Design.md`):

- **Opening:** the opening page quotes what Elior said in the courtyard, "He took them", now the belief he carries, and tells how Mira came to Greendale the night of the vision, drew a chalk circle in the castle courtyard and brought the party to her hut in Willowbrook; she has not yet said how a village healer learned to do that. At her hut Mira reveals the stakes: if King Alden dies, the bindings that protect Eldoria unravel. She gives Elior her **Keepsake Note** and **teleports** the party (Elior, Mira and Cedric) between three six-room relic sites. The curse is visibly worse at each (dead sea birds and grey surf, ash rain, silent mines), and Mira says the king is weaker at each departure.
- **Main quest – _The Three Virtues_:** each site has two side quests and a trial that tests its virtue:
    - **Sunken Ruins (Courage):** _Tideward Sigils_ and _Lanterns of the Deeps_. In the sanctum the Tide-Born Guardian puts out every light and asks Elior to walk: three steps east into black water, each with a line of fear, then the vow of what he did not abandon, **"myself"**. The Guardian tests him as Aren asked to have later visitors tested.
    - **Mount Ember (Wisdom):** _Mirrors of Emberlight_ (four brass segments, resin, `mend`, then rest till the noon beam fires) and _Breath of the Mountain_ (a heat ward, then the vents crossed only on their in-breath). The Phoenix appears only to one who rests and asks _"What is wisdom when time is short?"_: **"patience"**, the virtue a Warden lacked. The Ash Scholar holds Malakar's charred notebook: the good years, and the Wasting reaching Willowbrook.
    - **Caverns of Shadow (Selflessness):** _Miners' Rescue_ and _Oath of Stillness_ (bless the echo stones, still the pool with the Stillwater Phial, chant). The Echo Chambers replay the night the three Wardens parted. A wise, tired **Ancient Dragon** asks what Elior will return for what he takes: **"everything"**. Taking the scale weakens the prison it keeps.
- **Storytelling quest – _Echoes of the Hidden Bond_:** three clues, and the amulet glows at each, its picture sharpening: the **Sea-Sealed Letter** (Aren and Lysa, fellow Wardens, begging their friend Malakar not to reach for time magic; "You would have me wait while you die. — M."), the **Charred Inscription** (three hands, three virtues, Malakar's name struck deeper by his own hand) and the **Dragon's Memory** (the parents became the ward and are _"warded away, not dead"_; the ward is anchored in the amulet and the king's bindings, the king being their friend who gave his crown's oath to hold it, which is why Malakar drains the king). Act IV completes the truth: the ward is a seal that hides Elior and that Elior himself carries.
- **The lullaby:** the Polished Shell on the shore hums a woman's tune; the phantoms in the Stillness Vestibule hum it too; it is the tune of the dream voice.
- **Mira:** one slip per site (Lysa's crossed sevens; the Phoenix's line finished; the Dragon's "Little Mira"). Elior can ask her about his parents, Malakar, the amulet and Grandmother; before the confession she evades, after it she answers. Before the rite she confesses: she was the Wardens' pupil, settled in Willowbrook to watch over Elior, and Grandmother knew. "Why didn't you tell me?" She also answers for Lyria's letter: she burned it, because going would have left Elior alone in the one place Malakar was looking. Elior may forgive her or reproach her before the **Warding Rite** fuses the relics into the **Lifelight Elixir**. Teleporting the party to the fortress she says she knows the road: she walked it as a girl, when it was the Wardens' hall, where the relics were made at a workbench under the dome. The Warden sigil shows through the black stone at the gate arch.
- **Sir Cedric:** carries his guilt over the night the king was taken, one line per site; at the fortress gate Elior may answer him with the refrain. Mira refuses to stay behind and goes through the gates.
- **Choices:** four optional virtue moments: the Pier Warden's truth (truth), rest for the phantoms (mercy), mending the Dragon's prison (selfless), mending Malakar's struck name (mercy).
- **Canon:** the Ancient Amulet glows at each Echoes clue and shows the parents' faces, the third figure with his back turned; the Phoenix links patience to a friend who had none; the Dragon echoes the refrain and names the dream voice "the friend who stayed behind"; the voice says "Help me. I cannot hold it shut alone" and, almost lost, "…be patient where I was not…". The virtue tests are fixed answers, not branching choices.

## Act IV: The Final Confrontation

With all relics in hand, Elior and his companions infiltrate Malakar’s fortress. They navigate traps, confront illusions, and face Malakar’s lieutenants. In the Parents’ Vault, opened by the amulet, Elior learns that Malakar was his parents’ friend and fellow Warden, and that he did all this to bring them back; the ward that hides Elior and holds the wound shut is made of the parents themselves, and Elior carries it. In the throne room Malakar tempts Elior to join him, offering answers about his family in exchange for loyalty. Elior answers with the refrain.

**Key Events:**

- Malakar’s offer is true and terrible: his parents alive again, at the price of the king, the kingdom’s years and Elior himself. Elior refuses with the refrain.
- In the throne room one choice remains: the Lifelight Elixir breaks King Alden’s crystal prison or heals Malakar’s wound.
- With the help of his friends and the relics, King Alden is freed in every ending and Elior chooses how Malakar’s wound is closed: Redemption, Justice or Sacrifice.

**Highlights from the Act IV design** (see `Act4Design.md`):

- **Setting:** a fortress that mirrors Malakar's mind, orderly at the gates and grieving deeper in: Fortress Gates, Outer Courtyard, Mirror Labyrinth, Hall of Echoes, Tower of Shadows, Chamber of Whispers, Memory Vault, the Parents' Vault, the Throne Chamber and, at the end, Willowbrook Field. Mira and Cedric hold the threshold; Elior goes in alone.
- **Main quest – _The Final Stand_:** breach, lieutenant trials, the Parents' Vault and the final choice, after seven side quests. King Alden's rescue is part of the final choice.
- **The breach:** the **Shattered Ward Guardians** are dispelled with the ward stones and `light`, the **Trapped Servants** freed with Guardian's Essence and `bless`. The freed **Captain of the Guard** tells of the night the king was taken; giving him the Guardian's Chain is the act's selfless moment. The **Mirror Labyrinth** is three turns, walked where the reflection does not follow, and teaches `truth_sight`.
- **Redemption over violence:** the **Knight of Despair** in the Hall is the cloaked stranger of Acts I and II; `truth_sight` shows the grey cloak and the seal of the Secret Documents. He searched Willowbrook, framed Cedric and despairs because he no longer believes the king can be saved; redeemed with a word, he asks Elior to carry his apology to Cedric. The **Tower of Shadows** is passed by naming the friends who stayed behind (Mira, Cedric, Nyx). The **Sorceress of Lies** carries the silver mask that was on Elena's curse; she cursed Elena on Malakar's orders and watched the inn door for the healer who would come (the one from Willowbrook never did), has never been spoken to without a lie, and her lie that Elior's parents sold him is her own story. In the Memory Vault, Elior restores **the Shadow of the Past**, the uncorrupted remnant of Malakar; it says whole the sentence Elior has heard in pieces since the first dream, and the word is patience.
- **The truth:** Malakar and Elior's parents were **friends and fellow Wardens** who made the virtue relics together. When the Wasting threatened them, Malakar reached for **time magic** to save them and tore a wound that feeds on life. That wound is both his corruption and the reason he drains King Alden. In _The Parent's Legacy_, a vault opened by the **Ancient Amulet** before the throne room, the parents' final memories show they **poured themselves into the ward** to hide Elior and hold the wound shut, and Malakar rocking baby Elior, humming the cradle song.
- **Climax:** Malakar's offer is true and costly: break the ward and the days run back to before the fever, and Aren and Lysa wake at the workbench; the price is the king, the kingdom's years since, and Elior himself, who was born after the tear. Elior answers with the refrain. The amulet opens, the Shadow speaks through it, and the Lifelight Elixir either heals Malakar's wound or breaks **King Alden's** crystal prison, and that one choice decides the ending.
- **Endings:** Redemption (the golden path), Justice and Sacrifice. All three end at home in Willowbrook; Crown and Home are no longer separate endings. No ending is closed by the virtue record: the moments of the whole game colour the text (two truth moments add a line from Alden in Justice, two selfless moments a line from Aren in Sacrifice, each mercy moment a line from Malakar and the knight). When the wound is closed, the parents speak to Elior one last time and rest in peace.
- **Epilogue:** Grandmother hums the cradle song at the gate; Cedric receives the captain's and the knight's apologies; Mira carries the Sorceress's to Elena; Nyx's light in the hedge says the refrain one last time; Elior casts `grow` and the field is green. "Welcome home."
- **Spells:** Truth Sight, Redemption's Light, Heart's Truth, Forgiveness (Redemption only) and Grow.

## Epilogue

Peace returns to Eldoria. Elior is celebrated as a hero but chooses to return to Willowbrook, humbled by his journey. Mira, Cedric, and Nyx remain close allies, and the kingdom flourishes once more. His parents have spoken to him one last time and now rest in peace. In the last scene Elior walks out to the withered field from Act I, Grandmother humming his mother's cradle song at the gate, and casts `grow`; the field turns green, Nyx's light says the refrain once more, and Grandmother says "Welcome home."

## Story Path Summary

- **Beginning:** Elior’s awakening, his bond with his grandmother, and the mystery of his parents.
- **Middle:** Gathering allies, learning magic, retrieving relics, and facing moral choices.
- **End:** Confrontation with Malakar, saving the kingdom, and Elior’s return home to Willowbrook, where the withered field is green again as he casts `grow`.

The story adapts to player choices, offering three endings (Redemption, Justice and Sacrifice) shaped by optional virtue moments, but always centers on Elior’s growth from a simple farmer boy to the savior of Eldoria, and his quest to uncover the truth about his family.
