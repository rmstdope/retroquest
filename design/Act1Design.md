# Act I Rooms: Willowbrook & Surroundings

## Quests in Act I

- **Name:** Hint of Magic

  - **Trigger:** Starting of the game.
  - **Completion:** Learn the 'revive' spell from Grandmother.

- **Name:** Know your village

  - **Trigger:** Leave Elior's Cottage and begin exploring Willowbrook.
  - **Completion:** Visit all areas in the village.

- **Name:** Curiosity killed the cat

  - **Trigger:** Enter the Abandoned Shed for the first time.
  - **Completion:** Unlock the shed door.

- **Name:** Fishing expedition

  - **Trigger:** Talk to the fisherman for the first time.
  - **Completion:** Give a fish to the fisherman.

- **Name:** Oh, deer, oh deer

  - **Trigger:** Learn about the magical deer from the Blacksmith.
  - **Completion:** Observe the deer in the Hidden Glade.

- **Name:** Magnet fishing expedition

  - **Trigger:** Assemble the magnetic fishing rod.
  - **Completion:** Retrieve the shiny ring from the well.

- **Name:** Let there be light

  - **Trigger:** Talk to the Priest for the first time.
  - **Completion:** Light the candle.

- **Name:** Magic for real

  - **Trigger:** Learn your first spell.
  - **Completion:** Be taught magic by Mira.

- **Name:** Preparing for the road

  - **Trigger:** Learn magic from Mira.
  - **Completion:** Obtain all items needed for the journey.

- **Name:** The Faded Photograph

  - **Trigger:** Search Elior's Cottage and discover the photograph.
  - **Completion:** Examine the photograph to read its message, then show it to Grandmother.
  - **Reward:** Grandmother reveals that Elior's family is connected to the darkness threatening Eldoria.

- **Name:** Shadows Over Willowbrook (Main Quest)
  - **Trigger:** At the start of the act; Elior's dream introduces the mystery.
  - **Update:** Investigate the withered crops in the Vegetable Field.
  - **Update:** Speak with a villager about the disturbances in Willowbrook.
  - **Update:** Examine the Village Well.
  - **Update:** Discuss the crops, villagers' fears and well with Mira; she urges Elior to connect with nature.
  - **Update:** Give the Rare Flower to Mira to unlock Elior's magic; she promises him the Ancient Amulet once he is ready for the road.
  - **Completion:** When act 1 is completed

## Rooms
### 1. Elior’s Cottage

- **Description:** A modest cottage with a straw roof and a cozy hearth.
- **Items:** Lantern (start), bread (later), Elior’s journal (later), travel cloak (later).
- **Spells:** `revive` (learned from Grandmother).
- **Usable Items:** Lantern (lights dark areas), Elior's journal (read for story), hidden locket (give to Grandmother).
- **Characters:** Grandmother (offers advice, can be spoken to).
- **Exits:** Vegetable Field (south), Village Square (east)

### 2. Vegetable Field

- **Description:** Rows of withered crops under a gray sky.
- **Items:** rusty hoe (start), dull knife (start), Withered carrot (start), coin (1st) (later), coin (2nd) (later), carrot (later).
- **Spells:** None.
- **Usable Items:** Hoe (uncovers hidden coins), `revive` spell (on `Withered carrot` to transform it into `carrot`).
- **Characters:** None.
- **Exits:** Elior’s Cottage (north), Village Well (east), Chicken Coop (south)

### 3. Chicken Coop

- **Description:** A small wooden coop with nervous chickens.
- **Items:** egg (start), key (later).
- **Unusable:** feather (start), chicken (start).
- **Spells:** None.
- **Usable Items:** Bread (feed chickens, they reveal a hidden key).
- **Characters:** None.
- **Exits:** Vegetable Field (north)

### 4. Village Square

- **Description:** The heart of Willowbrook, with an old stone fountain and a notice board.
- **Items:** Bucket (start).
- **Unusable:** old notice (start).
- **Spells:** None.
- **Usable Items:** (None based on Golden Path actions in this room).
- **Characters:** Villager (shares rumors, can be questioned).
- **Exits:** Elior’s Cottage (west), Mira’s Hut (north), General Store (east)

### 5. Mira’s Hut

- **Usable Items:** Rare flower (give to Mira).

### 7. General Store

- **Description:** A cluttered shop with shelves of goods.

The Sword and Armor have no use in Act I and cannot be bought with the two coins available in the act.
- **Spells:** None.
- **Usable Items:** Coin (2nd, purchase rope and receive an apple bonus).
- **Characters:** Shopkeeper (sells goods, shares gossip).

### 8. Village Well

- **Description:** An old stone well. Its water is dark, still and sour-smelling until the well is purified, and crystal clear afterwards (the room description follows the well's state). No rope hangs by it.
- **Items:** Shiny ring (later), Well (start).
- **Spells:** None.
- **Usable Items:** Bucket (draws murky, sour-smelling water before the well is purified; clear water after), fishing rod + magnet + stick (assemble an extended magnetic fishing rod to retrieve the ring after purifying the well), `purify` spell (target the Well item after examining it).

- **Exits:** Vegetable Field (west), Blacksmith’s Forge (east), Abandoned Shed (south)

### 13. Hidden Glade

- **Description:** A peaceful clearing with sunlight filtering through trees.
- **Items:** Rare flower (later).
- **Unusable:** shiny pebble (start).
- **Spells:** `grow` (learned from inscriptions on a stone).
- **Usable Items:** `light` spell (cast in the glade to illuminate the moss-covered stone).
- **Characters:** Deer (present only when Elior rests in the room after having been informed about the deer).
- **Exits:** Forest Path (north), Village Chapel (south)

### 14. Village Chapel

- **Description:** A small stone chapel with stained glass windows. A dark corner by the altar swallows the light (the locket is not mentioned until the candle reveals it).
- **Items:** Candle (start), hidden locket (later).
- **Unusable:** prayer book (start).
- **Spells:** `bless` (learned when picking up the hidden locket; the Priest recognizes it).
- **Usable Items:** Candle (use with matches, lights dark corners, reveals a hidden locket), hidden locket (picking it up teaches `bless`; using it opens it to the engraving _From the founders' line, to the next._), `bless` spell (cast on self for protection).
- **Characters:** Priest (offers blessings, shares lore). His first talk tells the story of the founders, their relic and the bell; after that he answers in one short line that follows Elior's progress, the latest milestone winning: blessed by Elior (`cast bless on priest`) → Elior has cast `bless` on himself → Elior carries the locket (he points him to Grandmother) → the locket has gone home → the candle is lit but the locket not yet taken (he points to the corner) → otherwise he asks after the matches.

**The locket's history.** The locket is the founders' relic: an heirloom bearing the founders' crest (a willow above a bell) that has passed down Elior's mother's family, from Grandmother to her daughter. Elior's mother hid it in the chapel the night she left; that night the chapel bell rang at midnight with no hand on the rope. The Priest knows the legend and recognises the crest of the founders' line; Grandmother recognises it as her daughter's. This quietly sets up Elior's heritage and Act II's bloodline research.
- **Exits:** Hidden Glade (north), Road to Greendale (east)

### 15. Road to Greendale

- **Description:** The main road leaving Willowbrook, lined with ancient oaks.
- **Items:** wandering boots (later - from Merchant).
- **Unusable:** Rusty saw (start).
- **Spells:** None.
- **Usable Items:** Shiny ring (trade with Merchant), map (reveals shortcut to Greendale).
- **Characters:** Merchant (sells wares, provides travel advice).
- **Exits:** Village Chapel (west)

## Learnable Spells in Act I

- **`revive`**: Learned from Grandmother in Elior’s Cottage (Step 1).
- **`purify`**: Learned from the Fisherman at the Riverbank after giving him a fish (Step 12).
- **`bless`**: Learned when picking up the Hidden Locket in the Village Chapel; the Priest recognizes the relic (Step 17).
- **`heal`**: Learned from Mira in Mira’s Hut after giving her the Rare Flower (Step 18).
- **`unlock`**: Learned from Mira in Mira’s Hut after giving her the Rare Flower (Step 18).
- **`light`**: Learned from Mira in Mira’s Hut after giving her the Rare Flower (Step 18).
- **`grow`**: Learned from inscriptions on a moss-covered stone in the Hidden Glade after casting `light` on it (Step 22).

## Action that completes the act:

After receiving the Ancient Amulet from Mira, completing the _Preparing for the road_ quest, and completing every other side quest, use the `map` on the Road to Greendale. This unlocks the path to Greendale and triggers the transition to Act II.

    - Examine the photograph to read the message on its back, then show it to Grandmother to learn more about Elior's family.
