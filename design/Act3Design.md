# Act III: Trials and Revelations

## Narrative Overview

The act opens on the belief Elior voiced in Greendale’s courtyard with the vision still fading, “He took them. That is why they vanished, and that is why he wants me,” which nobody corrected. Mira came to Greendale the night of the vision (she had felt the amulet open to the forest), drew a chalk circle in the castle courtyard and brought Elior and Cedric to her hut in Willowbrook; how a village healer learned to do that is not yet said. Elior leaves the forest carrying the echo of Nyx’s last words: “Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.” The air feels thinner now, charged with the knowledge that Malakar holds King Alden captive and leeches his life to call forth a kingdom-wide curse. The path ahead is no longer about learning who Elior might become—it is about deciding the kind of person he will be while the kingdom watches.

Act III unfolds as a pilgrimage across Eldoria to recover three ancient relics—each bound to a virtue that shapes Elior’s character: Courage, Wisdom, and Selflessness. As Elior travels with Mira and Sir Cedric, he confronts enemies and riddles. He learns more of the creeping weight of his parents’ mystery. Elior has believed his parents were Malakar’s victims. By the end of the act he knows better: **Aren** and **Lysa** were Malakar’s closest friends, fellow Wardens of the Dawn who made the three virtue relics together with him, and the Dragon tells him they are **warded away, not dead**, though he does not yet know what that means. At each relic site the Ancient Amulet glows and the curse over the land is visibly worse, and the dream voice returns once, in the Dragon’s Hall.

## Journey Between Relic Sites

Mira, drawing on her mastery of arcane teleportation, whisks the party between the distant relic sites. Her spells bend space, allowing Elior, Cedric, and herself to traverse Eldoria’s vast reaches in moments—first to the storm-lashed coast, then to the volcanic slopes, and finally deep beneath the hills. Elior must read Mira’s Keepsake Note before the first departure. Before leaving the Sunken Ruins and Mount Ember, Elior must also uncover their parentage clues: the Sea-Sealed Letter and Charred Inscription. After securing all three relics and completing every non-main quest, Mira confesses who she is (see *Mira’s Confession*), and then performs the Warding Rite, creating the Lifelight Elixir and completing “The Three Virtues.” She can then teleport the party to the fortress threshold, saying she knows the road: she walked it as a girl, when it was the Wardens’ hall and not his fortress, and Elior’s parents made the relics there at a workbench under the dome. There she refuses to rest behind and goes through the gates with Elior (she holds the way out just inside with Sir Cedric). Examining the fortress gates marks Act III complete and begins Act IV.

**Sir Cedric's guilt.** Cedric walks with Elior from room to room and carries his guilt over the night King Alden was taken: one line per site and quest stage, a reaction after each of Mira's three "the king is weaker" beats, his answer when the Tide-Born Guardian asks what he will not abandon, his impatience on Mount Ember, the heavy lifting in the Caverns, and a gate line at the fortress that Elior may answer with the refrain. He speaks as a plain soldier; the full confession is left for the Knight of Despair in Act IV.

## The Stakes

-   Malakar’s ritual drains King Alden’s essence. If the king falls, the binding magics protecting Eldoria unravel.
-   The relics are needed to counter the ritual and shield Elior from Malakar’s influence.
-   The curse is visibly worse at every relic site, because the same draining magic that is killing King Alden is eating the land (see *The Curse Worsens*).
-   The relics were made by three friends. Malakar’s wisdom failed when time was short; the Phoenix’s riddle names the virtue he lacked.

## The Relics and Their Trials

### 1) Crystal of Light — Courage (Sunken Ruins)

The journey leads to the coast where the sea swallows a shattered temple known as the Sunken Ruins. At dusk, stone causeways rise from the surf, slick with salt and seaweed. Spectral water-wardens—spirits bound to guard the ruins—test intruders with fear itself.

-   Trial: In the sanctum the tide-born guardian puts out every light and asks Elior to walk. He wades three steps east into the black water (west turns back, no penalty), each step with a line of fear. On the third the water parts and the Crystal of Light is there; the guardian asks what he did not abandon. The accepted vow is “myself.” Near-miss answers (Mira, Cedric, friends, the king, parents) get gentle, specific refusals. After the vow the sigils kindle again and the sanctum is lit for good.
-   Curse: dead sea birds line the causeways and the surf runs grey (see *The Curse Worsens*).
-   Amulet: when Elior reads the Sea-Sealed Letter, the Ancient Amulet glows and shows a fragment of two faces, a man and a woman, laughing beside a third figure turned away.

Result: Elior claims the Crystal. The causeways collapse behind them; the sea keeps its secrets.

### 2) Phoenix Feather — Wisdom (Mount Ember)

Mount Ember is an old volcano whose heart pulses with elemental puzzles. Its slopes are etched with obsidian channels and brass mirrors that once guided sunlight to a high altar.

-   Guardian: A phoenix wrought of ember and memory rises from a crater. It asks, “What is wisdom when time is short?” The accepted answer is “patience.” After both Mount Ember side quests are complete, the Phoenix offers the Feather. The Phoenix links patience to a friend who had none: it tells Elior that the Feather was made for a Warden who could not wait when time was short, and who is the reason the answer matters. It does not yet name Malakar to Elior; the Charred Inscription and the Dragon do.
-   Curse: ash falls like rain over the slopes (see *The Curse Worsens*).
-   Amulet: when Elior reads the Charred Inscription, the Ancient Amulet glows again and the faces sharpen: the woman is Lysa, and the third figure still has his back turned.

Result: The Phoenix offers a single Feather, warm as a heartbeat.

### 3) Dragon’s Scale — Selflessness (Caverns of Shadow)

Beneath Eldoria’s oldest hills, the Caverns of Shadow breathe a chill that dims torches. There, a dragon sleeps—not a beast of greed, but a creature of burdens, keeping darkness pinned beneath its coils.

-   The Dragon: Ancient, wise, and tired. It knows Elior’s parents’ names, Aren and Lysa, and calls them Wardens of the Dawn. It reveals that they made the relics together with their friend Malakar, and that they gave themselves to hide Elior and to hold shut the wound Malakar tore in time: they are warded away, not dead. The ward is anchored in the Ancient Amulet and in King Alden’s royal bindings; Alden was the Wardens’ friend and gave his crown’s oath to hold it, which is why Malakar drains the king. The dragon guards a scale that can ward a soul—but taking it without due cause weakens the prison it maintains.
-   The refrain: the Dragon echoes “Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.”
-   The dream voice: after the Dragon’s Memory, the voice from the Prologue speaks once more in the hall, asking Elior for help in words he almost knows (“Help me. I cannot hold it shut alone”), and almost lost in the dragon’s breathing, “…be patient where I was not…”, the third fragment of the sentence heard whole in Act IV. The Dragon answers that it is “the friend who stayed behind,” and says no more.
-   Curse: the mines fall silent and the cavern’s old dripping stops (see *The Curse Worsens*).
-   Amulet: when Elior hears the Dragon’s Memory, the Ancient Amulet glows a third time, and Aren stands beside Lysa, hand in hand, while the third figure still has his back turned. The Dragon names the amulet as the anchor of the ward. The amulet shows the faces; it does not say whether the parents live.
-   Offering: After hearing the Dragon’s Memory and examining the Old Oath Scrolls found in Echo Chambers, the scrolls hold an old oath with one blank (“I take what is given, and I return ___.”) and the Dragon asks what Elior will give back. He answers with `say everything to ancient dragon` (or it / myself / what I take); the dragon yields the Scale when the blank is filled with a giving word.

Result: The Scale is cold and heavy—a pledge as much as a relic. The caverns brighten, not with light, but with trust.

## Malakar’s Shadow and Parentage Hints

-   Letters and sigils resurface. The Sea-Sealed Letter is written by **Aren and Lysa** to their friend Malakar, signed as fellow Wardens of the Dawn, and it begs him not to reach for time magic. They defied him as equals and as friends.
-   In the Sunken Ruins, a mural shows two figures shielding a child from a storm of sigils while a third reaches toward them.
-   On Mount Ember’s Mirror Terraces, the Charred Inscription records the three Wardens’ shared promise, the three virtues they bound into the relics, and the words “what we pour into the ward, the ward will keep.”
-   The Dragon names the ward: Aren and Lysa became it, and the Dragon says they are warded away, not dead.

## The Curse Worsens

At each relic site the curse is visibly worse than the last, and the beat plays once, when Elior reads or hears that site’s Echoes clue (the letter, the inscription, the Dragon’s Memory). The beat is one paragraph straight after the amulet’s glow; afterwards the Tidal Causeway, Mirror Terraces and Dragon’s Hall each end their description with one closing line about it, and Mira’s parting speech at each of the three departures ends with one sentence that the king is weaker.

-   Sunken Ruins: dead sea birds on the causeways, the surf running grey.
-   Mount Ember: ash rain falling over the slopes and the braziers guttering.
-   Caverns of Shadow: silent mines, the miners’ picks hanging unmoved.

When Mira next teleports the party she says the king is weaker. At the Mount Ember departure, with the ash falling, she also draws the line between the two sicknesses, before the king line: "There is a word the old folk will reach for when this ash comes down on them: the Wasting. It is not that. The Wasting killed. This drinks." Together with the clues, this builds to the Act III row of the red line: Elior’s parents were not Malakar’s victims; they were his closest friends.

## Quests in Act III

### Main Quest

-   Name: The Three Virtues
    -   Type: Main Quest
    -   Trigger: Speak with Mira at the start of Act III to learn of Malakar’s ritual and the need for the relics
    -   Prerequisites: Completion of Act II
    -   Dependencies: Every Act III non-main quest, including “Echoes of the Hidden Bond,” must be completed before this quest can be finished.
    -   Quest Chain Phases:
        -   Phase 1: Crystal of Light (Courage) — Requires completion of both Sunken Ruins side quests
        -   Phase 2: Phoenix Feather (Wisdom) — Requires completion of both Mount Ember side quests
        -   Phase 3: Dragon’s Scale (Selflessness) — Requires completion of both Caverns of Shadow side quests
        -   Mira’s Confession — After every Act III non-main quest is complete and before the rite, Mira tells Elior the truth about herself (see *Mira’s Confession*)
        -   Final: Warding Rite — After the confession, Mira combines and consumes the three relics, and presses the Healing Herbs from her shelf into Elior’s hands (they leave the hut), to create the Lifelight Elixir. This completes “The Three Virtues” and prepares the party for the Act IV assault.
    -   Story Impact: Establishes the relics as the counter to Malakar’s ritual; examining the fortress gates completes Act III and begins Act IV.

#### Mira’s Slips

Mira lets slip, once per relic site, that she knew the Wardens; each is recognised only in hindsight. Sunken Ruins: reading the Sea-Sealed Letter with Mira in the room (or, if read elsewhere, her next talk) makes her say “Lysa’s hand. She always crossed her sevens.” and turn to the sea. Mount Ember: on the first talk at the Lower Switchbacks with the Phoenix Feather, she finishes the Phoenix’s line (“…even when the world burns.”). Caverns of Shadow: at the end of the first talk, the Ancient Dragon sends Mira a message (“Little Mira. Tell her she has grown old.”); at the Cavern Mouth she receives it without answering. The confession then opens with “You noticed. I saw you notice.”

#### Mira’s Confession

Before the Warding Rite, Mira stops. She was the Wardens’ pupil: she learned from Aren, Lysa and Malakar, and when Aren and Lysa became the ward she settled in Willowbrook as its healer to watch over Elior. Grandmother knew, and kept the secret with her. Elior confronts her: “Why didn’t you tell me?” Mira answers that she could not risk the seal, that Malakar’s cloaked agent was searching Willowbrook for the amulet and any word could have reached him, that Lyria wrote to her from Greendale about a girl with a mask on her wrist and she burned the letter rather than show Greendale what the healer at Willowbrook could do, with Malakar's agent in both towns and Elior in one of them (she has never been so ashamed of anything), and that she is telling him now because Elior has earned the truth. The amulet, she adds, is the anchor of the ward his parents became. The confession itself is fixed, but before the rite Elior may answer it once: `say i forgive you to mira` or `say you should have told me to mira` (forgiveness or reproach, recorded as a flag and remembered by Mira; neither is a virtue moment). Mira performs the rite on her next conversation either way.

### Storytelling Quest (Spans All Three Locations)

-   Name: Echoes of the Hidden Bond
    -   Type: Storytelling Quest
    -   Trigger: Read the Keepsake Note Mira gives Elior at the start of Act III
    -   Structure: Three linked discoveries, one at each relic site
    -   Objectives:
        1. Sunken Ruins — Examine the mural to reveal the Sea‑Sealed Letter, then read the letter before leaving the site
        2. Mount Ember — Read the Charred Inscription on the Mirror Terraces before leaving the site
        3. Caverns of Shadow — Hear the Dragon’s Memory, a spoken recollection naming Elior’s parents, Aren and Lysa, and the ward they became
    -   The clues, in order: the **Sea-Sealed Letter** (Aren and Lysa, fellow Wardens, begging their friend Malakar not to reach for time magic), the **Charred Inscription** (the three Wardens’ promise and the three virtues) and the **Dragon’s Memory** (the parents became the ward; they are “warded away, not dead”). The Ancient Amulet glows at each one.
    -   Completion: Find all three testimonies to complete the quest and explain how Elior was hidden from Malakar
    -   Story Impact: Turns the red line: Elior’s parents were Malakar’s closest friends, not his victims. This storytelling quest must be completed before Mira can confess and perform the Warding Rite

## Spells Required in Act III

Act III requires the use of five spells. Act III setup grants any of these that are missing, even if the player did not learn them in their earlier acts; the listed sources describe their original introductions.

-   Spell: `unlock`

    -   Learned In: Act I (from Mira)
    -   Used In: Sunken Ruins — Collapsed Pier vault
    -   Purpose: Open the corroded locker to retrieve the three Prism Lanterns after the physical key fails

-   Spell: `light`

    -   Learned In: Act I (from Mira)
    -   Used In: Sunken Ruins — Tidal Causeway and Submerged Antechamber
    -   Purpose: Reveal the Rusted Locker Key in the Causeway and ignite the mounted Prism Lanterns to reveal the path to the sanctum

-   Spell: `purify`

    -   Learned In: Act I (from Fisherman at the Riverbank)
    -   Used In: Sunken Ruins — Outer Wards
    -   Purpose: Cleanse the brine-crusted warding pillars so the Tideward Sigil will take and stabilize the flood wards

-   Spell: `mend`

    -   Learned In: Act II (via craftsmen study/mentorship)
    -   Used In: Mount Ember — Mirror Terraces
    -   Purpose: Repair the prepared Mirror Mount after the segments are installed and bound with resin; the beam then fires at the next rest on the terraces (noon), opening the path to Fumarole Passages

-   Spell: `bless`
    -   Learned In: Act I (from the Priest)
    -   Used In: Caverns of Shadow — Stillness Vestibule
    -   Purpose: Sanctify the echo stones before performing the Resonant Chant so the illusions fully quiet (the Stillwater Phial must also have stilled the pools)

-   Quest Relevance: Examine the Runic Walls to learn the chant, then read the Old Oath Scrolls (they stay re-readable) before answering the Dragon's question.
-   Exits:
    -   West to Collapsed Galleries

5. Stillness Vestibule

-   Description: A hush falls over dark water pools; sound seems to fold into the stone.
-   Items: Echo Stones, Dark Pool, Stillwater Phial, and Quiet Charm.
-   Characters: Silence Keeper and Wandering Phantoms (under their whispers some hum the shell's tune; one clear word under every whisper).
-   Quest Relevance: Finalize the Oath of Stillness to silence illusions (completes “Oath of Stillness”).
-   Exits:
    -   North to Collapsed Galleries
    -   East to Dragon’s Hall only after “Oath of Stillness” is completed
    -   West to Cavern Mouth

6. Dragon’s Hall

-   Description: A vast chamber lit by slow‑breathing embers beneath scaled coils; age‑old sigils line the floor.
-   Items: Dragon’s Scale appears after the Dragon accepts Elior’s oath.
-   Characters: The Ancient Dragon
-   Quest Relevance: Main Quest phase completion for the Dragon’s Scale; the Dragon also delivers the Dragon’s Memory for the storytelling quest.
-   Exits:
    -   West to Stillness Vestibule

### Ending Room

#### Entrance to Malakar’s Fortress

-   Description: A blackstone bastion rises from a shattered ridge; gate sigils pulse like a heartbeat behind iron lattices. The air tastes of cold metal and distant thunder. Where the gate arch meets the wall an older carving shows through the black: the Warden sigil, a sun rising over three linked rings, because this was the Wardens’ hall. The Ancient Amulet is warm in Elior’s hand: the act ends on the Act III row of the red line, with Elior knowing his parents were Malakar’s closest friends; the Dragon says they are warded away, not dead, and Elior does not yet know what that means.
-   Items: Fortress Gates (examining the gates marks Act III complete)
-   Characters: None
-   Quest Relevance: Arrival point after securing all three relics; transition into Act IV’s assault on the fortress.
-   Exits:
    -   None (teleportation arrival point only)

## Optional choices

Optional virtue moments; none blocks the golden path, none adds a puzzle. Each records one count in the same three counters as Act IV (mercy, truth, selfless) and changes a few lines of dialogue.

| Where                         | The choice                                                                           | Records      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------ |
| Collapsed Pier, Pier Warden   | Tell the Pier Warden the true state of the ruins instead of the comforting lie       | **truth**    |
| Stillness Vestibule, Phantoms | Free the wandering phantoms without banishing them (`say rest to wandering phantoms`) before the chant; the chant then still completes the quest | **mercy**    |
| Dragon’s Hall, the Dragon     | After taking the scale, mend the prison it weakened (`cast bless on sigils`), gaining nothing | **selfless** |
| Mirror Terraces, Charred Inscription | After reading it, restore Malakar’s deeper-struck name (`cast mend on inscription`). The inscription’s closing line, the Phoenix’s farewell and the feather reply change once it is done | **mercy**    |

The endings read the counters across the whole game, as described in `Act4Design.md`.

## Golden Path (Detailed, Step-by-Step)

This sequence completes all Act III requirements in a linear path. Each step lists the room, the exact commands to enter, and their direct consequences.

### Act Start — Mira’s Hut and Teleportation

1. Room: Mira’s Hut

-   Commands and yields:
    -   Elior already carries his family heirloom, the Ancient Amulet, from the first turn of Act III (no step needed).
    -   `talk to Mira` → Starts “The Three Virtues”; Mira explains the relics and gives Elior the Keepsake Note.
    -   `examine keepsake note` → Reads the note and starts “Echoes of the Hidden Bond.” The note is in Mira’s hand and says only that Elior must read what the relics’ makers left behind.

2. Room: Mira’s Hut

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `talk to Mira` → Teleports the party to the Sunken Ruins (arrive at Tidal Causeway).

---

### Sunken Ruins — Courage (Crystal of Light)

3. Room: Tidal Causeway

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `examine mural` → Reveals the Sea-Sealed Letter.
    -   `take letter` → Adds it to inventory.
    -   `examine letter` → Reads the clue (written by Aren and Lysa, fellow Wardens, to their friend Malakar) and records progress for “Echoes of the Hidden Bond”; the Ancient Amulet glows and shows a fragment of two faces; Mira requires the letter before teleporting the party onward. The dead sea birds on the causeway mark the first curse escalation.
    -   `cast light` → Reveals the Rusted Locker Key in a hollow.
    -   `take key` → Adds the key to inventory.
    -   `north` → Moves to Shoreline Markers.

4. Room: Shoreline Markers

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `search` → Reveals Moon Rune shards among the steles.
    -   `take moon rune shards` → Adds Moon Rune shards to inventory (progress for “Tideward Sigils”).

5. Room: Outer Wards

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Moves from Shoreline Markers to Outer Wards.
    -   `search` → Describes three leaning warding pillars and drowned courtyard.
    -   `cast purify on warding pillars` → Cleanses brine/coral, allowing sigils to hold.
    -   `use moon rune shards with warding pillars` → Engraves the Moon Sigil across the pillars; stabilizes the ward; completes “Tideward Sigils”; weakens sanctum flood wards.

6. Room: Collapsed Pier

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Moves from Outer Wards to the Collapsed Pier, where the locker and Pier Warden are present.

7. Room: Collapsed Pier (Vault)

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `use key with locker` → The key jams in the fused lock.
    -   `cast unlock on locker` → Releases the fused mechanism.
    -   `open locker` → Opens the locker, revealing prism lanterns.
    -   `take lantern` → Takes a Prism Lantern.

8. Room: Submerged Antechamber

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `west` → Returns to Outer Wards; `south` enters the Submerged Antechamber.
    -   `search` → Reveals lantern brackets and carved niches along the approach.
    -   `use lantern with bracket` → Mounts a lantern (repeat for each of the three brackets).
    -   `cast light` → Ignites mounted prism lanterns; reveals submerged path; completes “Lanterns of the Deeps”; unlocks sanctum approach.

9. Room: Sanctum of the Tide

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Enters the Sanctum after both Tideward Sigils and Lanterns of the Deeps are complete.
    -   Arriving → the lights go out; the guardian says “Walk.” Casting light is refused (“Here you go without it.”).
    -   `east` (three times) → Wades into the black water: cold to the knees; a voice like Mira’s telling Elior to turn back; water at the chest with Cedric out of sight. On the third step the water parts, the Crystal of Light appears and the guardian asks, “You came through. What did you not abandon?”
    -   `west` from a step → Wades back to the edge, no penalty. `west` from the edge leaves the sanctum. Leaving and returning before the vow puts Elior at the edge again.
    -   `talk to tide-born guardian` → Before the vow: walk east, and the guardian will ask one thing. After the vow: the guardian tells of Aren, who asked to have later visitors tested as he was.
    -   `say myself to tide-born guardian` → Before the third step: “Walk first.” At the third step: vow accepted; the guardian explains what it meant, the sigils kindle again, the sanctum is lit for good, and `take crystal of light` works. Taking the crystal earlier is refused by the wards. The relic can be taken only after completing “Tideward Sigils” and “Lanterns of the Deeps”.
    -   Progress through the walk is saved: a save made mid-walk loads on the same step.

10. Room: Tidal Causeway

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `west` → Returns from the Sanctum to the Submerged Antechamber; `west` again returns to the Tidal Causeway.
    -   `talk to Mira` → Teleports the party to Mount Ember (arrive at Lower Switchbacks).

---

### Mount Ember — Wisdom (Phoenix Feather)

11. Room: Lower Switchbacks

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `talk to ash scholar` → Points you toward Mirror Terraces and Obsidian Outcrops; gives a Brass Mirror Segment and a Charred Notebook (optional reading).
    -   `examine Emberwater Canteen` → Reveals another Brass Mirror Segment.
    -   `take brass mirror segment` → Adds the segment to inventory.

12. Room: Obsidian Outcrops

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `north` → Enters Obsidian Outcrops from Lower Switchbacks.
    -   `search` → Reveals a Brass Mirror Segment and Binding Resin.
    -   `take brass mirror segment` → Adds segments to inventory (repeat as needed).
    -   `take binding resin` → Adds Binding Resin to inventory.

13. Room: Mirror Terraces

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Enters Mirror Terraces from Obsidian Outcrops.
    -   `search` → Shows sockets, mounts, and the etched light channels.
    -   `examine inscription` → Reads the Charred Inscription (the three Wardens’ promise) and records the second “Echoes of the Hidden Bond” clue; the amulet glows and the woman’s face is Lysa’s; ash rain falls; Mira requires the inscription before the party can leave Mount Ember.
    -   `examine mirror mount` → Provides lore on the mirror system and its purpose.
    -   `take brass mirror segment` → Takes the segment revealed by examining the mount.
    -   `use brass mirror segment with mirror mount` → Installs all segments in inventory; once all placed, the beam path forms.
    -   `use binding resin with mirror mount` → Secures the mirror segments.
    -   `cast mend on mirror mount` → Repairs cracks and mends the mirrors, but the sun is not yet in place
    -   `rest` → Brings the sun to noon; the beam fires, completing “Mirrors of Emberlight” and opening the east exit.

14. Room: Ember Gallery

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `south` → Descends from Mirror Terraces to Ember Gallery.
    -   `search` → Identifies ash‑fern and cooled slag deposits.
    -   `take ash-fern` → Adds Ash‑Fern to inventory.
    -   `take cooled slag` → Adds Cooled Slag to inventory.
    -   `use cooled slag with ash-fern` → Crafts a Heat-Ward Mix.

15. Room: Fumarole Passages

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `north` → Returns to Mirror Terraces; `east` enters Fumarole Passages.
    -   `use vent stone` (three times) → Synchronizes the vents.
    -   `use heat-ward mix` → Applies the heat ward: the vents start a steady breath (out, pause, in) and the south exit is listed.
    -   `rest` twice → The breath goes out → pause → in (it always starts on the out-breath on entry; `wait` does the same as `rest`).

16. Room: Phoenix Crater (Reveal the Phoenix)

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `south` → On the vents’ in-breath: crosses into the crater and completes “Breath of the Mountain.”
    -   `rest` → Reveals the Phoenix and changes the room description.

17. Room: Phoenix Crater (Relic Trial)

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `talk to phoenix` → Engages in a telepathic conversation, posing a riddle.
    -   `say patience to phoenix` → Answers “What is wisdom when time is short?”, links patience to a friend who had none, and gives the Phoenix Feather directly to Elior.

18. Room: Lower Switchbacks

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `north`, `west`, `west`, `south` → Returns from the crater to Lower Switchbacks.
    -   `talk to Mira` → Teleports the party to the Caverns of Shadow (arrive at Cavern Mouth).

### Caverns of Shadow — Selflessness (Dragon’s Scale)

19. Room: Cavern Mouth

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `talk to mine overseer` → Introduces “Miners’ Rescue,” directs Elior to the Tool Cache, and gives him the Miner's Key.

20. Room: Tool Cache

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `north` → Enters the Tool Cache; `search` reveals the locked Supply Crate.
    -   `use key with supply crate` → Unlocks the crate.
    -   `open crate` → Opens supply crates holding structural materials.
    -   `take reinforced braces` → Adds Reinforced Braces to inventory.
    -   `take support straps` → Adds Support Straps to inventory.
    -   `take wedge blocks` → Adds Wedge Blocks to inventory.

21. Room: Collapsed Galleries

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Enters the galleries from Tool Cache.
    -   `use reinforced braces with fallen rock` → Stabilizes the collapse.
    -   `use support straps with fallen rock` → Secures the supports.
    -   `use wedge blocks with fallen rock` → Frees the blocked passage.
    -   `talk to miners` → Completes “Miners’ Rescue” (no escort sequence); opens a stable inner route to the Echo Chambers.

22. Room: Echo Chambers

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Opens from Collapsed Galleries after “Miners’ Rescue” is complete; `search` reveals the Old Oath Scrolls.
    -   `examine old oath scrolls` → Records that Elior has read the old oath form; the scrolls stay and can be read again.
    -   `examine runic walls` → Reveals the Resonant Chant instructions.
    -   `take resonant chant rubbings` → Adds the rubbings to inventory.

23. Room: Stillness Vestibule

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `west` → Returns to Collapsed Galleries; `south` enters Stillness Vestibule.
    -   `cast bless on echo stones` → Sanctifies the stones for the rite.
    -   `take phial`, `use stillwater phial with pool` → Stills the pools (the phial is kept; examining it always shows the young woman and her three teachers). The chant breaks apart on ripples until this is done; bless and still may come in either order.
    -   `use resonant chant rubbings with echo stones` → Performs the chant; silences illusions and completes “Oath of Stillness,” opening the east exit to Dragon’s Hall.

24. Room: Dragon’s Hall (Story Clue)

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `east` → Enters Dragon’s Hall after “Oath of Stillness” is complete.
    -   `talk to dragon` → Grants the Dragon’s Memory (Storytelling Quest 3/3; completes the Storytelling Quest when combined with earlier clues): Aren and Lysa, Wardens of the Dawn, became the ward and, the Dragon says, are warded away, not dead. The Dragon echoes “Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.” The Ancient Amulet glows a third time and shows Aren and Lysa together, and the Dragon names it the anchor of the ward. The mines fall silent. The dream voice speaks once; the Dragon says it is “the friend who stayed behind.”

25. Room: Dragon’s Hall (Relic Trial)

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `say everything to ancient dragon` → The blank is filled; Elior speaks the old line back, the dragon accepts and the scale is added to the room's items. Taking answers (gold, nothing, thanks…) get “Then you would be taking.”; other answers get the question again.
    -   `take dragon's scale` → Add the scale to the inventory; completes Caverns phase.

### Return and Fortress Threshold

26. Room: Cavern Mouth

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `west`, `west` → Returns from Dragon’s Hall through Stillness Vestibule to Cavern Mouth.
    -   `talk to Mira` → Teleports the party back to Mira’s Hut.

27. Room: Mira’s Hut

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `talk to Mira` → After every Act III non-main quest is complete, Mira makes her confession: she was the Wardens’ pupil, stayed in Willowbrook to watch over Elior, and Grandmother knew. Elior asks, “Why didn’t you tell me?”
    -   _Optional:_ `say i forgive you to mira` or `say you should have told me to mira` → Elior answers the confession; Mira replies in kind and the rite follows either way.
    -   `talk to Mira` → Mira performs the Warding Rite, consumes the relics, presses the Healing Herbs into Elior’s hands (they leave the hut), creates the Lifelight Elixir, and completes “The Three Virtues.”
    -   `take Lifelight Elixir` → Adds the elixir to inventory.
    -   `talk to Mira` → Teleports the party to the Entrance to Malakar’s Fortress.

28. Room: Entrance to Malakar’s Fortress

-   Commands and yields:
    -   `examine gates` → Marks Act III completion and the beginning of Act IV, ending on the Act III row of the red line.
