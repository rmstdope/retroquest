General Store — one picture, shutters raised when open (agreed)

One picture, Hemlock painted at his counter. Only the shutters change when the store opens. Below: the room text a player reads in each state.

1. Shut, first visit (rope on the shelf)

A cluttered shop with the shutters half down. Rope, apples and trinkets crowd the shelves in the gloom. Hemlock stands at his counter, sorting apples, and keeps glancing at the lane to the chapel.

2. Shut, rope already bought

A cluttered shop with the shutters half down. Apples and trinkets crowd the shelves in the gloom. Hemlock stands at his counter, sorting apples, and keeps glancing at the lane to the chapel.

3. Open, Patches stayed with Grandmother (no cat)

A cluttered shop with the shutters up. Rope, apples and trinkets crowd the shelves, and the air smells of fruit, wax and old wood. Outside, the lane runs east to the chapel. Hemlock sorts apples at his counter between customers.

4. Open, Patches home on the counter

A cluttered shop with the shutters up. Apples and trinkets crowd the shelves, and the air smells of fruit, wax and old wood. Outside, the lane runs east to the chapel. Hemlock sorts apples at his counter between customers, and every so often looks down by the counter, where his cat sleeps.

The shutters change (agreed)

Words the painter reads for the change: “Raise the window shutters all the way.” The rest of the room keeps its painted light.
