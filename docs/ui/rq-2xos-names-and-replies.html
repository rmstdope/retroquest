Option A — “a”, “an” and “the” all reach the thing

Every command that takes a name drops a leading “a”, “an” or “the” before it looks. Highlighted lines are what changes from today.

In the village

You take the apple.

› talk to a merchant

Merchant: "Fresh goods from the coast! Have a look."

› buy a candle from shopkeeper

You buy the candle for 2 coins.

› take the apple

You take the apple.

There is no 'a' here to take.

› examine the
