Act 1 villagers, as old neighbours — agreed

Four Act 1 lines no longer treat Elior as a stranger to the village. Highlighted text is new; everything else plays as today.

Grandmother — first talk

Option A, with her hut named.

Desktop
› talk to grandmother

"There you are. Sit down a minute, the bread's nearly done."

She does not sit down herself.

"The village is on edge, you'll have noticed. Folk keep their voices down in the square these days, and nobody will say why."

"If you're going out, look in on Mira at her hut; she was asking after you. You know she has a way with herbs, and with... other things. Hemlock will have heard whatever there is to hear at the General Store; he always does. And show your face at the forge, or Borin will think you've forgotten him."

"You've a good heart, Elior. Don't let anyone talk you out of it."

Hemlock — first meeting

Option B: you know the cat.

"Oh! Forgive me, lad, I was watching for her. Patches. Gone for days now, and you've seen her on this counter often enough to know she never goes far. Can't think about business with her out there in the cold, so the store stays shut until she's home."

"She was last seen near the old well. If you find her, there's a reward in it for you."
