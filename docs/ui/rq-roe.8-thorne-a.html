How does a player learn the merchant is hungry?

The kindness is giving Grandmother's fresh bread and taking nothing back. Today nothing says he is hungry, so the gift comes from nowhere.

M1 — he says so when you talk (recommended)
› talk to merchant

◆You speak with the merchant.

The merchant offers a friendly nod.

merchant

Looking for something specific, or just browsing? …

merchant

Forgive me if I'm short, friend. I've not eaten since the hill road, and there's no inn this side of Greendale.

› give fresh bread to merchant

◆You give the fresh bread to the merchant.

The merchant unwraps the cloth and simply holds the loaf for a moment.

merchant

Still warm. ... What do I owe you? No? Nothing at all? Then you've a better heart than most I meet on this road, friend. I'll not forget it.

Dialogue is where a text adventure puts a need. Added at the end of every talk until he has been given food.
M2 — you see it when you look at him
› look at merchant

A traveling merchant with a cart full of wares and stories from distant lands.

His cheeks are hollow, and you notice he keeps glancing at the empty crust in his pack.

Subtler: only a player who looks closely finds it. Many will never see the moment exists.

Option A — Brenn is spoken of from the first talk

Brenn, the caravan guard, ran when the ravine path gave way and came back alone — which is how Thorne knows the caravan is lost. He is talked about, never seen. Here Thorne names him from the start, and after the rescue says he'll turn him out until Elior speaks for him.

A — from the first talk (recommended)
› talk to caravan master thorne (first time)
caravan master thorne

Thank the gods, someone who looks capable! … one of my most valuable caravans has gone missing in the Enchanted Forest. …

caravan master thorne

Brenn, my guard, came stumbling back alone two nights ago. Says the ravine path gave way and he ran. Ran! I'll deal with him once my people are home.

› talk to caravan master thorne (after the rescue, documents given)
caravan master thorne

I'm still processing the relief of having my caravan safely returned. …

caravan master thorne

Which leaves Brenn. A guard who runs is no guard. He's sitting outside the warehouse waiting for his wages, and wages are all he'll get from me.

› say forgive to thorne

You speak for Brenn: the path gave way under him too, and fear is not the same as cowardice. He came back to tell you, when he could have kept running.

caravan master thorne

… He did come back, didn't he. … All right. One more road. If he runs again, he walks home.
