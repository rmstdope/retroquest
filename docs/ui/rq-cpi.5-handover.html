Mira hands over the amulet — where the hint falls

Step 27, the last visit to Mira's hut. Everything outside the dashed box is what a player reads today. The box is the new hint. Nothing is confessed.

A — right after the gift (recommended)
› talk to mira

◆You speak with Mira.

Mira smiles, a deep wisdom in her eyes.

Mira

You have done well, Elior. You have gathered your supplies, honed your magical skills, and prepared your spirit. You are ready.

She presents you with the Ancient Amulet.

Mira

May this guide and protect you.

◆(Ancient Amulet added to inventory!)

Then, more quietly:

Mira

I knew your parents better than I have said. Once you are truly ready, I will tell you.

She holds your hand a moment longer, her tone serious.

Mira

Your road leads to Greendale, Elior. Once there, show the amulet to no one lightly. Those who know what it is will find you.

Mira

Trust in yourself, and remember: the fate of our village, or even more, may rest in your hands.

What the Act I design says: it comes with the amulet, "then, more quietly", before her practical warning about Greendale. The gift and the hint read together; the scene still ends on her usual send-off.
