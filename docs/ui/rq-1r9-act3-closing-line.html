Agreed — Option A: the Dragon's words, and not knowing

Act III’s last scene — examining the fortress gates — and the line that opens the act’s complete page. Today they read “They are not dead. They are warded away.” and “They are warded away, not dead.” Dashed outline = the words that change. Keeps the bead’s idea; the sentence before it already says the ward is still holding, so the third sentence of the suggested line is left out rather than said twice.
