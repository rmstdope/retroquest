First talk to Nyx

Desktop
> talk to nyx

Nyx regards you with ancient eyes that hold the wisdom of millennia.

“Brave Elior, your journey through the forest and your respect for all living things has proven your worth. I have watched as you helped the healers, protected the innocent, and showed wisdom in your choices. For this, I grant you my greatest gifts.”

She raises her hands, and magical energy flows into you like gentle starlight. You feel new knowledge settling into your mind - the gift of prophetic vision, allowing you to glimpse the threads of fate. Nyx then places two precious items in your hands.

You have learned the spell: Prophetic Vision
You have received: Nyx's Token
You have received: Forest Heart Crystal

“Use these gifts wisely, young one. The prophetic vision will help you prepare for the gathering storm that approaches. May the forest's blessing guide you on your path.”

Then her voice softens, as if she is repeating something she was told long ago. “Remember the old words, Elior: Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.”

The words from the stone in the glade. You read them aloud there and did not know what they meant. You still do not, quite.

First cast with Sir Cedric present (every side quest done)

Desktop
> cast prophetic vision

You close your eyes and reach out with your newly granted sight beyond sight. With Sir Cedric standing beside you, you share the vision as it rises before you:

A village at dusk, its roofs low and familiar. Beyond it, a field withered to grey stalks, the way yours was the morning all this began. And at the field's edge, a robed figure, its face lost in shadow, reaching one hand toward you as if across a great distance.

Then the voice from your dream, the one that called your name on the night of the storm: “Elior… how you have grown. Do not be afraid of me.” It is closer than it was. You do not know whose it is, only that you have heard it before, long before the dream.

The vision fades. Sir Cedric's expression has grown grave.
