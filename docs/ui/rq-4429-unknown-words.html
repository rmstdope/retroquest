Option A — a gesture, then a line

Each character answers a word they don't know the way they already talk: a small gesture, then one short line in their own voice. The word is never repeated back. The deer does not speak, so its answer is plain narration, not dialogue. Highlighted text is new. Today every one of these says: You say 'apples' to Tilda, but they don't seem to understand or respond to that particular phrase.

The deer (no speech)
› say apples to deer

The deer watches you, ears forward, and does not answer, not in words.

› say pears to deer

The deer lowers its head to the grass, then lifts it again to look at you.
