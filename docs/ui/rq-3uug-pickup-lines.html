All the lines

Every pick-up in Act I

Every item Elior can pick up in Act I, each with its own line in the style you chose: what he feels in his hand, then one thought. Each line is written to stay true wherever the item is lying, so it also works if he drops it and picks it up again somewhere else.

The words are drafts for you to change. Anything you strike or rewrite ships exactly as you leave it.

Taken together

There is no way to take everything in a room at once. Two things are taken together only when they share a name (two coins after a drop): each different item then says its own line once, one after the other. The old shared line, "This might come in handy, so you decide to pick it up.", is no longer heard anywhere in Act I.
