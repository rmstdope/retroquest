The Water Nymphs' riddles, as agreed

Water, then mask, then truth. From the second wrong answer to a riddle, the nymphs add a hint in their own voice and repeat it on every later miss. Misses are remembered for the current riddle across leaving the glade and reloading a save, and start over with each new riddle. Synonyms in the dashed boxes are accepted. Today's grey line about 'what you've learned about the forest' is gone.
