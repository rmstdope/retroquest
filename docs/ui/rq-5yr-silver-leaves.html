Giving the leaves to Lyria

FIRST AND ONLY TIME
Any time after picking them up — before or after her own errand is done. The leaves leave the pack.

> give silver leaves to master healer lyria

You offer the silver leaves to Master Healer Lyria.

Lyria turns one leaf toward the window, and for a moment she forgets to breathe. “Silverbark. From the old tree itself, not a cutting. I have only ever read of these.” She lays them in a shallow dish as if they might bruise. “Steeped, a single leaf breaks a fever in a night. Greendale will have a gentler winter because of you, Elior.”

LOOKING AT THE LEAVES BEFORE GIVING THEM
The leaves' description gains a hint, so a player knows who to show them to.

> examine silver leaves

Delicate leaves that shimmer with a soft argent sheen even in shadow. Their surfaces refract light into faint auroral threads, and a cool, clean scent clings to them. Holding them you sense tranquil, ancient vitality. A healer would know what to do with these.
