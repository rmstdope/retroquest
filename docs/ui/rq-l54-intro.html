ACT IV: THE FINAL CONFRONTATION

With the Lifelight Elixir in hand, you stand before Malakar's fortress. The obsidian gates loom ahead, wreathed in shadow and ancient magic. Mira steps through the gates beside you, pale but unbending. She and Sir Cedric take up their places just inside the threshold — someone must hold the way out. A small light settles on the gatepost: Nyx, keeping close.

Mira: "Someone has to keep the way out open, and that's me. So come back through it."

Sir Cedric: "I held a gate once and lost a king behind it. I will not lose this one, or you."

Nyx: "I'll be the light at the edge of your eye. Don't look for me. Just know."

Mira presses something into your hand: a small silver medallion, two hands clasped on its face. Sir Cedric lays his hand over hers; the light on the gatepost leans in.

Mira: "If it goes quiet in there, say our names. We'll hear."

From here, you go on alone — or nearly.

This is the culmination of your journey—a test of courage, wisdom, and selflessness that will determine the fate of the kingdom.
