The room's picture is the stage — agreed

Laptop. The room's picture fills the play screen, trimmed at the edges to fit the window. Top-left: a plate with "You are in", the room name and who and what is here as chips (they wrap; the plate grows). Top-right: Bag, Spells, Quests, ⟲ Story so far and 🧭 Map. Map opens on pointing (or pressing) into today's map of the ways out; press a room to walk. Bag, Spells and Quests open a drawer from the right with today's three tabs. Until the narration paper arrives, the story is read in a paper band along the bottom, full width; one row of actions under it. Walking fades the next picture in.

Out of this piece: the narration paper, speech, the arrival description, the story-so-far drawer (until then ⟲ Story so far scrolls the band back to the start), the phone, and a map of every place visited (to be filed separately).
