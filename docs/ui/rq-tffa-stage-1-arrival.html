1 · The description on arrival

When you walk into a room, its description is narrated over the middle of the picture, without paper. It fades once it has been read (when the narrator finishes, or after a reading time when narration is off), or at once when you click. Then the picture is clear; look shows the description again, in the paper at the bottom.
