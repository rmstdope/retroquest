Option B — recommended

Looking never stops the voice; acting does

Switching to Map, Bag, Quests or Spells on a phone, and opening a name’s menu from the story, leave the voice reading — the panels you look at stay as the voice has reached them and catch up as it goes. Picking an action from a name’s menu is a complete command, so it cuts in like any command. A press on an exit or chip in a held panel still only shows the rest of the turn (it belongs to a place the voice hasn’t left yet), as today.

Recommended: it is the same rule as typing — only a command interrupts. Cost: on a phone, the Map you open mid-reading can still show the old place for a moment, then switch while you look at it.
