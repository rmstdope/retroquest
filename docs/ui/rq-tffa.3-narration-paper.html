Narration is a paper along the foot of the stage — agreed

Laptop. A turn's narration is read on a paper along the bottom of the picture, above the actions. It holds this turn only: what you did ("› take bread"), the answer, item chips and quest cards. It grows as the narrator reaches each part (a short glide), up to half the screen, then scrolls with "More below ↓". "▾ Hide" in its top-right corner (or N) slides it away and leaves a "▴ Narration" tab at the right; the voice keeps reading. The next turn with something to tell opens it again; a turn with nothing to tell leaves no paper and no tab.

Until the story-so-far drawer arrives, ⟲ Story so far fills the paper with the whole story ("✕ Back to this turn"). Until the speech piece arrives, what people say stays in the paper as today. Phones and short windows keep today's layout until the phone piece.

The journal is your father's. Most pages are lists: seeds, weather, the price of nails in Greendale. Then, near the back, the hand changes. The letters lean forward as if he wrote in a hurry, and the ink is darker. There is a way to call a thing back, he writes, if you know its true name and are not afraid of the answer. Below it, three marks you know from the amulet, drawn twice, the second time pressed so hard the nib tore the page. The rest of the page is water-stained and you cannot read it.
You turn the page. Pressed flat between the leaves is a sprig of something grey-green that still smells faintly of summer, and beside it, in the same hurried hand: Mira will know. Do not tell her I sent you. Grandmother has stopped kneading. She is watching you read.
