Each says it once you have a reason to hear it

Marcus says it in his plea, once you have met Elena and heard "weeks ago" from her. The Historians say it after you have read the visitors' register and seen the last entry. Thorne says it in his first plea, since the road is his own trouble. Each line lands next to the clue it answers, so the player makes the link themselves.
