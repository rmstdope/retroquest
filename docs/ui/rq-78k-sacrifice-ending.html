The Sacrifice ending gets Malakar's full reaction

The agreed version. Malakar answers the word with a whole line about the parents, and at the amulet asks Elior to keep something. Grow is called the smallest spell, never the first (Act I teaches revive first). Saying the word again plays only the existing dim line. New text is highlighted.

Wide window
› say sacrifice to malakar

“Take my magic,” you say. “All of it. Close the wound with that.”

Malakar: "No. Your parents poured themselves into that wound once already. It does not get to have you too."

The open amulet grows heavy in your hand, ready.

› say sacrifice to malakar

You have said it. The amulet is waiting.

› use amulet on wound

Malakar half rises, reaching for the amulet, and stops his own hand.

Malakar: "Then keep something. One thing. Anything. Do not let me take it all."

You press the open amulet to the wound and let go. The light, the blessing, the truth sight, every spell you carried runs out of your hands into the tear, and the tear drinks it and closes. When it is done, one small spell is still in you, the smallest of them all: grow.

Your magic is gone. Only grow remains.

King Alden: "He will answer to me, and he will live to do it. Thank you, farmer."
