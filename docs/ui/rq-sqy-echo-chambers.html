The Echo Chambers replay the night the three friends parted (agreed)

listen in the Echo Chambers, or examine the runic walls a second time, plays the echo once. Afterwards the Silence Keeper, on a later talk, says what the phantoms are, once.

1 — first time: listen (or walls a second time)
› listen

◆You hold still. The whispers in the alcoves gather, and for a moment they are not copying you at all. Three voices, close and tired, as if the night they belong to never ended.

A man's voice, steady

'Mal. Put the lamp down and listen to me.'

A second man, quick and low

'I have listened for a year, Aren. The Wasting is in Willowbrook. It is in her.'

A woman's voice, hoarse

'Then let it stay in me. Not this. Time is not a door, Mal.'

The second man

'It is only a door if I close it again.'

The steady voice

'It is a wound. You know it is. Stay. Wait with us.'

The second man

'You would have me wait while you die.'

◆Somewhere in the stone, a door closes. The whispers go back to copying your breath.

Each voice tagged by kind; nobody named by the game.
2 — afterwards
› listen

Only your own breath comes back from the stone, and under it, very faintly, a door closing.

› examine walls

◆The runes describe a chant to quiet the phantoms: 'Let stillness echo, let silence bind.' You notice you could make rubbings of these ancient symbols.

Heard once; walls back to their usual reply.
3 — Keeper, next talk after the echo, figures still there
› talk to silence keeper

The Silence Keeper does not turn: 'Forty years I have listened to those stones. They are cold. Bless them; give them the chant.'

silence keeper

'The figures by the pools? They are not ghosts, not as the villages tell it. They are moments. Someone cut at time, years ago, and what came loose drifts down here, saying the same thing over. Tell them to rest; most of them only want leave to stop.'

Appended once to whatever she would say. Not on her very first talk (her story); then on the second.
4 — Keeper, figures already sent to rest
› talk to silence keeper

The Silence Keeper does not turn: 'Forty years I have listened to those stones. They are cold. Bless them; give them the chant.'

silence keeper

'The figures by the pools are gone, then. They were not ghosts, not as the villages tell it. They were moments. Someone cut at time, years ago, and what came loose drifted down here, saying the same thing over. You gave them leave to stop.'

Past tense instead.
