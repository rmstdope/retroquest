3 · cast mend again
> cast mend on inscription

You cast mend on the Charred Inscription.

The three names sit level and whole. There is nothing left to mend.

No second ✦ line.
4 · examine after mending
> examine inscription

You trace the charred letters with your finger, feeling the heat that once shaped them. The inscription reads:

We are three, and we are one promise.
I, Aren, pour courage into the Crystal of Light.
I, Lysa, pour selflessness into the Dragon's Scale.
I, Malakar, pour wisdom into the Phoenix Feather.
What we pour into the ward, the ward will keep.

The three names sit level in the stone, as they did the day the promise was made. Your parents and Malakar made this promise together, and the wisdom was his.

Only the closing line changes.
5 · talk to phoenix, story not yet told
> talk to phoenix

The phoenix inclines its head. 'You saw his name on the terraces, burned deeper than the others. No fire did that. He came back, years after, and struck it again himself, to cut himself out of the promise. The stone would not let him.' 'You gave him back his place. He could not do that for himself.' 'Remember him when time is short for you.'

Story once, ending on the new farewell.

7 · feather earned after mending
> say patience to phoenix

The phoenix's eyes flare with approval. It plucks a single glowing feather and offers it to you. 'Wisdom is patience, even when the world burns. Take this, and remember.' 'He gave your answer too. Then time grew short for his friends, and patience was the one thing he had none of. Do not become what he became.' You think of the third name on the terraces, level with the others again.

Only the last sentence differs.
