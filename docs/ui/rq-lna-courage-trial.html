The trial of courage — Sanctum of the Tide

Agreed: the lights go out as Elior arrives; he walks three steps east into the black water (west turns back, no penalty); on the third the water parts, the Crystal of Light is there, and the Guardian asks what he did not abandon. Near-miss answers get gentle, specific refusals. A save puts him back on the same step; leaving the Sanctum puts him back at the edge. The lights return after the vow. The Aren line plays when Elior talks to the Guardian afterwards.

Arriving (first time; the lights go out on arrival)

> east

Sanctum of the Tide

A vaulted chamber where water hangs like a dark mirror…

Then every light goes out at once — the sigils, the motes, the glow at your back. The Tide-Born Guardian speaks from the black: 'The lanterns brought you here. They will not take you further. Walk.'

Characters: Tide-Born Guardian, Sir Cedric

Exits: east, west

Casting light in the dark

> cast light

Tide-Born Guardian: 'Not here. Here you go without it.'

Talking at the edge

> talk to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'Walk east into the water. When you come through, I will ask you one thing.'

Saying “myself” before walking

> say myself to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'Words are easy on the shore. Walk first.'

Step 1

> east

You step down into the black water. It is cold to the knees, and you cannot see your own hands.

Exits: east, west

Step 2

> east

A voice like Mira's, close behind you: 'Turn back, Elior. There is nothing out there.'

Exits: east, west

Turning back (from any step)

> west

You wade back to the edge of the basin. The water closes behind you without a sound.

Exits: east, west

Step 3

> east

The water is at your chest. You call for Sir Cedric and hear nothing — the door is too far behind. You take the step anyway.

The water parts around you. In the hollow it leaves, the Crystal of Light burns in its basin, the only light in the world.

Tide-Born Guardian: 'You came through. What did you not abandon?'

Items: Crystal of Light

Taking the crystal before answering

> take crystal

Wards flare as you touch the crystal, locking it in place.

Near-miss answers

> say mira to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'Mira was not in the water with you. Her voice was. Who walked?'

> say cedric to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'The knight stayed at the door. You could not see him. Who did you still have?'

> say my friends to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'Your friends were behind you, in the light. Who was with you in the dark?'

> say the king to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'The king is the reason you came. He is not who came through.'

> say my parents to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'They are with you always, and still they did not walk. Who did?'

> say courage to guardian

Tide-Born Guardian: 'The tide listens, then falls quiet. That is not what you carried through the water.'

The vow

> say myself to guardian

The waters draw back as if breathing in. Your vow anchors the chamber's heart, and the relic heeds your courage. You may now take the Crystal of Light.

Tide-Born Guardian: 'Most name a person, and leave themselves behind in the dark. You walked in whole, and you walked out whole. That is the courage the crystal asks for.'

(Sir Cedric's exchange with the Guardian, already agreed, follows here.)
