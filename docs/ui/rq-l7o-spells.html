The Hall of Echoes

The hall, on first look
look

This vast hall stretches into shadow, its vaulted ceiling lost in darkness above. Every footstep awakens whispers from ages past, as if the very walls have absorbed the voices of countless souls. Ethereal sounds drift through the air—fragments of conversations, battle cries, and laments that seem to come from everywhere and nowhere. Tattered banners hang high along the walls, grimed past reading. At the hall's heart stands a figure in tarnished armor, motionless as a statue but radiating an aura of profound sorrow.

Optional targets elsewhere

None is needed to finish the act. Each answers with its line every time it is cast while its moment lasts. Once the orbs are mended, or the refrain is said, those two answer the ordinary line: “Your sight sharpens, but there is no illusion here to see through.” / “You listen for a hidden heart, but there is no lie here to hear beneath.”
