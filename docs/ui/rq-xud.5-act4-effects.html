Act IV under the Act I rule: no answer repeats your command

The same rule as Act I: an answer never repeats your command. A repeat is dropped, or the thing is named in the read sentence. Gifts and new spells are read aloud. Any red box with something true about the world is read, and only a bare “no” stays silent. Below is every Act IV line that changes. New words are green, and each card shows what it says today.

Gesture openings — the only Act IV choice still open

In Act I, “You walk the rows, pouring the clear well water…” and “You tell Tilda it was you…” stayed as read sentences, because they tell more than the command. Act IV has many of these. Dashed outline = a gesture opening. This drawing keeps them, read, as in Act I.
