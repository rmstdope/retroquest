A click meant for a story card that leaves by itself

While the pointer rests on a card, does its reading time keep counting?

A — PausedRecommendedAgreed

A card someone reads slowly stays its full time after they look away.

Recommended: The usual rule for things that leave by themselves (notifications): the timer stops under the pointer and the time left runs on after.

The gate creaks open. Beyond it, the orchard lies grey under frost.
↖
1. Pointer arrives, 8 s leftThe thin bar stops where it is.
The gate creaks open. Beyond it, the orchard lies grey under frost.
↖
2. Five seconds laterBar still stands still; still 8 s left.
The gate creaks open. Beyond it, the orchard lies grey under frost.
3. Pointer leavesThe bar moves on from where it stopped; the card goes on 8 s later.

A narrated part has no bar and its voice is not paused: if the line ends under the pointer, the card waits, and goes on 1.5 s after the pointer leaves.

B — Counts on

The card only waits once its time is up; a short look still loses reading time.

The gate creaks open. Beyond it, the orchard lies grey under frost.
↖
1. Pointer arrives, 8 s leftThe bar keeps shrinking.
The gate creaks open. Beyond it, the orchard lies grey under frost.
↖
2. Five seconds later3 s left. If it runs out, the card stays, bar empty.
The gate creaks open. Beyond it, the orchard lies grey under frost.
3. Pointer leavesThe card goes on 3 s later (or 1.5 s later if its time ran out under the pointer).
