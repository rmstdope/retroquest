A click meant for a story card that leaves by itself

When a story card goes by itself just as you click it, what does the click do?

A — Card waits while the pointer is on itRecommendedAgreed

A mouse resting on the card keeps it up until moved off; touch gets only the fade.

Recommended: The usual rule for things that leave by themselves (pause while pointed at); no hidden dead time on the picture.

Pointer elsewhere: the card fades as today (0.15 s); a click during the fade is the card’s, and after that the picture acts at once. Reduced motion: the same, with no fade. Touch: a tap during the fade is the card’s; a tap after it acts on the picture.

B — Card takes clicks only while it can be seen

Late clicks act on the picture again, as in the three earlier reports.

Reduced motion: no fade, so no grace at all. Touch: the same.

C — First click where the card stood does nothing

On touch, the first tap there after a card goes is always lost, however late.

Reduced motion: the same. A click elsewhere on the picture acts at once.

D — Keep today’s 1.5 seconds

Nothing changes for the player; the time window stays, and the next report is answered by tuning it again.

Reduced motion and touch: the same 1.5 s.
