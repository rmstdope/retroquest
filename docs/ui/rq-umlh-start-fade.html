Fading into the game

Agreed. Continue, a picked save, New game, and every load during play (the Load window, Quick load, typing load): what is on screen fades to dark over 1.5 s, then the room or intro rises over 1.5 s. The music follows the screen (the same music carries on untouched). The words and narrator start once it is fully up; the “Game loaded” notice comes with them. A click on the picture or Enter skips it. Press a button above to replay.
