The sharp knife is used up on the vines

Today cutting the vines takes the sharp knife out of Elior's pack, and the snare only appears after that, so the kit could never be freed. Something has to give.

K1 — the knife is kept (recommended)
› use sharp knife with vines

◆You use the sharp knife to cut through the thick vines, clearing the way to a small alcove.

You wipe the blade on the moss and keep it; it may yet be useful.

› look

In the alcove a fox kit is caught in a poacher's snare, too tired now to struggle.

› use sharp knife with snare

◆You use the sharp knife on the snare.

You kneel and saw through the wire, slow and careful. The moment it gives, the fox kit twists free, stares at you for one heartbeat, and bolts into the undergrowth.

The written command stays as designed. The cost: a knife in the pack for the rest of the act with nothing else to do.
K2 — free it by hand
› use sharp knife with vines

◆You use the sharp knife to cut through the thick vines, clearing the way to a small alcove.

(the knife is gone, as today)

› open snare

You work your fingers under the wire and ease the noose open, wincing as it bites. The fox kit twists free, stares at you for one heartbeat, and bolts into the undergrowth.

Nothing about the knife changes. A new command to discover, and “open snare” is less obvious than using a blade.
