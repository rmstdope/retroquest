Fewer guards than the posts were built for patrol the approach, and those who remain keep their voices low. The royal standard is gone from the highest spire.

Castle Courtyard
> look

An expansive courtyard within the castle walls, featuring training grounds where knights practice their swordwork. Ancient oak trees provide shade for stone benches, and a stable houses magnificent warhorses, though half its stalls stand empty since the king's own guard rode out to search for him. The castle's main hall rises before you, its great doors carved with the symbols of Greendale's noble houses.

Great Hall
> look

A magnificent hall with soaring ceilings supported by massive stone columns. Tapestries depicting legendary battles cover the walls, and the king's throne stands empty on a raised dais at the far end, a dark cloth laid across its arms. Behind it, the doors to the throne room are barred. Sunlight streams through tall stained-glass windows, casting colorful patterns on the stone floor. This is where the lords of Greendale hold court; for now, the Lord Commander holds it alone.

Training Master, first talk
> talk to training master
Training Master

Ah, a visitor to our training grounds. I've been instructing knights and squires here for many years.

It pains me to see a fine knight like Sir Cedric carrying such a heavy burden. He held the watch on the king's wall the night His Majesty vanished, and the next morning two men of the night watch swore he had left his post. They took his knighthood for it. But I've known Cedric for years, and he has never left a post in his life. Something about that whole affair never sat right with me. The watch's story was… too neat.

I've heard rumors that there are papers somewhere that tell a different story. Perhaps someone with your skills could help uncover the truth and restore honor to a good man who has suffered long enough.

Cedric's Lost Honor, in the quest journal

When the quest begins

Sir Cedric was stripped of his knighthood after the night King Alden vanished: two men of the night watch swore he had left his post. Investigate what happened that night and find evidence to clear his name.

After reading the diary

According to the squires and their diary, one of the watchmen who accused Cedric fled to Heavensforth and sent papers back with a merchant caravan that never arrived. Find these papers to restore his honor.

After examining the papers

You have found the papers: a watchman's confession that he and another were paid to leave the king's postern unguarded and to blame Sir Cedric, with the letter that paid them, sealed with a twisted rune. Present them to the Lord Commander in the Great Hall to restore his honor.

Completed

You have cleared Sir Cedric's name. The night watch was paid to leave the king's postern open and to blame him, and the letter that paid them was written in Malakar's name.
