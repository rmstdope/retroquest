Say… fans out the words Elior has met

Pressing Say… on a person opens a column of word pills beside it, the same way Buy…, Use with… and Cast on… open theirs. Pressing a word says it at once, exactly as typing say stay to grandmother would. There is no typing box in the ring (the command line still takes typed words). The column holds Yes and No, then every word Elior has met in this act, A to Z. A word not yet said carries a dot, and so does Say… while any word is unsaid.
