Ash Scholar

Ash Scholar

'This was in the same ledge. A notebook, burned nearly through. Whoever wrote in it knew these mirrors better than any of us, and I cannot make out who that was. Perhaps you can.'

You receive the Charred Notebook.

The notebook

Reading the notebook · any time, any number of times
› examine notebook

You open the Charred Notebook. Most pages have crumbled to flakes; two survive, in a neat and patient hand.

Lysa says I measure the light too carefully. Aren says that is why it never misses. We set the third mirror today, and the whole terrace caught fire with it, the good kind.

The Wasting has reached Willowbrook. Lysa coughs at night and pretends she does not. Aren sits up with her and pretends he is not afraid. They tell me to wait. I have waited all my life.

The second entry is pressed so hard into the page that the pen has torn it. The names in it are your parents'.
