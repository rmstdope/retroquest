1 · Memory Vault, first look (description kept)

Memory Vault

This mystical chamber exists partially outside of time itself, its walls covered in floating orbs of crystallized memory that flicker with scenes from long ago. The air ripples with temporal distortions, and fragments of the past drift like spectral snow through the space. At the room's center, a shadowy figure wavers between existence and void, reaching out with desperate longing toward memories that seem to slip away like morning mist.
