Option A — the dot takes the press (recommended)

Elior's Cottage, 1000×700, Showing on, Grandmother's words scrolled up. The Table dot shows through the faded top edge of the first card. Wherever a dot shows, it behaves like a dot: the pointer turns to a hand, “Table” appears, and pressing presses the table — exactly what pressing the Table dot does when the words are hidden. The rest of the card is untouched: pressing it anywhere else hurries the narration, as today.

Red dashed rings and red notes explain the drawing; they are not part of the design.
