ways out become buttons on the picture

Nothing a sighted player sees changes. A screen reader finds each way out on the picture the same way it already finds people and things, after them, in the order left to right.

A way that is closed right nowLeft out — a sighted player sees only painting there too.

Up, down and other directionsRead the same way: "Go down to …", "Go up to …".

new words are also spoken as they appear

Each bubble is its own paragraph and each sentence ends with a pause (today “light up.He” is read as one word), and the screen reader speaks each new story bubble when it appears, without moving the player's place — the way chat windows and text adventures usually work. Nothing on screen changes.

What is heard, unasked, after the action

  1. “You hand the fisherman the bait.”
  2. “The fisherman's eyes light up. ‹pause› He wraps it in a scrap of sailcloth.”

Spoken lines (a person talking) are spoken too, with the speaker's name first: “Mira: …”.

Cost: if the game's recorded narration voice is also on, the two voices talk over each other — so while recorded narration plays, the screen reader is not given the words (it can still read them in the story).
