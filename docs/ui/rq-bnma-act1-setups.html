Journal — A: four pages of handwriting

Each of the four paragraphs is its own handwritten block, as the journal does today with its two. The narrator reads each block as its own piece, so a player can skip one and hear the next.

Desktop
> read journal

The ink is slightly smudged on this page, as if written in haste or with a trembling hand...

The storm kept me up half the night. Then a light split the sky over the fields, and it was not lightning.
When I slept, I dreamed. Dark, and rain on shutters I could not see. A man’s hand lifted a child out of the dark. There was a tune, three notes up and home again, and a tall figure that whispered my name. When I answered it, it said three words more, and I wrote them down the moment I woke so I would not lose them: …and let them rest…
I knew that voice. Not the way you know a neighbour’s, but the way you know a song someone sang to you before you had words for it. It made my chest ache. I can’t think from where.
When I woke, the tune was still going. Someone out by the hearth was humming it.

Riverbank

A gentle river flows past the village and makes a slow bend here, past the reeds and a broad, flat stone at the water’s edge. Insects hum over the water. A weathered fisherman sits on an upturned crate at the bend, his line in.

> examine fisherman

An old man on an upturned crate at the bend, his line in the water and his bad arm held close to his side.

Village Chapel

A small stone chapel. Its stained glass windows cast colours across the worn pews, and the air is cool and smells of candle wax. A kindly priest tends the altar. By the door the bell rope hangs straight and still. Over the door a shape is cut into the stone, worn almost smooth. A dark corner by the altar swallows the light.
