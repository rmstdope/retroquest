Act II — Sir Cedric recognises the Warden sigil

Agreed. On the first talk with the amulet carried, Cedric breaks off his greeting, names the sigil as the mark of the Wardens of the Dawn, then asks for the knight's test.

First talk, amulet carried

Desktop
> talk to sir cedric
Sir Cedric

Greetings, traveler. I am Sir Cedric. I have been seeking individuals of courage and skill for a matter of great—

Sir Cedric stops mid-sentence. His eyes have fallen on the Ancient Amulet at your chest.

Sir Cedric

That sigil. The sun rising over three linked rings. I have seen it only in the old histories in the castle archive: it is the mark of the Wardens of the Dawn. They guarded this kingdom long ago, and most have forgotten them.

Whoever wore that amulet before you was a Warden, and your family is bound to them. I know no more than that. But I know enough to listen to you.

Dark forces are gathering; I call it the 'Gathering Storm', and I need allies with magical knowledge and proven abilities. The sigil tells me where you come from; it does not tell me what you can do. Can you demonstrate your martial abilities?

He sees the amulet on a later talk

Once only, on the first talk that finds it carried. The recognition comes first, then whatever he would have said on that talk anyway. Two examples:

Before the test
> talk to sir cedric

Sir Cedric's eyes fall on the Ancient Amulet at your chest, and he goes very still.

Sir Cedric

That sigil. The sun rising over three linked rings. I have seen it only in the old histories in the castle archive: it is the mark of the Wardens of the Dawn. They guarded this kingdom long ago, and most have forgotten them.

Whoever wore that amulet before you was a Warden, and your family is bound to them. I know no more than that. But I know enough to listen to you.

Sir Cedric

I await your demonstration of combat skills before we can proceed with more serious matters.

The talk where he hands over the charm
> talk to sir cedric

Sir Cedric's eyes fall on the Ancient Amulet at your chest, and he goes very still.

Sir Cedric

That sigil. The sun rising over three linked rings. I have seen it only in the old histories in the castle archive: it is the mark of the Wardens of the Dawn. They guarded this kingdom long ago, and most have forgotten them.

Whoever wore that amulet before you was a Warden, and your family is bound to them. I know no more than that. But I know enough to listen to you.

Sir Cedric

My friend, I cannot express how grateful I am. You have restored my honor and cleared my name of those terrible accusations. …

Sir Cedric reaches into his pack and withdraws an ancient wooden charm.

Sir Cedric

Please accept this Nature's Charm. …

The rest of that talk is unchanged.
