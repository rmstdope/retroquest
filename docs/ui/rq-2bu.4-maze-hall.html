The mirror labyrinth and the Hall of Echoes — the whole stretch, in play order

Your three choices put together: the pendant and stone used together, the knight present, the True Self at the maze's heart. The echoes speak plainly; saying the truth to the reflection shows a quiet "remembered" line. Lines marked NEW are new; everything else is already in the game and stays word for word.

1 · The ways that were shut now open
› go north (from the Fortress Gates, once the barrier is broken)

Hall of Echoes

This vast hall stretches into shadow, its vaulted ceiling lost in darkness above. Every footstep awakens whispers from ages past, as if the very walls have absorbed the voices of countless souls. Ethereal sounds drift through the air—fragments of conversations, battle cries, and laments that seem to come from everywhere and nowhere. At the hall's heart stands a figure in tarnished armor, motionless as a statue but radiating an aura of profound sorrow.

Today's words, kept. The "not yet" line at the gates goes away.

› go north (from the Outer Courtyard, once the servants are freed)

Opens into the Mirror Labyrinth. The "not yet" line in the courtyard goes away. Before the servants are freed the way north stays shut — see below.

› go north (courtyard, servants still in chains)

NEW · The trapped servants drift across the archway, their chains of shadow trailing behind them. You cannot pass while they are bound.

2 · The maze, first visit

Mirror Labyrinth

Endless corridors of gleaming mirrors stretch in impossible directions, each surface reflecting not just your image but fragments of possible futures and buried memories. The reflections move independently, some beckoning you forward while others shake their heads in warning. Crystal facets catch phantom light from unseen sources, creating a kaleidoscope of truth and deception that makes your head spin and your heart race.

NEW · New side quest — The Maze of Mirrors

Countless mirrors show false paths and twisted reflections. Find the one true way through the labyrinth.

› examine mirrors

NEW · Each mirror shows a different Elior — older, younger, crowned, broken — with a different corridor stretching away behind him. Every path looks real. Most of them are lies.

› use servant's pendant

You hold the Servant's Pendant and feel a warm connection to the freed servants. Their voices whisper guidance through the crystal: 'The mirrors show many paths, but only echoes from the ancient halls reveal the true way forward.'

› use servant's pendant (again)

The servants have already shared their guidance about the mirror labyrinth.

3 · The hall, first visit
› examine echoes

NEW · You stand still and listen. Beneath the battle cries and laments, one voice repeats the same words over and over, patient as a heartbeat: 'Carry our voices into the glass. Where the servants' light and the echoes meet, the true path shows itself.'

Says the same thing every time it is examined.

› take echo stone

NEW · You lift a smooth grey stone from a niche beneath the banners. It is warm, and when you hold it to your ear you hear the hall's echoes still speaking inside it.

› examine echo stone

NEW · A smooth grey stone that hums with the voices of the Hall of Echoes. Held close, it repeats what the echoes know.

› examine knight of despair

NEW · A knight in tarnished armour, a shredded grey cloak hanging from his shoulders. His visor is down, and he does not move. When he speaks, his voice is hollow: 'Return when you have the truth.'

› talk to knight of despair
Knight of Despair

NEW · There is nothing you can say to me that I have not already told myself. Go. Return when you have the truth.

› go east (the stair to the Tower of Shadows)

NEW · The Knight of Despair stands before the stair to the tower. He does not lift his sword. He simply does not move, and there is no way past him.

4 · Too early
› go north (or east or west, in the maze, before it is solved)

NEW · You step toward a corridor of light and walk straight into cold glass. Somewhere behind you, a reflection laughs.

5 · The maze, solved
› use echo stone with servant's pendant

(Also: use servant's pendant with echo stone, or use echo stone while carrying the pendant.)

NEW · You hold the Echo Stone and the Servant's Pendant together. The echoes whisper where to walk, and the pendant glows brighter at every true step. You walk only where your reflection does not follow. One by one the false mirrors darken, until a single corridor remains — and at its end, a plain mirror that shows you exactly as you are.

6 · The maze, after

Mirror Labyrinth

NEW · The false mirrors stand dark and silent now. A single clear corridor runs through the labyrinth to a plain mirror at its heart, where your own reflection waits, keeping still.

› use echo stone with servant's pendant (again)

NEW · The true path is already clear. The false mirrors stay dark.

› cast truth sight (anywhere there is nothing hidden)

Casting it on the knight is the next piece of work.

7 · The True Self (optional)
› examine true self

NEW · Your own reflection, in the plain mirror at the labyrinth's heart. It does not copy you. It waits, watching, as if it already knows what you will say.

› talk to true self
True Self

NEW · You told Mira you came for the king. Tell me why you really came.

› say truth to true self

NEW · You tell it the truth: that you did not come only for the king. You came because you want Malakar to pay for what he took from you, and you are afraid of what that wanting makes you. The reflection nods, slowly. For a moment it wears your father's face. Then it is only you again, standing a little straighter.

NEW · Elior will remember this.

› talk to true self (after)
True Self

NEW · Remember what you told me, when you stand before him.

› say truth to true self (again)

NEW · The reflection has heard your truth. It keeps still.

› say anything else to true self

NEW · The reflection only shakes its head.

8 · Save and reload

At any point in this stretch, a reload returns the same thing: which ways are open, whether the pendant's hint was heard, where the Echo Stone is, whether the maze is solved and Truth Sight known, and whether the truth was told to the reflection.
