Dragon's Hall

A hall of living stone opens beyond sight, its ceiling lost in shadow. Embers burn slowly along the floor, pulsing like a heartbeat, and somewhere in the dark, water drips. Rings of old sigils spiral across the black tiles around the Ancient Dragon. It lies coiled at the centre, scales like polished obsidian, and each slow breath hums in your bones.

You sense that these hallowed stones now await the ancient chant that will complete the ritual and banish the restless spirits from this sacred place.

Stillness Vestibule

A hush falls over this chamber like a shroud. Dark pools mirror the ceiling, so still they look frozen in time, and the stone seems to swallow every sound. At the centre, three weathered echo stones stand in a triangle. Their carved channels look made to catch whispered words and hold them.

Dragon's Hall

A cathedral of living stone where time itself seems to bow in reverence, this vast chamber stretches beyond mortal comprehension beneath a vaulted ceiling lost in eternal shadow. Primordial embers burn with slow, hypnotic rhythm along the floor, their amber light pulsing like the heartbeat of the earth itself as they illuminate the massive coils of an ancient being. Arcane sigils of forgotten civilizations spiral across obsidian tiles in intricate patterns that seem to shift and breathe when glimpsed from the corner of your eye, each rune thrumming with power that predates human memory. The air itself shimmers with residual magic, thick with the scent of ozone, aged parchment, and something indefinably ancient that speaks to the deepest parts of your soul. At the chamber's sacred heart, the Ancient Dragon rests in timeless majesty, its scales gleaming like polished obsidian kissed by starfire, each breath sending ripples of power through the mystical atmosphere that makes your very bones resonate with otherworldly harmonics.

Items you can see: Sigils

Characters present: Ancient Dragon

Exits: west (Stillness Vestibule)
