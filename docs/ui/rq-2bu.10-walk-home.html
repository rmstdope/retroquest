The walk home — agreed

The shape you chose: an arch opens north after the ending, the walk home, grow remembered on arrival, four companions to talk to, and grow closing the game with a line that names the ending. Dashed outline = new words. Dimmed = already agreed with the endings piece.

1 · The ending finishes (any ending)
› cast forgiveness on wound

[the farewell] … The light thins. They smile, and they are gone. The amulet in your hand is only a locket now, warm and quiet. Your parents are at rest.

◆You have completed the quest 'The Final Stand'.

Behind the throne an arch you had not noticed stands open. Beyond it, far off, the road runs home.

2 · Looking around the Throne Chamber afterwards
› look

[that ending’s room description] …

Behind the throne an arch stands open on the road home.

Exits: north (the way home), south

The arch sentence is added to the end of each of the three after-the-ending room descriptions.
3 · Walking home — Redemption or Justice
› north

You walk out of the fortress and do not look back. The road home is long, and somehow shorter than it was. Days later the hedges of Willowbrook close around you, and then you are standing at the edge of your own field.

Willowbrook Field

The field behind your home, as you left it: rows of withered crops under a pale sky, the earth cracked and grey. Grandmother waits at the gate. Mira stands beside her, and Sir Cedric leans on the fence. In the hedge a small light flickers: Nyx, keeping close. The air is quiet.

Characters: Grandmother, Mira, Sir Cedric, Nyx

Exits: none

You kneel at the edge of the field. Your hands remember the first spell you ever learned.

◆You remember the grow spell!

4 · Talking, all optional
› talk to grandmother
Grandmother

“You saw them. I can see it in your face.”

You tell her what they said: that they are proud of her, that they are resting.

Grandmother

“Resting. Good. Then so can we. Go on, love. The field has waited long enough.”

› talk to mira

Redemption:

You tell her what Malakar said: that he is sorry, and that he remembers her.

Mira

“He remembers. Then I will go and see him, one day. Not yet.”

Justice:

You tell her Malakar is sealed away.

Mira

“Good. And I am sorry, both at once. He taught me my first spell, too.”

Sacrifice:

She takes your hands and feels how quiet they are.

Mira

“All of it? Oh, Elior. Then I will teach you again, slowly, if you want to learn.”

› talk to sir cedric
Sir Cedric

“I have walked a long way beside you. This is a good place to stop.”

› talk to nyx

The little light brushes your cheek, warm, and settles back into the hedge.

5 · Casting grow ends the game
› cast grow

◆You cast grow.

You press your hands into the dry earth and say the smallest spell you know. Green runs out from your fingers along the rows. Leaves unfurl, the earth darkens, and the whole field breathes out at once.

Grandmother opens the gate.

Grandmother

“Welcome home, Elior.”
