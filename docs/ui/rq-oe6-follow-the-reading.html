The story follows the reading · Agreed

Glide with the word, hold the story still

While the narrator reads an answer, the story keeps the word being read on screen. Once the word gets within two lines of the bottom edge, the story glides up a line at a time to keep it there.

Until the reading ends, is skipped, or an action cuts in, the story cannot be scrolled. Any attempt (finger, wheel, keys) brings up the hint: Tap to hurry · double-tap to skip, or on a keyboard Space to hurry · Esc to skip. More below is hidden while it reads and comes back when the reading ends.

Only the story does this. The room card and the chapter page stay as they are.

Try it: press Play and watch the highlighted word. Then scroll the story up with your mouse or finger (or press Scroll away) while it reads, and see what happens.

Rejected: turning the page, keeping it centred, coming back after a pause, a button to go back, and scrolling stopping the reading.
