Spectral Librarian, option A — a keeper of shelves

In the Hidden Library. Habit: everything is said in terms of keeping, shelving and handling books. It grows out of the lines already agreed (“I am a keeper, not a teller”, “not written on any shelf of mine”).

Greeting (wards not yet mended)

Today: 'Welcome, seeker of knowledge. I am the eternal guardian of this repository. The protective enchantments around the most valuable texts have been damaged by time. Prove your worthiness…'

DESKTOP
> talk to spectral librarian
Spectral Librarian

'Mind the shelves. Everything here has been kept a long time, and the wards on the oldest texts are failing. I can keep a book; I cannot mend one. Mend the wards, and I will show you where to look for the mark you carry.'

The talk after the wards are mended

Today: 'Ah, you have repaired the protective enchantments with your mend spell. This shows both magical ability and respect for ancient knowledge. I shall reveal what you seek.'

Only the first speech changes; the rest is as agreed earlier.

DESKTOP
> talk to spectral librarian

Spectral Librarian: 'You mended the wards with your mend spell. They have been failing longer than I care to count; thank you. Now, what you came for.'

The spirit's eyes glow brighter as they speak:

Spectral Librarian

'I am a keeper, not a teller. The answer you came for is in that chronicle. The Wardens of the Dawn wrote it, and their mark is on its cover, the same mark you wear at your throat. Read it, and you will learn the dispel spell.'

They gesture to a crystal resting on the shelf:

Spectral Librarian

'And take this Crystal Focus. What you do with them is yours to choose.'

Today: …They radiate wisdom accumulated over centuries of guarding these precious texts. There's something both melancholy and noble about their eternal vigil.
