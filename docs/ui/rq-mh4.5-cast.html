Acts II–IV — the cast

Agreed with the team over the casting sheets (four auditioned voices per character, rounds until one was marked final). Voice ids are Fish Audio library voices; the manner in brackets is each speaker's fixed style. “Act II/III/IV” is said “Act Two/Three/Four”. Lyria is said LEER-ee-a.
