Option A — earlier speech dims a step with every new turn

Speech dims by turn, the way it already does on a phone: everything said in the newest turn is full strength, the turn before about 55%, older turns about 30%. A turn with nothing spoken still counts as a turn, so the old lines step back. When the conversation ends is unchanged: looking at something else still clears the right side.
