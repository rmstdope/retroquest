Tobin and the quiet charm — every case

Everything agreed so far, plus one line from the lead miner on the next talk after the gift. Highlighted text is new. Before the gift, Tobin and the miners behave exactly as already agreed for the mines.

Give the charm to Tobin (also: young miner, boy)
› give quiet charm to young miner

You give the quiet charm to Tobin.

Tobin turns the pale stone over in his fingers. As his hand closes around it, the endless dripping of the gallery seems to step back a pace, and his shoulders come down for the first time since you met him.

Tobin: 'It's quiet.' His voice is a cracked whisper. 'I could still hear it. Me, shouting. All this time, I could still hear it.'

Across the gallery, the lead miner sets down his pick and does not pick it up again.

› examine tobin
