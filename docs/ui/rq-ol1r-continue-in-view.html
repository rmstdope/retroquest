A long arrival keeps its “continue” in view

The arrival text lives only in the clear space between the “You are in” card and the Narration tab (on a computer: between the top bar and the Actions bar). When it is too tall, the continue pill stays put at the bottom of that space and the text scrolls above it, fading only at an edge where it continues. While the narrator speaks, the text follows the lit word. Short arrivals are unchanged.
