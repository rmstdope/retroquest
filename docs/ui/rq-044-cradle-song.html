The cradle song through Act IV

Agreed version. One line of lullaby, "Hush, little seed, the frost is going. Hush, the field will wake.", breaks off in a cracked orb, plays whole in the Parents' Vault, comes back in pieces from Malakar in Redemption and Justice, and is sung by Grandmother at the gate as Elior arrives, in every ending. Highlighted text is new; struck text goes.

1 · Memory Vault · before the orbs are mended
› examine orbs

The orbs are cracked and clouded. In one you glimpse three young people laughing over a workbench; in another, a cradle by a fire. Each scene breaks apart before you can hold it.

From the cracked one by the cradle a voice sings, low, "Hush, little seed—" and breaks off.

› examine orbs

Whole now. In one, three young Wardens laugh over a workbench; in another, a cradle sits by a fire. The clearest scenes all lie beyond the door to the east.

4 · Throne Chamber · Justice, the seal
› use amulet on wound

You press the open amulet to the wound. Threads of gold rise from King Alden's crown and weave into the ward's light. The tear closes, and Malakar goes with it, drawn into the seal. He does not fight.

Malakar: "I am sorry. Tell them— no. They know."

The last of him to go is his voice, low: "…the field will wake." Then the seal is shut.

King Alden: "Then it falls to me to set right what he broke. Rest now. You have done enough."

5 · Willowbrook Field · arriving, Redemption or Justice

You walk out of the fortress and do not look back. The road home is long, and somehow shorter than it was. Days later the hedges of Willowbrook close around you, and then you are standing at the edge of your own field.

At the gate Grandmother is singing to herself: "Hush, little seed, the frost is going—" She sees you, and stops.

You kneel at the edge of the field. Your hands remember the first spell you ever learned.
