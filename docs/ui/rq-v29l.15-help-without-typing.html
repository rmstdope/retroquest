Agreed: option A. The new section “Playing without typing” opens Help, above the typed commands. Outlined text is new; struck-through text goes (the two loose lines move into the new section; the stray comma goes). On a phone the F1 line is left out, as today, and “Move the mouse over” reads “Tap”.

Laptop (with a keyboard)
RetroQuest
Help
Playing without typing: Move the mouse over the picture to see the names of what is here. Press a thing or a person for a wheel of what you can do with it. Press a name in the story for the same choices. In the wheel, Use with… takes the thing in hand; then press what to use it with, or ✕ Cancel. Press a thing in your bag for its wheel. Press a way out of the picture to walk there, or a place on the map to travel to it. Press the room's name for Look, Search and Rest. Press F1 to show or hide the command bar; type commands there instead. Available Commands: Movement: go <direction>, move <direction>, <direction>, <short_direction> (e.g., go north, , move north, north, n) Interaction: talk to <character>, speak to <character>, converse with <character> say <word(s)> to <character> buy <item> from <character> … Examination, Inventory Management, Magic, Game Management, Miscellaneous as today Narration: Tap the story or press Space to hurry the current line; hold to show the rest at once. Any action cuts in. Set its volume, down to silent, in Settings. Press the room's name for Look, Search and Rest. Press F1 to type commands instead; F1 again hides the box. Esc — close what's open, or open the menu F — full screen on or off N — hide or show the words … music credits as today
