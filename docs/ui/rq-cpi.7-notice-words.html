The words of the “remembered” line

It comes last, after the character's reaction, in the amber used for game events, once per moment. Acts II–IV will reuse the same three lines, so these words are the game's names for the three virtues.

Desktop

W1 — sentence, everyday words (recommended)
› use sharp knife with snare

You kneel and saw through the wire, slow and careful. The moment it gives, the fox kit twists free, stares at you for one heartbeat, and bolts into the undergrowth.

✦ Your mercy will be remembered.

› say truth to tilda
Tilda

Magic? In Willowbrook? ... Well. I'll not tell a soul if you'd rather I didn't. But I'll know, and I'll be saving you the first loaf every morning.

✦ Your honesty will be remembered.

› give fresh bread to merchant
merchant

Still warm. ... What do I owe you? No? Nothing at all? Then you've a better heart than most I meet on this road, friend. I'll not forget it.

✦ Your generosity will be remembered.

Reads as part of the story. “Honesty” and “generosity” are what a player would call it.
