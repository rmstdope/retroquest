Sir Cedric faces the knight in the Hall of Echoes (agreed)

Cedric follows Elior in once the gates fall and stays by the hall's south door for the rest of the act. He hears the confession himself. He holds back, and forgives the knight only if the player then puts the hall's ghostly warriors to rest in front of him. The player is never given a choice. The epilogue remembers what he decided.
