Optional lines are voiced — option A: recordings only

Every fixed line a player can reach only through an optional moment in Acts II–IV gets a recording in its speaker's voice: the virtue moments, the later lines they colour, the three endings and their variants, and optional scenes. Nothing else about how the story is read changes. 🔊 = heard; the orange bar = the line being read now.
