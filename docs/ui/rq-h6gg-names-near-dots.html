Option C — Dot only until you point at it (recommended)

Where there is no free room near a dot (Shelves and Books here), it shows the dot alone while the words are open; pointing at the dot, or reaching it with the keyboard, shows its name right beside it as it does today. Hide the words and both names come back beside their dots. Every other name sits right beside its dot, in the strip under the card. This is how maps handle crowded labels: a label that cannot sit by its place is left off, never sent far away.
