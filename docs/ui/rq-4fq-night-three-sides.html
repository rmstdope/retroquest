The night the king was taken, from three sides

The agreed version. Highlighted text is new. The knight admits it in his confession, which every player sees. Once the captain is awake, his line opens with a memory of King Alden. The first time you talk to him after the knight is redeemed, he also tells of the man in grey; later talks drop it. The knight still kneels in the hall, and his waiting line is unchanged. The player connects the two.

Captain, still enthralled (unchanged)
› talk to captain

The Captain of the Guard stares through you. 'I held the gate for King Alden once. Now I hold it for... I cannot remember.'

Captain, awake, before the redemption
› talk to captain

The Captain of the Guard lifts his head and meets your eyes. 'I remember now. King Alden used to walk the walls at night and talk to whoever was on them. That is how they got him. Cedric stood his post — it was shadows that came for Alden, not any blade of ours. If you see Sir Cedric again, tell him I am sorry I ever doubted him.'

The redemption
› say redemption to knight

You say the word, and the knight's despair breaks against the chain's light like a wave on stone. Slowly, he lifts his visor. The face beneath is grey and tired, and very human.

Knight of Despair: "I was the stranger in Willowbrook. Malakar sent me for an amulet, and for the child it hid. It was my token that fouled your well."

Knight of Despair: "The night King Alden was taken, I paid the watch to look away. The captain of the watch was a good man. I made sure he was asleep. And I put my seal to the lie that shamed Sir Cedric."

Knight of Despair: "And when I saw what my master was doing to the king, I stopped believing he could be saved. That is all despair is. You have given me something to believe in again."

Knight of Despair: "When you see Sir Cedric again, tell him the knight in grey is sorry. Tell him I will say it to his face, if he will let me."
