Tap for the next step, hold to skip

One rule everywhere the story is being told: a tap never does more than one step (it finishes the line being read, then goes on to the next), and holding for about 0.7 s skips the whole telling. A ring fills while you hold; let go early and nothing is skipped. Esc skips at once. The double tap to skip goes away.

Try it

Click (or press Enter/Space) on the picture for one step. Hold the mouse button, or hold Enter/Space, to skip. Switch between the two ways of showing the hold.

Other details

  • A tap acts when you let go (as a button does), so a hold never takes a step first and then skips.
  • Hold time: 0.7 s. Letting go before it is full does nothing and counts as no tap either, so a hold given up never moves the story on by accident.
  • Keyboard: holding Enter or Space works the same as holding the mouse. Esc skips at once, with no hold.
  • Reduce motion: the ring and the fill do not animate; a steady “Skipping…” label shows while holding, and the skip happens at the same 0.7 s.
  • Screen readers: the hint text says “Hold to skip”, and Esc is the quick way.
