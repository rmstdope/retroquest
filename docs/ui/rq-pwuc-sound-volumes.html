Agreed: no on/off switches in Sound. Music, Sound effects and Narration each have a label, their grey note and a volume slider on its own line (0–100). At 0 the number reads 0. A player who had one of them switched off finds it at volume 0. At narration volume 0 the story is still told line by line, silently.
