3 · A long answer (the journal)

The text climbs the left side and fades out at the top, and it scrolls. The shadow covers more of the picture than option A's cards do.

The ink is slightly smudged on this page, as if written in haste or with a trembling hand…

The storm kept me up half the night. Then a light split the sky over the fields, and it was not lightning.

When I slept, I dreamed. Dark, and rain on shutters I could not see. A man's hand lifted a child out of the dark. There was a tune, three notes up and home again, and a tall figure that whispered my name. When I answered it, it said three words more, and I wrote them down the moment I woke so I would not lose them: …and let them rest…

I knew that voice. Not the way you know a neighbour's, but the way you know a song someone sang to you before you had words for it. It made my chest ache. I can't think from where.

When I woke, the tune was still going. Someone out by the hearth was humming it.
