On a phone, leaving the story or starting an action catches up

Phones show one section at a time. If you leave the story while the voice is reading, the rest of the turn is shown at once and the voice stops. The section you open is then up to date. Press Map or Quests at moment 1.

Cost: you lose the voice for the rest of that turn. But you had left the text it was reading, and nothing you look at is ever out of date.
