Forest hermit, option B — the forest is “she”

At the edge of the Enchanted Forest. Habit: the hermit talks about the forest as a person with moods, always “she”. Nothing else in the act talks this way, so it stays clear of the mountain hermit once he becomes Cedric's old sergeant.

First talk (gives the charm)

Today: The forest hermit studies you with ancient eyes. 'I have been waiting for you, young one. The forest spirits whispered of your coming…' 'Ancient guardians protect its secrets…'

DESKTOP
> talk to forest hermit

You approach the forest hermit.

The forest hermit glances at the treeline before looking at you, as if checking who might be listening.

Forest Hermit

'You're going in. She's in a sour mood this season, so mind yourself.'

Forest Hermit

'She moves her paths when she's bored, and she keeps old things in her that don't care for strangers. Everything that lives in there is hers. Ask before you take, and never take more than you give.'

Forest Hermit

'But you... there is old magic on you, and not of your own making. She'll notice it. Take this charm—it tells her you're under my protection. And don't go to her unready; sort out your pack before you cross.'

You receive a protective charm!
