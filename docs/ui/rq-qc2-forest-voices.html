The forest voices — the full script

Every line the four forest characters say, in every state, Agreed. Light-touch voices: the tree spirit speaks in seasons, the nymphs answer with questions, the sprites finish each other’s sentences, Nyx says plainly what she saw Elior do. A dashed outline marks new words; anything not outlined stays exactly as today. Nothing else on screen changes: same colours, same layout, same commands.

The tree spirit

Look at it

Desktop
> examine ancient tree spirit

You study the Ancient Tree Spirit. The spirit lives in the silver-barked tree. Its face is only there when you stop looking for it: two knots for eyes, a split in the bark for a mouth. Moss grows on it in rings, one for every winter. The bark is warm under your hand, and below your feet the roots shift slowly, like someone turning over in their sleep.

You approach the Ancient Tree Spirit.

A face gathers in the knots of the silver bark and turns toward you. “You walk like spring. Everything new, nothing kept.”

“I don’t teach for nothing. Bring me something that hasn’t grown yet.”

Give the acorn

> give enchanted acorn to ancient tree spirit

You offer the enchanted acorn to the Ancient Tree Spirit.

The bark around the knots softens. “Next spring’s tree, and you carried it here instead of planting it. Good. Listen, then.”

The voices of the grove settle into you: the creak of branches, the talk of roots, the small sounds in the undergrowth. You can understand them now.

You have learned the Forest Speech spell!

“Now you can hear them, you’ll hear the grove is uneasy. The nymphs in the glade east of the entrance have something for these roots. Answer them, and bring back what they give you.”

The water nymphs

Look at them

Desktop
> examine water nymphs

You study the water nymphs. Three of them, or four; it is hard to count water. They sit waist-deep in the stream, and the stream runs through them. When you lean closer, every one of them leans closer too.

The forest sprites

Look at them

Desktop
> examine forest sprites

You study the forest sprites more closely. Motes of light no bigger than a thumbnail, each with wings like a split beech-nut husk. They never all hold still at once. The longer you watch, the more it seems they are watching you back.

Talk — first time

Desktop
> talk to forest sprites

You approach the forest sprites.

“A traveller—” “—with muddy boots—” “—wanting the deep paths.”

“They hide from the dark—” “—and from anyone unwarded.” “Bring light and a ward—” “—and they’ll stop hiding.”

“And the nymphs have riddles—” “—east of here—” “—if you’re clever.”

Describes today’s gate: both the light and the ward are needed.

First meeting

Desktop
> talk to nyx

The light over the altar folds into a woman no taller than you, barefoot, leaves caught in her hair. She looks at you the way you look at a road you have walked before.

“Elior. You gave the tree your acorn instead of keeping it. You answered the nymphs without bargaining. You brought me the three charms. That is enough.”

She touches your forehead. Something settles behind your eyes, like a word you had forgotten coming back. She puts two things in your hands.
