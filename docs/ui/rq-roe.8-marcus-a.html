How does a player learn the merchant is hungry?

The kindness is giving Grandmother's fresh bread and taking nothing back. Today nothing says he is hungry, so the gift comes from nowhere.

M1 — he says so when you talk (recommended)
› talk to merchant

◆You speak with the merchant.

The merchant offers a friendly nod.

merchant

Looking for something specific, or just browsing? …

merchant

Forgive me if I'm short, friend. I've not eaten since the hill road, and there's no inn this side of Greendale.

› give fresh bread to merchant

◆You give the fresh bread to the merchant.

The merchant unwraps the cloth and simply holds the loaf for a moment.

merchant

Still warm. ... What do I owe you? No? Nothing at all? Then you've a better heart than most I meet on this road, friend. I'll not forget it.

Dialogue is where a text adventure puts a need. Added at the end of every talk until he has been given food.
M2 — you see it when you look at him
› look at merchant

A traveling merchant with a cart full of wares and stories from distant lands.

His cheeks are hollow, and you notice he keeps glancing at the empty crust in his pack.

› give fresh bread to merchant

◆You give the fresh bread to the merchant.

The merchant unwraps the cloth and simply holds the loaf for a moment.

merchant

Still warm. ... What do I owe you? No? Nothing at all? Then you've a better heart than most I meet on this road, friend. I'll not forget it.

Subtler: only a player who looks closely finds it. Many will never see the moment exists.

Option A — Marcus says it was bad luck, every time

After Elena is cured, Marcus believes the curse was simply bad luck. The truth is the silver mask Elior saw on her wrist: someone set it on her on purpose. Here his belief closes every talk after the charm, until Elior tells him.

A — on every talk after the cure (recommended)
› talk to innkeeper marcus (after the charm)
innkeeper marcus

My daughter is healthy and happy again, all thanks to you! …

innkeeper marcus

An ill wind, the healer called it. Bad luck, blown in off the road, settling on the one soul in this house who never harmed anyone.

› say truth to marcus

You tell Marcus about the silver mask on Elena's wrist: the curse was no ill wind. Someone set it on her on purpose.

innkeeper marcus

On purpose. … Someone chose my girl. … Then this house sleeps with one eye open from now on. Thank you for telling me. I'd sooner know it than be comforted by a lie.

✦ Truth — this will be remembered

› talk to innkeeper marcus (after)
innkeeper marcus

My daughter is healthy and happy again, all thanks to you! …

(no bad-luck line any more)

Cost: Marcus repeats himself until Elior speaks. Same shape as the hungry merchant in Act I.
