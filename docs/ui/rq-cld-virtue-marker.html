Act IV · mercy for the ghostly warriors
› cast redemption's light on warriors

Soft light spills from your hands across the hall. One by one the ghostly warriors lower their blades and look at one another as if for the first time. Then they fade, at peace.
Sir Cedric watches the last of them go. Then he crosses the hall and offers the knight his hand.

Sir Cedric

'If they can be let go, so can this. I forgive you. Get up.'

✦ Mercy — this will be remembered

Act IV · the truth to the True Self
› say truth to reflection

You tell it the truth: that you did not come only for the king. You came because you want Malakar to pay for what he took from you, and you are afraid of what that wanting makes you. The reflection nods, slowly. For a moment it wears your father's face. Then it is only you again, standing a little straighter.
