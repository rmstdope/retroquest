Where the news is written down

The game already has two kinds of journal. J1 adds a new entry to the main quest of the act, Shadows Over Willowbrook. That quest already records each new discovery about the village's trouble, and it shows the usual "quest updated" banner under the Merchant's answer. J2 adds a new page to the book Elior carries, which the player sees only by reading it again. Wording is a first draft.

J1 — main quest journal (recommended)
› talk to merchant

◆You speak with the merchant.

Merchant

You'll want news, if you're bound for Greendale. It's a sombre city this season: they say King Alden has fallen ill, and no physician can name the sickness. And the roads have gone bad besides. I passed a cart on its side by the hill road, and nobody with it. And it's not only your fields that are failing. I've seen crops gone grey in every valley from here to the capital, ever since the king took to his bed. Keep to the daylight, friend.

J2 — Elior's own journal
› read journal  (after the merchant)

◆You open your journal and begin to read the latest entry.

Met a merchant on the Greendale road today. He says King Alden is ill and no one can tell why, and that the crops are failing far beyond our valley. I keep thinking of our fields. What if this is all one sickness?

In Elior's own voice. The cost: he only sees it if he reads the journal again, and the dream entry he reads today is no longer the "latest entry". That means a second question: how to reach the dream entry.
