General Store

Store 1: the lane Hemlock already watches (chosen)

Shop shut (Patches missing)
A cluttered shop with the shutters half down. Rope, apples and trinkets crowd the shelves in the gloom. Hemlock stands at the window, watching the lane to the chapel.
Shop open
A cluttered shop. Rope, apples and trinkets crowd the shelves, and the air smells of fruit, wax and old wood. Outside, the lane runs east to the chapel. Hemlock dusts jars between customers, and every so often looks at the counter, where his cat sleeps.

Village Chapel

Chapel 1: the door and the lane (chosen)

A small stone chapel at the end of the village lane. Its stained glass windows cast colours across the worn pews, and the air is cool and smells of candle wax. A kindly priest tends the altar. By the door the bell rope hangs straight and still. Over the door a shape is cut into the stone, worn almost smooth. A dark corner by the altar swallows the light.
