The sergeant and Cedric's sword

Agreed: the old man on the mountain path says plainly that he is Cedric's former sergeant, the sword shows its notch, Cedric is quietly moved in the courtyard, and the old man hears how it went. These are the words as they ship.

Option A for every choice. The forest hermit is unchanged.

DESKTOP
> look at mountain hermit

A broad old man in a patched grey castle cloak, sitting straight-backed by a small fire. His hands are scarred across the knuckles, and he watches the path between Willowbrook and Greendale the way a sentry watches a gate.

> talk to mountain hermit

You approach the mountain hermit.

The mountain hermit looks you up and down, the way he might inspect a recruit.

Mountain Hermit

'Greendale, is it? Thirty years I drilled squires in that castle's yard, and I've never heard the city so uneasy. The king gone, the guard half-empty. Walk carefully.'

He unwraps a battered Training Sword from an old cloth.

Mountain Hermit

'This was Cedric's. I put it in his hands when he was a squire no taller than you. They say he slept on watch the night the king went, and lost his spurs for it. Take it to him in the castle yard. Tell him the sergeant never believed a word of it.'

You receive a Training Sword!

Looking at the sword in the pack
> look at training sword

A well-balanced practice sword with a dulled blade. The grip is worn smooth by years of a young hand, and there is a small notch near the guard. Though not sharp enough for real combat, it's perfect for demonstrating martial skills and training exercises.

Later, before Elior has shown Cedric the sword
> talk to mountain hermit

You approach the mountain hermit.

The mountain hermit jerks his chin toward Greendale.

Mountain Hermit

'You've still got that blade, I see. Get it to Cedric, and mind you give him my words.'

In the castle courtyard, with Sir Cedric there
> use training sword

You draw the training sword. Before you can take your stance, Sir Cedric catches your wrist and turns the blade to the light.

Sir Cedric

'That notch by the guard. I put it there at fourteen, swinging at a post I'd been told to leave alone. Where did you get this?'

You tell him about the old man by the fire on the mountain path, and what he asked you to say.

Sir Cedric is silent for a long moment. When he speaks, his voice is low.

Sir Cedric

'The sergeant never believed in anything he couldn't hit. That he believes in me...' He lets go of your wrist and steps back. 'Show me what he taught you, then.'

[the forms and the rest of Cedric's speech play on exactly as today]

Later, after Cedric has recognised it
> talk to mountain hermit

You approach the mountain hermit.

The mountain hermit hears you out and grunts, but there is something softer in his face.

Mountain Hermit

'Knew the notch, did he? Good. Then he knows. Go on, now. Greendale needs him more than I do.'
