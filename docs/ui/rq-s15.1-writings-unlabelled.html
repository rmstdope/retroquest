Option A — written words carry no label

The journal page and the letter look exactly as they do today: an unlabelled quote. The signature line at the end stays the reveal.

Reading the journal
› use journal

◆You open your journal and begin to read the latest entry.

The ink is slightly smudged on this page, as if written in haste or with a trembling hand...

"What a night. The wind howled like a hungry wolf, and rain lashed against the shutters… When I finally drifted off, the dreams came… Elior... I woke with a start, my heart pounding. What did it mean?"

No name over the page; the player knows it is their own journal from the line above.
Examining the letter
› examine letter

◆You examine the Sea-Sealed Letter.

◆You unfold the salt-preserved vellum carefully. The ancient ink, protected by crystalline varnish, reveals words in a familiar hand:

'My beloved Mira, if you read this, then our fears have come to pass and Malakar has discovered our betrayal. We were his apprentices once—Lyra and I both served in his shadow…'

'The three relics hold the key to a ward that can hide our son from his sight… The ward will hold until he is strong enough to face what we could not.'

◆The letter is signed with two names: Lyra and Theron—your parents. Your hands tremble as you realize this testament of their defiance has waited in the depths all these years.

Nothing names the writers until the last line: “signed with two names: Lyra and Theron”.
