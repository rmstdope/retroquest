Act III — the parents' three clues, agreed

The three Echoes of the Hidden Bond clues and the mural that hides the letter. Aren and Lysa were Malakar's fellow Wardens and friends, defied him, and became the ward. The amulet's glow, the curse beats, the refrain and the dream voice follow each clue and are agreed in other pieces of work.

1 · Sunken Ruins — the mural and the Sea-Sealed Letter

Mural, before and after the letter is found
> look at mural

A faded mural shows two figures shielding a small child from a storm of sigils, while a third reaches toward them. Salt has softened the colours; you cannot tell whether the third hand means to help or to take.

> examine mural

Fine salt lines run along the mural's base. Pressing the stone, your hand finds a hollow and a dry scrap falls into your palm. A Sea-Sealed Letter peels free, its ink half-hidden by varnish made of salt and tide.

> examine mural

The two figures still shield the child, and the third still reaches toward them. Where a small box was sealed into the paint, empty stone now shows its place.

Finding the letter works as today; only the picture changes.
The letter, with Malakar's reply
> examine letter

You examine the Sea-Sealed Letter.

You unfold the salt-preserved vellum carefully. The ink, protected by crystalline varnish, is still clear:

Malakar, our friend —

We made the relics together, the three of us, and swore they would only ever guard, never take. Now you speak of time as if it were a door you could open and close again. It is not a door. It is a wound, and what comes through it will not stop at saving us.

We are not afraid of what is coming. We are afraid of what you will become to stop it. Wait with us. Be patient, for once. If you will not, we will stand against you, not as your enemies but as your friends, and as Wardens of the Dawn.

— Aren and Lysa

Beneath their names, in a sharper hand and darker ink, someone has written a reply:

You would have me wait while you die. — M.

Your parents wrote to Malakar as a friend. He read it, and answered.

> take letter

You carefully fold the letter — a plea to a friend, kept by the sea.

Looking at the letter shows the same text. It still has to be read before Mira teleports the party on.

2 · Mount Ember — the Charred Inscription

Three signed vows
> look at inscription

Words burned deep into cooled lava, the letters blackened by intense heat. Three hands wrote here, one after another.

> examine inscription

You trace the charred letters with your finger, feeling the heat that once shaped them. The inscription reads:

We are three, and we are one promise.

I, Aren, pour courage into the Crystal of Light.

I, Lysa, pour selflessness into the Dragon's Scale.

I, Malakar, pour wisdom into the Phoenix Feather.

What we pour into the ward, the ward will keep.

The last name is burned deeper than the others, as if the stone was struck again long after it cooled. Your parents and Malakar made this promise together, and the wisdom was his.

Reading it again shows the same text. It still has to be read before the party leaves Mount Ember.

3 · Caverns of Shadow — the Dragon's Memory

One telling, on the first talk
> talk to dragon

The ancient dragon's voice resonates through your mind like distant thunder:

ancient dragon

Young one, I remember your parents well. Aren and Lysa, Wardens of the Dawn. They came to me with their friend Malakar, the three of them laughing, and made the relics as equals.

When Malakar tore a wound in time, it was they who stood against him. They poured themselves into a ward. It hides you from his sight, and it holds his wound shut. It is anchored in the amulet you carry, and in the bindings of your king. That is why Malakar drains him.

They are not gone as the dead are gone, child. They are warded away, not dead.

Then, in another piece of work: the refrain, the dream voice and the amulet's third glow.
> talk to dragon

The ancient dragon rumbles softly:

ancient dragon

The memory has been shared. Your path leads forward now, not back. The scale awaits when you are ready to prove your selflessness.

The quest's third clue is recorded on the first talk, as today. Later talks keep today's line.
