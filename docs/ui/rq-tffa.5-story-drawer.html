The story so far drawer — agreed

Laptop. ⟲ Story so far opens a drawer from the right, half the window wide, hanging under the row of buttons and ending above the action row. It opens at the newest turn, with the whole story above it as today. ✕, Escape, pressing the lit button again, pressing anywhere outside it, or doing something closes it. Opening Bag, Spells or Quests swaps it for their drawer.

Rejected: a drawer as narrow as the Bag drawer; opening at the start of the act; staying open while playing; a full-height drawer with the buttons stepping aside; covering the action row; no way back to the newest.
