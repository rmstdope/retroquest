Tower — Option A: carrying the token is enough; using it is only the hint

The player can name the friends straight away, as long as the token is in the pack. "use loyalty token" in the tower no longer opens anything; it gives the hint. Highlighted text is new.

Using the token — the first hint
› use loyalty token

You hold the token up, and it grows warm. Under the whispers, very faint, you think you hear your name, in Mira's voice. They are not gone. Say their names.

Arriving (room text unchanged; the whispers are listed)
› east

Tower of Shadows

A spiraling tower of black stone pierces the gloom, … the very shadows appear to mock your every movement with silent, dancing forms.

You hear: whispers

Exits: west

› up

You set your foot on the stair, and the whispers close in: your friends have left you, no one is coming, you were always alone. You stumble back down.

› talk to whispers

They do not answer you. They only say it again, closer: your friends have left you. No one is coming. Name one who stayed, if you can.

First name
› say mira to whispers

The token warms against your palm, and the whispers falter.

Mira: "I'm at the gate, Elior. I said I'd hold it, and I'm holding it."

Second name
› say cedric to whispers

The token warms again. The whispers draw back from the stair.

Sir Cedric: "A knight does not leave his post. Mine is the threshold. Go on, lad."

Third name — the stair opens
› say nyx to whispers

A small light flickers at the edge of your eye.

Nyx: "Left? I'm right here. I never was anywhere else."

The whispers have nothing left to say. They thin like smoke, and the stair above lies open.

Exits: west, up
