Tower of Shadows

Arriving · look
A spiraling tower of black stone pierces the gloom, its walls seeming to absorb light itself. Tendrils of living shadow coil around the structure, reaching out with grasping fingers toward any source of warmth or hope. The air grows thicker with each step upward, and whispered doubts slither into your mind like poison. Windows reveal only deeper darkness, and the very shadows appear to mock your every movement with silent, dancing forms.
A stair winds up inside the black stone, narrow and dark. The walls are cold, and there are no windows. The shadows on the stair move a moment after you do. With every step, something quiet at your ear tells you why you should not have come.

Chamber of Whispers

Arriving · look
This circular chamber pulses with an otherworldly beauty that feels wrong, as if reality itself has been twisted into a more pleasing shape. Crystalline formations jut from the walls, each one humming with barely audible whispers that speak of your deepest desires and fears. The floor shifts beneath your feet like liquid glass, and the air shimmers with illusions that promise everything you've ever wanted while hiding terrible truths in their depths. At the chamber's heart the Sorceress of Lies waits, her staff crowned with a small silver mask. Your breath catches: it is the mask from Elena's wrist.
A round room at the top of the tower, and the only beautiful room in the fortress. That is what is wrong with it. Crystals grow from the walls, each humming something kind and untrue, and the floor gives a little under your feet, like ice that might hold.
