Mira’s Act III opening — agreed

Mira’s first talk in Act III drops from eight speech lines to four, in her plain voice: the danger, the three relics, the note (read it now), and “say the word”. What the Crystal does moves to her Tidal Causeway line; her Mount Ember line no longer counts virtues. Lines marked NEW are new or changed; everything else is unchanged.
