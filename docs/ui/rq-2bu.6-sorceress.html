The Sorceress of Lies — the whole stretch, in play order

Your choices put together: the silver mask, the token raised once in the tower, Elior recognising the mask on walking in, Heart's Truth learned when she sees the knight's badge, and the Sorceress staying unmasked by the north arch. Her gift here is her mask. Lines marked NEW are new; everything else is already in the game and stays word for word.

1 · Tower of Shadows (description kept)

Tower of Shadows

A spiraling tower of black stone pierces the gloom, its walls seeming to absorb light itself. Tendrils of living shadow coil around the structure, reaching out with grasping fingers toward any source of warmth or hope. The air grows thicker with each step upward, and whispered doubts slither into your mind like poison. Windows reveal only deeper darkness, and the very shadows appear to mock your every movement with silent, dancing forms.

› go up (before the token)

NEW · You set your foot on the stair, and the whispers close in: your friends have left you, no one is coming, you were always alone. You stumble back down.

› use loyalty token (kept)

You hold the Loyalty Token close to your heart, feeling the warmth of true friendship and trust. The token's power forms a protective barrier against the lies and deceptions that whisper through the shadowy tower.

› use loyalty token (again — kept)

The Loyalty Token is already protecting you from deception.

› go up

Up to the Chamber of Whispers. The token's protection carries with Elior; it is never needed again.

2 · Chamber of Whispers, first look

Chamber of Whispers

This circular chamber pulses with an otherworldly beauty that feels wrong, as if reality itself has been twisted into a more pleasing shape. Crystalline formations jut from the walls, each one humming with barely audible whispers that speak of your deepest desires and fears. The floor shifts beneath your feet like liquid glass, and the air shimmers with illusions that promise everything you've ever wanted while hiding terrible truths in their depths.

Save and reload

At any point a reload returns the same state: whether the token has been raised (and so the stair up), whether the quest has started, whether the badge has been shown (and so Heart's Truth is known), whether Heart's Truth has been cast on her, whether she is redeemed (and so the arch north, her name and lines, the chamber's words and what Elior carries).
