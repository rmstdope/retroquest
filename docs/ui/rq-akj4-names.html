Option A — spell cards colour the people you can meet (recommended)

The “New spell” card colours a name in blue exactly when a quest card would: the people the player can find and talk to. People who are only remembered (your mother, your father, the Wardens, the carter) stay plain, as they do on quest cards today. Red dashes mark what changes.
