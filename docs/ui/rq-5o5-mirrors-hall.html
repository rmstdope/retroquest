Mirror Labyrinth

Arriving · look
Endless corridors of gleaming mirrors stretch in impossible directions, each surface reflecting not just your image but fragments of possible futures and buried memories. The reflections move independently, some beckoning you forward while others shake their heads in warning. Crystal facets catch phantom light from unseen sources, creating a kaleidoscope of truth and deception that makes your head spin and your heart race.
OPTION 1 · broken up (recommended: the brief says the chaos starts here, and short sentences make it felt)Mirrors. Everywhere, at every angle, and no two show the same thing. In one you are older. In one you never left Willowbrook. Corridors open in the glass and close again when you turn your head. Your reflections do not keep time with you; some of them are already walking away.
OPTION 2 · calm and uncannyThe corridors here are lined with mirrors, and each one shows you a little differently: older, younger, a boy who never left Willowbrook. The corridors seem to lead everywhere. Your reflections do not quite keep time with you, and some of them are already walking away.

Hall of Echoes

Arriving · look
This vast hall stretches into shadow, its vaulted ceiling lost in darkness above. Every footstep awakens whispers from ages past, as if the very walls have absorbed the voices of countless souls. Ethereal sounds drift through the air—fragments of conversations, battle cries, and laments that seem to come from everywhere and nowhere. At the hall's heart stands a figure in tarnished armor, motionless as a statue but radiating an aura of profound sorrow.
A long hall of old banners, so high the ceiling is lost. Everything that was ever said here is still being said, very quietly: orders, jokes, names, someone counting. At the far end, before the stair, a knight in tarnished armour stands without moving, the way a man stands at a grave.
Calling to the knight(redemption) before you hold up the chain
You open your mouth, and the knight's despair rolls over you like cold water. The word dies in your throat. You need something to shield you from it.
