Ending — Option A: the last line, after the welcome

The moor line comes after Grandmother's "Welcome home, Elior." as a closing image that widens from the gate to the whole land. The same in all three endings.

You press your hands into the dry earth and say the smallest spell you know. Green runs out from your fingers along the rows. Leaves unfurl, the earth darkens, and the whole field breathes out at once.

Across the village, the chapel bell rings once, with no hand on the rope.

Grandmother: "That is the third time. The first was for her, the night she went. The second was for you, the morning you left. I do not know who this one is for."

You do.

In the hedge the little light brightens. For the last time you hear Nyx, very small:

Nyx: "Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth."

Grandmother opens the gate.

Grandmother: "Welcome home, Elior."

That night the moor water holds two moons again.
