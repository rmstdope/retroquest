Know your village — naming the places still unvisited

The agreed design: the hint names the nearest unvisited place, the quest log ticks off all fifteen, and the map at the road names the nearest when that is all that is left.

1 · The hint, standing in the Village Square (coop, glade and road unvisited)

› hint

You stop and think over what you know.

Shadows over Willowbrook: The amulet is yours, but Willowbrook still holds you: something you began here is unfinished.

Know your village: You have not looked in on the hens at the Chicken Coop in a long while.

Names the unvisited place nearest to Elior; ties go to the earlier place in walking order. It changes as he moves.

3 · The map at the road, only unvisited places left (coop and glade)

› use map
The map stays a plain map of Willowbrook. The hidden road will not show itself while something here is unfinished: the Hidden Glade, where you have not been in a long while.

Names only the nearest. If any other task Elior began is still open, the map says “the tasks you began in the village” as it does today. The main quest's hint line is unchanged.

4 · The hint line for each place

Elior's Cottage
You have hardly looked at your own home, Elior's Cottage, in days.

Village Square
You have not stood in the Village Square and watched the village go by in a long while.

Vegetable Field
You have not walked the rows of the Vegetable Field in a long while.

Chicken Coop
You have not looked in on the hens at the Chicken Coop in a long while.

Mira's Hut
You have not gone up to Mira's Hut, north of the square, in a long while.

General Store
You have not stepped into the General Store in a long while.

Blacksmith's Forge
You have not stood in the heat of the Blacksmith's Forge in a long while.

Village Well
You have not leaned over the Village Well in a long while.

Abandoned Shed
You have not been near the Abandoned Shed south of the well in years.

Old Mill
You have not been down to the Old Mill in a long while.

Riverbank
You have not sat on the Riverbank in a long while.

Forest Path
You have not walked the Forest Path past the river in a long while.

Hidden Glade
South of the Forest Path lies the Hidden Glade, where you have never once stood.

Village Chapel
You have not sat in the Village Chapel in a long while.

Road to Greendale
You have not walked out to where the Road to Greendale leaves the village in a long while.

5 · All visited

The quest completes with its existing words: “You have walked every corner of Willowbrook. The pull has gone quiet, and the village feels like home.” It gives no more hint line.
