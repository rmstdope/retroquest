The Knight of Despair — the whole stretch, in play order

Your choices put together: Cedric stays at the gates, Truth Sight and the chain in either order, the confession in one scene, the knight kneeling in the hall afterwards as the Redeemed Knight, and the optional Ghostly Warriors. Lines marked NEW are new; everything else is already in the game and stays word for word.

1 · The hall, before (kept from the maze piece)

Hall of Echoes

This vast hall stretches into shadow, its vaulted ceiling lost in darkness above. Every footstep awakens whispers from ages past, as if the very walls have absorbed the voices of countless souls. Ethereal sounds drift through the air—fragments of conversations, battle cries, and laments that seem to come from everywhere and nowhere. At the hall's heart stands a figure in tarnished armor, motionless as a statue but radiating an aura of profound sorrow.

NEW · New side quest — The Knight of Despair

NEW · A knight in a grey cloak bars the stair to the Tower of Shadows, lost in a despair no blade can reach. Learn the truth of him, and find a way past.

Starts the first time Elior enters the hall (or, in a game saved inside the hall, at the first look at or talk with the knight).

› talk to knight of despair (before Truth Sight — kept)
Knight of Despair

There is nothing you can say to me that I have not already told myself. Go. Return when you have the truth.

› go east (until he is redeemed — kept)

The Knight of Despair stands before the stair to the tower. He does not lift his sword. He simply does not move, and there is no way past him.

2 · Truth Sight
› cast truth sight on knight of despair

NEW · Your sight sharpens, and the gloom peels away from the knight. His grey cloak is a traveller's, hooded and road-worn, and its clasp bears a twisted rune you know: the rune on the token that fouled Willowbrook's well, and the seal on the Secret Documents that cleared Sir Cedric's name. This is the cloaked stranger the villagers whispered about. Behind the visor, the knight is weeping.

› examine knight of despair (after)

NEW · The grey cloak, the twisted rune at its clasp, the tears behind the visor. The cloaked stranger from Willowbrook stands before you, and he knows you have seen him.

› talk to knight of despair (after)
Knight of Despair

NEW · So you see me. Then you know what I am, and what I have done. There is no redemption for a man like me. Do not waste the word.

› cast truth sight on knight of despair (again)

NEW · You have already seen the knight as he truly is.

Ghostly Warriors (optional, records mercy)
› examine ghostly warriors

NEW · Pale shapes of soldiers drift among the banners, fighting a battle that ended long ago. Their blades pass through one another without a sound.

› talk to ghostly warriors

NEW · The warriors do not hear you. They are still fighting.

› cast redemption's light on ghostly warriors (before it is learned)

The game's usual line for a spell Elior does not know.

› cast redemption's light on ghostly warriors

NEW · Soft light spills from your hands across the hall. One by one the ghostly warriors lower their blades and look at one another as if for the first time. Then they fade, at peace.

NEW · Elior will remember this.

Afterwards the warriors are gone, and the hall's description loses its warriors sentence.

Save and reload

At any point a reload returns the same state: whether the quest has started, whether the knight has been seen through, whether the chain is raised, whether he is redeemed (and so the stair, his lines, the room's words and what Elior carries), and whether the warriors are at peace.
