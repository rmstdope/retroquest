Short screen: the plate folds while someone speaks

Agreed design. On the picture stage, when the room plate at full height would leave the conversation too little room to show the newest line's speaker and first lines (a laptop window about 600 tall with narration open), the plate folds to one line while the conversation is on the picture. It opens again when the conversation is cleared or the narration is hidden.

1 · Short window (1000×613), Grandmother speaking, narration open. The plate folds to one line: the room's name, its names in a row (still pressable), ⟲ Story so far. The newest line shows from its speaker down; the ✕ sits above her portrait, which rests at the foot of the conversation.
