Act III — the curse grows worse at each relic site

Agreed. Once at each site, straight after the amulet's glow at that site's clue, the curse shows itself; the room where it happened keeps one closing line; and when Mira next moves the party on, her parting words end with one sentence about the king weakening.

The moment it happens — once, at each site's clue

1 · Sunken Ruins · examine letter (first reading)
> examine letter

…Your parents wrote to Malakar as a friend. He read it, and answered.

At your side, the Ancient Amulet grows warm, then glows. In its light you glimpse two faces, a man and a woman, laughing beside a third figure who has turned away. Then the light fades.

Out past the arches the tide turns, and the surf that breaks on the causeway runs grey. Dead sea birds lie along the stones, more than you can count, their feathers dull with salt. Whatever is draining the king is draining the sea as well.

2 · Mount Ember · examine inscription (first reading)
> examine inscription

…The last name is burned deeper than the others, as if the stone was struck again long after it cooled. Your parents and Malakar made this promise together, and the wisdom was his.

The Ancient Amulet glows again, brighter than before, and the faces sharpen. The woman has Grandmother's eyes. She can only be Lysa, your mother. The third figure still has his back turned.

Ash begins to fall over the slopes, slow and grey as rain. Down the mountain the braziers gutter one by one and sink to embers. The curse lies heavier here than it did by the sea.

3 · Caverns of Shadow · talk to dragon (first talk)
> talk to dragon
ancient dragon

"…They are not gone as the dead are gone, child. They are warded away, not dead."

The Ancient Amulet blazes in your hand a third time. Aren stands beside Lysa now, hand in hand. Behind them, the third figure still has his back turned.

Far off in the mines the ring of picks stops, and does not start again. Then the cavern's old dripping stops too, and the silence is complete. The curse has reached even here, beneath the oldest hills.

The ancient dragon rumbles softly: 'The memory has been shared. Your path leads forward now, not back. The scale awaits when you are ready to prove your selflessness.'

The curse comes after the amulet's glow, before the dragon's later-talk line.

Thin causeways of old stone lift from the black water like ribs. Moonlight skims the wet stones; salt and wind carry half‑heard songs. Broken arches stand like quiet mouths, guarding what the sea keeps. Dead sea birds line the stones, and the surf runs grey.

Mirror Terraces · look, after the inscription

Stepped platforms rise in concentric benches, each ring studded with sockets for polished mirrors and brass mounts. … the place smells of warm metal, singed cloth, and the mineral tang of cooling slag. Ash falls over the terraces like slow grey rain, and down the slope the braziers have guttered to embers.
