Mira's hint — the exact words

She has just said "You are ready." The design's line then says "Once you are truly ready", which may read as a contradiction, or as a deliberate second kind of ready. Shown in place A.

W1 — as the design wrote it (recommended)

…She presents you with the Ancient Amulet.

Mira

May this guide and protect you.

Then, more quietly:

Mira

I knew your parents better than I have said. Once you are truly ready, I will tell you.

"Ready for the road" against "truly ready" for the truth: the echo is the hint that there is more. Matches the design text word for word.
W2 — no echo of "ready"

…She presents you with the Ancient Amulet.

Mira

May this guide and protect you.

Then, more quietly:

Mira

I knew your parents better than I have said. One day, when the time is right, I will tell you.

Avoids any sense that she contradicts herself, but loses the echo; the design document would be updated to match.
W3 — she stops herself

…She presents you with the Ancient Amulet.

Mira

May this guide and protect you.

Then, more quietly:

Mira

Your parents… I knew them better than I have said.

She looks away.

Mira

Not yet. When you are truly ready, I will tell you.

More drama: she breaks off mid-thought. One beat longer and more of a slip than a promise.
