How does a player learn the merchant is hungry?

The kindness is giving Grandmother's fresh bread and taking nothing back. Today nothing says he is hungry, so the gift comes from nowhere.

◆You give the fresh bread to the merchant.

The merchant unwraps the cloth and simply holds the loaf for a moment.

merchant

Still warm. ... What do I owe you? No? Nothing at all? Then you've a better heart than most I meet on this road, friend. I'll not forget it.

Dialogue is where a text adventure puts a need. Added at the end of every talk until he has been given food.

After the vision, Elior can answer Cedric

Agreed design. Sharing the vision no longer ends Act II by itself: Cedric waits, and the act ends on the player's next talk with him, or when they tell him the truth about the voice. Telling him is not remembered as a moment of truth. Dashed = new.

1 — tell him the truth
› cast prophetic vision on sir cedric

◆You close your eyes and reach out with your newly granted sight… (the vision, unchanged)

“Elior… how you have grown. Do not be afraid of me.” … long before the dream.

The vision fades. Sir Cedric's expression has grown grave.

sir cedric

Then Malakar is no tale, and it is you he is hunting.

And yet the voice did not sound like a hunter's. Sir Cedric waits, watching you, as if he can see you have something to say.

› say truth to cedric

You tell Sir Cedric the part you would rather keep to yourself: the voice did not sound like a hunter. It sounded glad to see you.

sir cedric

Then it is worse, not better. A thing that wants you to come to it.

All your life, Malakar has been a name from old books… Malakar is real, and the bloodline he seeks is yours.

Sir Cedric rests a hand on his sword. "Wherever this road leads, you will not walk it alone." Act II is complete. The storm has a name now.
