Act II — every search that moves the story is hinted at first

The floorboard and the bricked cellar are described in the room; the Gate Captain, the squires and the historians tell you the rest. A room's hint is there only while the search will work, and becomes a sentence that remembers the find. The historians point to the archive as you hand over the journal and every time you talk to them until you have searched it.

The new words are marked in green here only so you can find them; in the game they read as ordinary text.
