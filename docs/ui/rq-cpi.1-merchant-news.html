The Merchant's news — two ways to hint at the fields

Both versions tell the same news. The difference is the hint sentence, outlined: W1 points at Willowbrook's own fields, while W2 has the Merchant say he has seen the same withering all along the road, so the blight is clearly bigger than this village. The third frame shows the case where Elior gives him the ring before ever talking to him.

W1 — "the same season"
› talk to merchant

◆You speak with the merchant.

Merchant

You'll want news, if you're bound for Greendale. It's a sombre city this season: they say King Alden has fallen ill, and no physician can name the sickness. And the roads have gone bad besides. I passed a cart on its side by the hill road, and nobody with it. Odd thing: it all began the same season your crops started to wither. Keep to the daylight, friend.

Looking for something specific, or just browsing? If you have any fine jewelry, especially a shiny ring, …

Short. The connection is offered as a passing remark.
