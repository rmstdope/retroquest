What if Elior gives the merchant other food?

The design names Grandmother's fresh bread. Elior may also carry berries, an apple, an egg, a carrot or a fish, which he needs for the road.

G1 — only the bread counts (recommended)
› give apple to merchant

◆You offer the apple to the merchant.

merchant

That's kind, friend, but you'll want every bite of that where you're going. Keep it.

He refuses, so nobody gives away food they need for the road. The fresh bread is a gift from home, which is why it means something.

› give apple to merchant

What do I owe you? No? Then you've a better heart than most I meet on this road, friend.

✦ Your generosity will be remembered.

Easier to find. A player can give away the food they need and have to gather more.
