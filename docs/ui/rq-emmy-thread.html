The history thread, act by act

Every new line, highlighted, with where and when a player meets it. Fourteen finds plus one proposed ending line: two on the main path in Act I and IV (the priest, two room descriptions), the rest optional. Act I never names the Wardens; the oath comes in Act II, Malakar's friends in Act III, the king's keeping in Act IV — each act names only what its part of the story allows. Spoken lines go to the team to record later.

Act I · chapel — main path

First talk, as chosen. Everyone hears it.

› talk to priest

…Their mark was a tree under two moons; it is cut over the door, if you look, though the weather has nearly had it. They came over the moor on a still night, and the moon lay in the moor water as bright as the one above it. Two moons, they said, one for the sky and one for the ground, and they named the land Twinmoor. The relic passed quietly down their line…

Act I · chapel — optional

Looking at the mark.

› examine mark over the door

A tree under two moons, cut into the stone over the door. The weather has worn it almost smooth. The lower moon is cut shallower than the upper, like a moon seen in water.

Act I · village square — optional

A new rumour in her rotation; she knocks on wood as usual elsewhere.

› talk to tilda

Tilda: "My mother used to say, two moons on the moor, no rain at the door. Still nights, she meant. We've not had a still night since the fields went grey."

Act I · cottage — optional

Her next talk after she has seen the locket.

› talk to grandmother

Grandmother: "Your mother used to walk up to the moor on still nights to see the two moons. She came home with wet hems and not a word of sorry."

She pushes the bread board toward you.

Act II · Great Hall — optional

A talk after the archive search is done.

› talk to historians

Historians: "The land grants are older than the crown. The first is not a grant at all: a page in the Wardens' hand naming the moor Twinmoor, and under it the first king's mark, swearing to hold what they bind. Every king since has sworn the same words at his crowning. We had thought them only words."

Act II · Great Hall — optional

A talk after the archive search is done. The oath's words are a new motif and stay exact everywhere.

Court Herald: "I read the oath at His Majesty's crowning. 'What the Wardens bind, the crown will hold.' I did not know who the Wardens were. I am told you do."

Act II · hidden library — optional

A talk after the Librarian is friendly.

Spectral Librarian: "They named it Twinmoor before there was a crown to name it. Ask the crown what it promised them."

Act II · with Sir Cedric — optional

A talk once his name is cleared.

› talk to sir cedric

Sir Cedric: "Every knight swears on the crown's oath. 'What the Wardens bind, the crown will hold.' I swore it at sixteen and never asked whose words they were. I will ask now."
