The words on the picture fade away by themselves

Every line on the picture, the narrator's on the left and what people say on the right, starts to fade the moment it has been read, and is gone 5 seconds later. It fades whether or not more lines come after it, and whether it has been pushed up or not. When it is gone, its room closes. The whole turn can still be read in Story so far. This replaces today's dimming by turn, where older lines are kept at about half and a third of full strength.

Try it

Press Play the turn. The small blue clocks under each line show how far into its fade it is. They are only here to judge the timing and will not be in the game.
