Sir Cedric's family charm — in play order

Chosen: he mentions the charm once, as he sends you to the forest, in a plain, knightly voice, and refers back to that refusal when he hands it over. Lines marked NEW are new; struck lines are removed; everything else stays word for word.

1 · He refuses, as he sends you to the forest
› talk to sir cedric (after the knight's test)
Sir Cedric

Excellent demonstration! Your combat skills are impressive. I can see you have the training and discipline needed for the challenges ahead.

Sir Cedric reaches into his pouch and hands you a bag of coins.

Sir Cedric

Please accept these 100 gold coins. … quality rope from the Market District. The realm faces a great threat, and we must be prepared.

NEW · Sir Cedric hesitates, then rests a hand on a small pouch at his belt.

Sir Cedric

NEW · One more thing, since the forest is where you are bound. My family has kept a charm for generations: Nature's Charm, carved for the knights who first made pacts with the forest spirits. The old texts say it is one of three that can call the sprite Nyx. I will not hand an heirloom of my house to someone I have known for a day. Prove yourself in more than a sparring yard, and we will speak of it again.

Sir Cedric

NEW · Please accept this Nature's Charm. It has been in my family for generations, …
Do you remember what I told you in the yard? That I would not give an heirloom of my house to someone I had known a day. I have known you long enough now. Take Nature's Charm. It was blessed by the ancient knights who first made pacts with the forest spirits, and it is one of the three that call Nyx. You will need it more than I will.

3 · He gives it, but never refused you
› talk to sir cedric (name cleared before you reported the knight's test)

Sir Cedric reaches into his pack and withdraws an ancient wooden charm.

Sir Cedric

NEW · Please accept this Nature's Charm. It has been in my family for generations, blessed by the ancient knights who first made pacts with the forest spirits. A day ago I would not have given it to someone I had only just met. But you have done more for me than men I have known for years. The old texts say this is one of three sacred charms needed to summon Nyx. I believe you will need it for the greater challenges ahead.

Edge case: if a player clears his name before ever reporting back from the knight's test, the refusal was never heard, so the hand-over cannot refer back to it.
