Option C · Pictures in the story

The picture belongs to the arrival: every time you enter a room, the story opens that part with its picture, full width. Earlier rooms keep their (smaller, faded) pictures further up, so the story reads like an illustrated book of your journey.

Cost: closest to a picture book and simple on a phone, but the picture scrolls away as you play, so it heads the room only until you start doing things there.
