2 · The gates, before

Fortress Gates

Towering obsidian gates loom before you, carved with writhing shadows that seem to move in the corners of your vision. Ancient ward stones pulse with malevolent energy, casting eerie purple light across the threshold. The air crackles with dark magic, and whispers of forgotten souls echo from beyond the barrier. Spectral guardians drift between the stones, their hollow eyes fixed upon any who dare approach this cursed entrance.

NEW · A shimmering barrier of dark energy throws you back. It pulses in rhythm with the ward stones.

3 · Looking around
› examine ward stones

You examine the ward stones closely. Each stone is carved with ancient protective runes that pulse with dark energy. The stones appear to be the anchor points for the shadow guardians and magical barriers. A fragment has broken off from one stone, perhaps weakening the network.

› examine barriers

Shimmering barriers of dark energy block the entrances to the fortress. The barriers pulse in rhythm with the ward stones, drawing power from their dark magic.

› examine guardians

The spectral guardians drift menacingly between the ward stones, their forms shifting between solid and ethereal. Their hollow eyes burn with malevolent purpose, and they seem bound to the stones' dark energy.

› take guardian's chain

As you lift the Guardian's Chain, you feel a surge of protective energy coursing through your body. The chain's light grows brighter, recognizing you as its new bearer.

4 · Too early, and elsewhere
› cast light (before the barrier is broken)

The shadow guardians resist your light magic while the ward stones remain active. You must disable the magical barriers first.

› use ward stone fragment (in the courtyard)

The ward stone fragment only responds to the specific magical barriers of the fortress gates.

5 · Breaking through
› use ward stone fragment with barriers

You press the ward stone fragment against the magical barriers. The purple energy flickers and fades as the defensive wards are disrupted. The barriers collapse with a resonant hum, clearing the way forward.

› cast light on ward stones

Your light magic blazes forth, dispelling the shadow guardians in brilliant flashes of purifying energy. As they vanish, their essence coalesces into a glowing orb that settles gently on the ground - the Guardian's Essence, proof of your courage.

NEW · Side quest complete — Shattered Ward Guardians

Elior's light breaks the guardians' hold on the gates. The ward stones fall dark, and the way into Malakar's fortress lies open.

› cast light (again)

The shadow guardians have already been banished. The way forward is clear.

› use ward stone fragment with barriers (again)

The barriers have already been disabled. The ward stone fragment has served its purpose.

7 · The courtyard, before

Outer Courtyard

What was once a magnificent courtyard now writhes under a shroud of despair. Withered fountains weep black tears, and twisted statues reach toward a sky that seems perpetually stormy. Ethereal figures wander in endless circles, their faces etched with torment as dark chains bind their spirits to this cursed ground. NEW → By the dry fountain a soldier in a soot-black royal tabard stands rigid, staring at nothing. ← The very stones beneath your feet pulse with anguish, and the air tastes of lost hope and forgotten dreams.

› use guardian's essence on servants

You present the Guardian's Essence to the trapped servants. The golden light recognizes their suffering and your courage in facing the shadow guardians. The servants' eyes brighten with hope as they sense your worthiness to help them.

› cast bless on servants

NEW → By the fountain, the captain blinks, as if waking from a long sleep. ← In gratitude, they offer you a loyalty token and a pendant that will help guide you through the trials ahead.
