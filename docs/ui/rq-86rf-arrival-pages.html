rq-86rf — a long arrival on a short laptop window turns into pages

Agreed: when an arrival part is too tall for the space between the top buttons and the actions, it is split between paragraphs into pages that each fit, in full-size type. A click or Enter turns the page; with narration on, the narrator turns it. No page counter; no going back. A paragraph too tall for a page on its own is split between its sentences. Drawn at half size.
