Option A — Elior speaks, labelled “Elior”

Every time Elior speaks aloud, his words become a speech line of their own, labelled Elior and styled like everyone else's, heard in Elior's voice. The words “you say” go: the label already says who speaks. The rest of the narration stays, word for word, in the storyteller's voice.
