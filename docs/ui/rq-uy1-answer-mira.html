Answering Mira — agreed

After her confession, Elior may forgive Mira or tell her she should have told him. It is optional, the first answer stands, and it changes her second line at the fortress gate. It does not count toward the endings.

The hut

1 · End of the confession

… the confession, unchanged, up to Elior's 'Why didn't you tell me?' and her two answers …

Mira

I am telling you now because you have earned the truth. The amulet you carry is the anchor of the ward your parents became.

Mira

NEWSay what you need to say to me, Elior. I would rather hear it now.

NEW(Answer her with: say i forgive you or say you should have told me to Mira.)

She looks down at the relics, and then back at you.

Mira

When you are ready, speak to me again, and I will begin the Warding Rite.

Mira

And ask me anything you like now — about your parents, Malakar, the amulet, your grandmother. I will not hide from you again.

(Ask her with: say parents, say malakar, say amulet or say grandmother to Mira.)

One new speech line and one grey line, before she turns to the relics.

2 · Elior forgives her
> say i forgive you to mira

NEWYou say 'I forgive you' to Mira.

NEWMira closes her eyes. For a moment she looks younger, and very tired.

Mira

NEWThank you. I did not dare ask for that. I will try to deserve it.

Remembered for later.

3 · Elior says she should have told him
> say you should have told me to mira

NEWYou say 'You should have told me' to Mira.

NEWMira does not look away.

Mira

NEWI should have. I chose the seal over you every day, and I would choose it again. But I am sorry for every one of those days.

Remembered for later.

4 · Either answer, said again
> say i forgive you to mira
Mira

NEWI heard you, Elior. I will not forget it.

The first answer stands; it cannot be changed.

5 · No answer — he just talks to her
> talk to mira

… the Warding Rite, unchanged. The answer is no longer offered; nothing later changes.

Answering is optional; the rite still follows on the next talk.

The edges

After the rite

Mira shakes her head.

Mira

Ask me of your parents, Malakar, the amulet or your grandmother, Elior.

The same reply as anything else she does not recognise (from the questions about the past). Nothing is remembered.

Loose matching
> say forgive me? no — i forgive you, mira

Counts as forgiving: anything containing forgive.

> say you should have told me sooner to mira

Counts as the reproach: anything containing should have told or should have said.

> say i forgive you but you should have told me to mira

Both: forgiving wins.

Typed words that miss both count as anything else said to her.
