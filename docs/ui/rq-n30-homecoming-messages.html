The homecoming answers the messages Elior carried

The agreed version. Cedric: the first talk carries both apologies, later talks get his old line. Mira: the first talk adds Elena's message after her ending line (it is the same in every ending), and later talks get her ending line alone. Nyx: the refrain is part of the last cast, before "Welcome home". The farewell now says "proud of her", matching Grandmother. Highlighted text is new.

Cedric, first talk
› talk to cedric

You tell him what the captain of the guard said: that he is sorry he ever doubted him.

Sir Cedric: "He was the youngest on the walls that night. I never blamed him. I am glad he remembers."

Then what the knight in grey said: that he is sorry, and that he would say it to your face.

Sir Cedric: "The knight in grey. I thought I would want to hear that more than I do. Tell me where he is."

He looks out over the field for a while.

Sir Cedric: "Later. I have walked a long way beside you. This is a good place to stop."

Grandmother, first talk (unchanged)
› talk to grandmother

Grandmother: "You saw them. I can see it in your face."

You tell her what they said: that they are proud of her, that they are resting.

Grandmother: "Resting. Good. Then so can we. Go on, love. The field has waited long enough."

The last cast
› cast grow

You cast grow.

You press your hands into the dry earth and say the smallest spell you know. Green runs out from your fingers along the rows. Leaves unfurl, the earth darkens, and the whole field breathes out at once.

In the hedge the little light brightens. For the last time you hear Nyx, very small:

Nyx: "Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth."

Grandmother opens the gate.

Grandmother: "Welcome home, Elior."
