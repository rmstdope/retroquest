What the square says (unchanged)

Village Square
The heart of Willowbrook: cobblestones around an old stone fountain, and a weathered notice board covered in faded announcements. The market is busy, but the talk at the stalls is low, and it stops when you pass.

Looking at the stalls

› examine market stalls

Half the stalls are bare boards with the awnings rolled up. The ones still open sell bread, onions and very little else.

Bread, cheese, onions, all laid out under the awnings. The stallholders weigh and wrap and say only the price.
