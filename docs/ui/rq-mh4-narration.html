Narrated story

  • Each block appears when its voice starts; nothing unspoken is shown.
  • The spoken block is marked and a highlight follows the spoken word.
  • Tap the story (Space / Enter) hurries one block; double-tap (Esc) shows the rest silently.
  • Any action or typed command cuts in.
  • Narration, every character’s lines and quest cards are read; a room’s description only on the first visit; events stay silent with their sound effect; lines without a recording appear at once.
  • The chapter page and the quest-complete pop-up are narrated too.
  • Narration is on for a new player; Settings › Sound › “Narration” (“The story and every character read aloud”) with “Volume” turns it off.
  • The hint shows on the first 3 narrated turns of a game: “Tap to hurry · double-tap to skip” on phones and tablets, “Space to hurry · Esc to skip” on a laptop.
  • Help: “Narration: tap the story or press Space to hurry the current line; double-tap or press Esc to show the rest at once. Any action cuts in. Turn narration off in Settings.”
  • No replay of a narrated line (for now).

Pick a state:

Pick a state to start. Sound plays through the browser’s own voices, standing in for the recorded ones.

Decided along the way: typed commands are not read aloud; turns that come back after loading a save are not narrated again; on a laptop Space/Enter hurry only when you are not typing in the command box; Esc closes an open menu before it skips.
