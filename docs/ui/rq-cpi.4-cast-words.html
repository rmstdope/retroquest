W1 — the words around the carving (recommended)

Sets the carving apart and lets it linger; matches the tone of the glade.

Casting light (first time)
› cast light

◆You cast a light spell that illuminates the glade.

The unnatural darkness lifts, and golden sunlight spills through the branches. The inscription on the mossy stone glows faintly, revealing ancient runes. They speak of a seed, a song, and the sun's embrace to awaken life.

As you read the runes, you feel a connection to the ancient magic of nature. You have learned the grow spell!

Carved beneath the runes, in a plainer hand, are words you can read:

Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.

You read them aloud. They mean nothing to you, and yet they stay with you.
