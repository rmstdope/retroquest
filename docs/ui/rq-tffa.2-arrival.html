Arriving in a room — agreed

Laptop stage. On the first visit to a room, its description is told on the middle of the picture: light text with a soft dark glow, no paper, the spoken word lit as in the story. The story steps aside to a "▴ Narration" tab. Something that happens on arrival is told first, in italics, then the description. It fades about 1½ s after the narrator finishes, or after a reading time shown by a thin bar when narration is off; a click or Enter goes on a part, an action or typing clears it at once. Coming back to a room, or look, shows the description in the story.

Out of this piece: the narration paper itself, the phone and short windows (today's behaviour there).
