rq-74xp — a name stays put while the player reaches for it

Agreed: Option A, reach on or just beside, phone holds nothing. The rule covers every time the names re-arrange, not only the arrival words closing.

Option A — the name you are on stays; the rest move

When the words close, the one name the pointer is on (or just beside) stays where it is. Every other name moves to its best spot, gliding there. The held name goes home the moment the pointer leaves it.

Reach, option 2 — held when the pointer is on it or just beside it

A name is held when the pointer is on it or within about a fingertip (roughly 24 pixels) of it. If two names are that close, the nearer one is held.

Phone, option A — nothing held

A phone has no pointer resting anywhere. The tap that closes the words is the player's own, and they watch the names settle before tapping again.
