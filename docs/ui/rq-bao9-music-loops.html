The music loops come round without a click. Nothing changes on screen; these are the four moments the player hears differently, with the option chosen for each.

Option A — the old music plays on until the new is ready

The player steps into a room with its own music. The new music has to arrive in full before it can loop without a click, which may take a moment on a slow connection.
