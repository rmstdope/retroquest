The change card

When something in the bag turns into another state of itself, one card comes up on the picture: the old name, an arrow, and the new name with its picture. It rests long enough to read, then flies into the Bag like any new thing. No grey “lost” card at all.

Only for one thing in the bag changing its own state (filling, emptying and the like). Combining two things into a new one keeps today’s grey cards and new-thing flight. The waterskin is a made-up long name to test the fit.

Today: a grey card “− Bucket” rises out of the Bag, then a bucket picture with no name flies back in — the same picture as the empty bucket. It reads as if the bucket was lost.
