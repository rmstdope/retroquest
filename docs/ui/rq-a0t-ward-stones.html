The words — every case at the gates

Every line a player can read at the gates, before and after the barrier falls. Highlighted text is new or changed; plain text is today's wording, kept. Wrong-stone line uses whichever stone was named.

Arriving (description)

Towering obsidian gates loom before you, carved with writhing shadows that seem to move in the corners of your vision.

Three ancient ward stones, left, middle and right,

pulse with malevolent energy, casting eerie purple light across the threshold. The air crackles with dark magic, and whispers of forgotten souls echo from beyond the barrier. Spectral guardians drift between the stones, their hollow eyes fixed upon any who dare approach this cursed entrance.

Trying to leave before
› north

A shimmering barrier of dark energy throws you back. It pulses in rhythm with the ward stones.

Looking at all three
› examine ward stones

Three ward stones stand before the gates, evenly spaced, each carved with the same runes. Their light swells and fades together, and the barrier swells and fades with them. All but one: the right stone brightens a breath after the others, like a man who has lost count.

Looking at one in time
› examine left stone

The left stone swells and fades with the barrier, exactly on the beat.

(the middle stone: same line, “middle”)

Looking at the one out of time
› examine right stone

The right stone brightens a breath after the barrier does, every time, like a man who has lost count.

Light on all of them
› cast light on ward stones

Your light washes over all three stones at once. They drink it together and keep their count.

Light with no target
› cast light

Your light falls on the guardians and slides off them. They stand on the barrier, and the barrier stands on the stones.

(replaces “You must disable the magical barriers first.”)

Light on a wrong stone
› cast light on left stone

The left stone drinks your light and pulses brighter, and the guardians lean closer for a moment. The barrier keeps its rhythm.

Light on the right stone
› cast light on right stone

Your light finds the right stone between its beats. It flares, stutters, and goes dark. A fragment cracks from its face and drops to the flagstones. The other two stones falter, lose the count, and go dark with it; the barrier flickers out of time and is gone.

Without the barrier the guardians have nothing to stand on. They thin to smoke and are gone, and where they drifted lies the chain that bound them, and a small orb of golden light: the Guardian's Essence.

Afterwards (description, kept)

Towering obsidian gates loom before you, carved with writhing shadows that seem to move in the corners of your vision. The ward stones stand dark and silent now, and the barrier is gone. Whispers of forgotten souls still echo from deep within the fortress.

Looking at the stones after
› examine ward stones

Three ward stones, dark and silent. The right one is missing a piece of its face.

› examine left stone

The left stone is dark.

Light on a stone after
› cast light on middle stone

The middle stone is dark. Your light shows only its runes.

Light with no target after (kept)
› cast light

The shadow guardians have already been banished. The way forward is clear.

The fragment
› examine ward stone fragment

A jagged piece of the right ward stone, carved with half a rune. It is cold, and keeps no time at all.

› take ward stone fragment

You pick up the fragment. It is cold, and it no longer keeps any time at all.

The chain
› examine guardian's chain

A silver chain, each link inscribed with runes. It bound the guardians to the stones; now it binds nothing.

› take guardian's chain

As you lift the Guardian's Chain, you feel a surge of protective energy coursing through your body. The chain's light grows brighter, recognizing you as its new bearer.
