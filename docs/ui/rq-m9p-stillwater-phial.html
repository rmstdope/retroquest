The Stillwater Phial — agreed

The phial shows young Mira every time it is examined. One drop stills the pools, in either order with the blessing, and the phial is kept. Without stilling, the chant breaks on ripples. The Silence Keeper hints until the pools are still, and the pool can be examined before and after. Highlighted text is new.

Examine the phial (every time)
› examine phial

A crystal vial containing water so still it seems solid. The liquid captures and holds reflections that shouldn't be there. Tilted to the light, it shows not your face but a young woman at a long table, bent over a book, and three teachers about her: one pointing at the page, one laughing, one watching from the shadow by the door.

Examine the pool — before stilling
› examine pool

Obsidian water, so still it seems frozen. Yet when you lean close, the faintest tremor runs across it, too fine to see unless you are looking for it.

Silence Keeper — pool not yet stilled
› talk to silence keeper

The Silence Keeper whispers, their voice barely disturbing the air: 'The echo stones await sanctification. Only when blessed can they amplify the ancient words that will quiet the restless spirits. But a chant will not carry over troubled water, and these pools are not as still as they seem. Speak the resonant chant to the stones, and the path to the ancient one will open.'

Chant — blessed, pool not stilled
› use rubbings with echo stones

You recite the resonant chant at each stone.

The words leave the stones and skim out across the pools. Water that looked frozen shivers under them, and the chant breaks apart on a thousand tiny ripples. It will not carry over water that is not truly still.

Stilling the pool (before or after the blessing)
› use stillwater phial with pool

You unstopper the phial and let a single drop fall into the nearest pool. The ripple does not spread; it sinks, and the stillness goes down after it until the black water lies flat as glass, holding the stones and the dark above in perfect reflection.

The pools are mirror-still. Now the chant will carry.
