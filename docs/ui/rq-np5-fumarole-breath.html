Steady the vents first, then time the crossing

The steps that exist today stay as they are: three vent stones, then the heat-ward mix. Only after that does the way south appear, and from then on the vents breathe in a steady rhythm that the player must time. Before the ward, the vents are wild and there is no rhythm to read. Highlighted text is new.

Applying the ward — the rhythm begins
› use heat-ward mix

You daub the heat-ward mix into the vents and seams. The rock grows a touch cooler around your hands as the ward seals into place.

The wild gusting settles into a slow, even breath: out, a held pause, and in. Each time it draws in, a cool draught runs south.

After the ward — going south on the exhale
› south

Only resting moves the breath on

The breath stays where it is while the player looks, talks or uses things. Resting (and waiting, if we add that word) moves it one step: out → pause → in → out. Each rest says what the vents are doing now. A failed attempt south also moves it on, so walking into the heat is never a dead loop. Highlighted text is new.
