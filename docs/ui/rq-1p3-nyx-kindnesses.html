Nyx names the kindnesses Elior chose — agreed

Nyx names the kindnesses in the same breath as the deeds the forest asked for, just before “That is enough.” Dashed outline = new words; everything else is the scene as already agreed for the forest voices.

All three kindnesses

ALL THREE
Flower for Wren, truth to Marcus, a word for Brenn — always in that order.

> talk to nyx

You approach Nyx.

The light over the altar folds into a woman no taller than you, barefoot, leaves caught in her hair. She looks at you the way you look at a road you have walked before.

“Elior. You gave the tree your acorn instead of keeping it. You answered the nymphs without bargaining. You brought me the three charms. No one asked these of you. You gave Wren your flower from the mountain, and she held it up to the window. You told Marcus the truth about his daughter when a kinder lie was easier. You spoke for Brenn when Thorne would not. That is enough.”

She touches your forehead. Something settles behind your eyes, like a word you had forgotten coming back. She puts two things in your hands.

You have learned the spell: Prophetic Vision
You have received: Nyx’s Token
You have received: Forest Heart Crystal

“The storm is heading for your village. This lets you see it coming.”

Then her voice softens, as if she is repeating something she was told long ago. “Remember the old words, Elior: Power follows purpose; purpose follows truth.”

The words from the stone in the glade. You read them aloud there and did not know what they meant. You still do not, quite.
