Agreed details

  • The middle title reads “YOU ARE IN” over the room's name. The settled corner shows the name alone.
  • The title plays on every walk into a room, first visit or not, and when a saved game is loaded. On a first visit the name heads the arrival words and settles when they are done.
  • The title never stands in the way. A press on something in the picture works as usual and settles the title at once, and so does any key or a press on empty picture.
  • Timing: fades in over about 0.6 s, stays about 3 s, then crosses into the corner over about 1.5 s. Marks wait at a quarter strength while the middle title is up. With “reduce motion” on, there are no fades.
  • No one and nothing here is listed by name any more; they are reached on the picture, which a player can also Tab through. The corner name is not a button. Marks step out from under it as they do today for the card.
  • Phone: the corner name on the left, “⟲ Story so far” on the right. A long name such as “Submerged Antechamber” gives way with an ellipsis there; the middle title wraps onto two lines if it must.
  • The name is read aloud only as part of the story, as today.
