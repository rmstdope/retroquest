The opening leads to the lantern

Agreed: the quest log grows a journal, the cottage is grey morning, and Grandmother nudges at each step before the dream. The first talk to her is unchanged. Outlined text is new.

1. Just woken (lantern dark)

Cottage

A modest cottage with a straw roof and a cozy hearth. The scent of fresh bread and old books fills the air. The curtains are still drawn on a grey morning, and the corner by your bed is too dark to see into. A sturdy wooden table sits in the center, with a small loaf cooling on it, and a gentle fire crackles in the hearth. Grandmother stands by it, humming, and keeps an eye on the bread.

Quest log / hint
Hint of magic

You woke with three words written down, somewhere in the dark by your bed, and a tune still going in the house. The lantern would show you where.

Talking to her again
“Back again? There's bread on the board, if you're hungry. And light that lantern before you go feeling about in your corner; you'll have the shelf down.”

2. Lantern lit, journal not read

Cottage (from now on, for the rest of the act)

A modest cottage with a straw roof and a cozy hearth. The scent of fresh bread and old books fills the air. Grey morning comes through the lace curtains onto shelves lined with trinkets and memories. A sturdy wooden table sits in the center, with a small loaf cooling on it, and a gentle fire crackles in the hearth. Grandmother stands by it, humming, and keeps an eye on the bread.

Quest log / hint
Hint of magic

You woke with three words written down, somewhere in the dark by your bed, and a tune still going in the house. The lantern would show you where.

The lantern found your journal. The three words are in it, with the rest of the dream.

Talking to her again
“Back again? There's bread on the board, if you're hungry. You were scribbling in that journal before your eyes were open. Read me what it says, if you can make it out.”
