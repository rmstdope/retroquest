Option A — the name only as the label

When a line of speech is introduced by nothing but the speaker's name, the player sees the labelled quote and no separate name line.

Speech introduced only by a name
› talk to tom
Tom

'The well's been dry three days now. Someone ought to look.'

The name appears once, as the small label over the quote.
Speech introduced by narration (unchanged)
› talk to mira

Mira smiles warmly as you enter her fragrant hut.

Mira

'Welcome, Elior. I sense a stirring of the old magic about you.'

'Bring me the herbs, and we shall begin.'

Narration that names the speaker stays as it is; the label shows on the first quote, not again when the same person goes on.
