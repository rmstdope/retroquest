Option B — a bright ring around the chosen one

Continue (or Start) keeps its solid gold, so the game's suggestion stays visible. The selected choice gets a bright cream ring with a small gap. Enter chooses whatever is selected.

Agreed with it

  • ↑ and ↓ move the ring between the choices and wrap at the ends; Enter chooses the ringed one.
  • The ring is on the main choice (Continue, Start, or New game) as soon as the buttons are there.
  • Pointing the mouse at a choice moves the ring onto it.
  • Whenever the ring moves (arrow, Tab or the mouse): a tiny soft glass ting. Choosing (Enter or a click): a warm brass lantern latch clicking shut. Both follow the Sound effects switch and volume.
