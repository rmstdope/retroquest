The three endings, every state

As agreed: two moments of a kind open Justice or Sacrifice (at least one from before the fortress); the parents speak in turn; say the word, then use the amulet on the wound; the word is final; after Sacrifice only grow remains; earlier mercy adds one Malakar line in Redemption. Words ship as drawn.

What opens Justice and Sacrifice

What a player must have done earlier for Justice and Sacrifice to open. Redemption is always open. The refusal lines are the same in both options.

Redemption and the parents' farewell

The Redemption ending from where the throne-room piece stopped, with the parents' farewell. The farewell is the same in all three endings except one line chosen by the ending: Redemption (Lysa) “You gave him back to us, our friend. We never dared hope for that.” · Justice (Aren) “You named what he did, and you did not hate him for it. That is the hardest kind of fair.” · Sacrifice (Lysa) “You gave it all away, and kept the smallest thing. That was always the best of you.”

1 · After the elixir on Malakar (already agreed)

Malakar sits by the wound, the grey gone from his face. The wound still flickers in the air.

> use ancient amulet on wound

The wound waits for something gentler than the amulet. Malakar looks up at you, as if he is waiting to hear something.

> say sorry to malakar

Malakar only waits.

2 · Forgiveness
> say forgiveness to malakar

“I forgive you,” you say. The words are harder than any spell, and then they are easy. Something warm settles in your chest, like a lesson you always knew.

◆You have learned the Forgiveness spell!

Malakar

You would forgive me. They would have too.

Malakar

And not for the first time, I think. You have shown mercy before, where none was owed. I can see it on you.

The second Malakar line appears only if the player showed mercy somewhere earlier in the game.
3 · The wound heals
> cast forgiveness on wound

You lay forgiveness on the wound. Its edges stop flickering. The days that never happened fall quiet one by one, and the tear draws closed like a healed cut. Above the circle the crystal clouds, cracks and falls away, and King Alden breathes again.

Malakar

Thank you, Elior. I will stay here, by what I broke, and remember them properly this time.

Where the wound was, the light of the amulet gathers into two shapes you have only ever seen in the vault's memories: a man with your eyes, and a woman with Grandmother's smile. Your parents.

Aren

Elior. Look at you. Taller than me, I think.

Lysa

We watched over you all this time, from inside the light. We could not hold you, but we could hold the door shut.

Lysa

You gave him back to us, our friend. We never dared hope for that.

Aren

The ward is finished, and so are we. Tell your grandmother we are proud of you. Tell her we are resting.

Lysa

Go home, love. The field is waiting.

The light thins. They smile, and they are gone. The amulet in your hand is only a locket now, warm and quiet. Your parents are at rest.

◆You have completed the quest 'The Final Stand'.

Justice and Sacrifice

Justice and Sacrifice, from a broken prison. The words are said to Malakar with the usual “say … to …”, like “say redemption to knight”, so no new command. The farewell shown is the one the parents speak in turn; it follows whichever farewell is chosen.

Justice (prison broken, Justice open)
> say justice to malakar

“You tore the world, Malakar,” you say. “You drained the king. You sent a man to hunt a child in Willowbrook, and you let Sir Cedric take the blame. I will not pretend you didn't.”

Malakar

No. You won't. Your mother never could either.

The open amulet grows heavy in your hand, ready.

> use ancient amulet on wound

You press the open amulet to the wound. Threads of gold rise from King Alden's crown and weave into the ward's light. The tear closes, and Malakar goes with it, drawn into the seal. He does not fight.

Malakar

I am sorry. Tell them— no. They know.

King Alden

Then it falls to me to set right what he broke. Rest now. You have done enough.
