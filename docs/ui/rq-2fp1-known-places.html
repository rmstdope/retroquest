A — Only the places you know, list grows

Places you have not seen yet are left out. A place joins the list, in walking order, the moment you can see the way to it from somewhere you have been (the same moment it appears on the map). The count is of the places you know, so it grows: 2 of 5, then 8 of 12, then 14 of 15. The hint follows the same rule: it only ever names a place already on this list. The dashed outline marks what changes.
