Action panel — the last choice does it (agreed)

An action with nothing left to choose happens the moment its last choice is tapped. Do it appears only where an action can be finished early (Use, Cast) or with typed words (Say). Keyboard: Enter or Space on a choice is the same as a tap; focus returns to the verb afterwards.
