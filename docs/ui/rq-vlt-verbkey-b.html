Option B — recommended

Space and Enter on a focused button press it

With focus on a verb, a target chip, Cancel or Do it, Space and Enter press that button, exactly like a click: the voice keeps reading, and only a complete command stops it. Space and Enter still hurry when focus is anywhere else (the story, nothing in particular), and Esc still skips from anywhere.

Recommended: a focused button answering its own keys is the standard on the web, and it makes building a command by keyboard work the same as by mouse. Cost: a player who tabbed to a verb earlier must press Esc, or click the story, to move the voice on.
