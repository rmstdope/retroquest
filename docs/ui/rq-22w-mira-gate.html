Mira goes through the gate with Elior

Agreed version. Mira refuses to rest and goes through the fortress gates with Elior; just inside, she and Sir Cedric hold the way out and Elior goes on alone, as Act IV already plays. Highlighted text is new; struck text goes.

Act III · Entrance to Malakar's Fortress · first talk
› talk to mira

You speak with Mira.

Mira: "The journey has taken its toll on me, Elior. The magic required to transport us here has drained much of my strength."

Mira: "I must rest and recover while you and Sir Cedric press forward into the fortress. The fate of the kingdom now rests in your capable hands."

Mira: "Go with courage, wisdom, and selflessness—the three virtues you have proven. The Lifelight Elixir will guide you to victory. I will await your return."

Mira leans on the cold stone of the gate. Her hands are still shaking from the last circle.

Mira: "I know how I look, Elior. Do not ask me to stay behind."

Mira: "Your parents walked into the dark without me once. I will not let you do the same. Go with courage, wisdom, and selflessness—I am coming with you."

Act IV opening page

ACT IV: THE FINAL CONFRONTATION

With the Lifelight Elixir in hand, you stand before Malakar's fortress. The obsidian gates loom ahead, wreathed in shadow and ancient magic. Mira and Sir Cedric take up their places at the threshold — someone must hold the way out. Mira steps through the gates beside you, pale but unbending. She and Sir Cedric take up their places just inside the threshold — someone must hold the way out. From here, you go on alone. This is the culmination of your journey—a test of courage, wisdom, and selflessness that will determine the fate of the kingdom.

The final confrontation awaits. Every choice you make will echo through eternity.
