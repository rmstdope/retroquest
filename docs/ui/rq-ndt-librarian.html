Greeting — desktop
> talk to spectral librarian

The Spectral Librarian turns toward you, and stops. Their eyes have found the Ancient Amulet at your throat.

Spectral Librarian

I have not seen that worn since the last hand wrote in that chronicle.

Spectral Librarian

Mind the shelves. Everything here has been kept a long time, and the wards on the oldest texts are failing. I can keep a book; I cannot mend one. Mend the wards, and I will show you where to look for the mark you carry.

3. The talk that completes the mending, with the amulet

Reveal — desktop
> talk to spectral librarian
Spectral Librarian

You mended the wards with your mend spell. They have been failing longer than I care to count; thank you. Now, what you came for.

Their eyes go back to the Ancient Amulet.

Spectral Librarian

I wore that mark once, when I had a body to wear it in. Whose yours was, I cannot tell you; only that the Wardens did not give them away. We taught the unbinding to those we trusted, and wrote it down for the rest.

The spirit's eyes glow brighter as they speak:

Spectral Librarian

I am a keeper, not a teller. The answer you came for is in that chronicle. We wrote it, and our mark is on its cover. Read it, and you will learn the dispel spell.

They gesture to a crystal resting on the shelf:

Spectral Librarian

And take this Crystal Focus. What you do with them is yours to choose.

Was: “The Wardens of the Dawn wrote it, and their mark is on its cover, the same mark you wear at your throat.”
