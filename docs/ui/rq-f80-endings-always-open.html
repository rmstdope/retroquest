Option B — every ending always open; what Elior has been colours the text

Nothing in the throne room refuses an ending any more. The three refusals are deleted. What Elior did on the way adds a line to the ending he picks: mercy already adds lines from Malakar; truth now adds a line from King Alden in Justice; giving without asking adds a line from his parents in Sacrifice. A player who did none of it gets the ending exactly as today. Highlighted text is new; struck-through text goes.
The cost: the choice no longer "rewards" doing more, only colours it — and the player who skipped everything can still give up their magic.
